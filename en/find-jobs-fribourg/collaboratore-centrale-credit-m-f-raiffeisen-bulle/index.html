Be part of our team!

For the Raiffeisen Moléson Bank, a very well established bank in the southern canton of Fribourg, we are looking for a Middle Office Collaborator Crédit (m/f)

What are you waiting for?

Assistance to clients and advisers

  • Support advisors in the preparation and monitoring of their business activities.
  • Prepare customer appointments and gather the necessary documentation.
  • Ensure administrative treatment resulting from customer interviews.
  • Manage simple contacts related to distribution (recording, document requests, restarts, updating customer data).

Basic benefit management

  • Carry out administrative operations related to the opening, modification and termination of banking relations.
  • Process requests for accounts, means of payment, bank cards and other commodities.
  • Provide clients with information within their jurisdiction and process requests that do not require expert advice.
  • Ensure the various administrative tasks related to distribution (mail, e-mails, document tracking, etc.).

Credit support

  • Assist advisors in the preparation of credit applications.
  • Collect, monitor and analyze records necessary for the preparation of records.
  • Participate in the administrative assembly of funding files.
  • Monitor the credit portfolio in accordance with current directives.
  • Coordinate the exchange of information between the various internal and external stakeholders involved in the processing of cases.
  • Ensure the quality, completeness and compliance of files submitted for decision.

Investment support

  • Assist advisors in opening deposits and administrative management of investment relationships.
  • Control customer documents and instructions.
  • To carry out the necessary administrative and seizure operations in banking systems on the terms of reference of advisers.
  • Ensure administrative monitoring of investment files.

Technical support and continuous improvement

  • Provide technical and administrative support to advisors in the use of banking tools and processes.

What are you bringing?

  • Banking training
  • Good knowledge of basic banking services
  • Credit powers
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Ability to work independently
  • Organizational sense and rigour
  • Customer-oriented personality and service
