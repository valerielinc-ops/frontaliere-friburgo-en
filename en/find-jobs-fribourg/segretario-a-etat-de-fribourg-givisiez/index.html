The Cantonal Social Insurance Institution (KSVA) is an independent public-law institution which manages and coordinates the activities of the Equalisation Fund of the Canton of Freiburg, the IV Office and the Central Services. It thus constitutes a unique competence center for the insured persons and companies of the Canton of Freiburg of all social insurances of the first pillar.
