Full description

Who are we?

The SeCA Construction and Planning Service is the competent body for spatial planning. It monitors compliance with legal standards and establishes notice for prefectures.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • You are in charge of processing the legal part of the files in relation to spatial planning and construction. In particular, you ensure that appeals are heard and propose draft decisions.
  • You also manage records of restoration of the state in accordance with the law of illegal constructions located outside the area to be built and support the section in the preparation of a notice of law, in addition to ensuring legal oversight.
  • You are called upon to deal with other government departments, Prefectures, municipalities and individuals.

Profile desired

  • Master in Law
  • Good IT knowledge and opportunities for Internet legal research
  • Excellent editorial level
  • Team and initiative
  • Mastery of the French language with good knowledge of the German language
