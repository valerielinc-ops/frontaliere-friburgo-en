Assistants hold a secondary judicial office and have judicial decision-making powers (Art. 4 JG). They shall be provided as required by the authority concerned. The attendants receive session allowance in accordance with Art. 79a JR. Members are elected by the Grand Council on the opinion of the Judiciary Council. The Judicial Council advertises the judicial offices for the Grand Council (Art. 12 JG).
