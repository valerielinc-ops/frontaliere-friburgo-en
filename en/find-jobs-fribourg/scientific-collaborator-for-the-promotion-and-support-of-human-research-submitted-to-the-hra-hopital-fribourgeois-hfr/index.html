Full description

In order to enhance the quality of research projects submitted to the Federal Human Research Act (HRA) and to provide assistance and expertise in the development, drafting and submission of research protocols, HFR and UNIFR are in the process of developing and implementing research protocols.

  • Verify that researchers comply with the recommendations of good practice when drafting applications for authorisation for research and clinical trials submitted to the ethics committees, from an ethical, epidemiological and scientific point of view.
  • Follow up on applications to ethics and swissmedic boards.
  • Support researchers in their exchanges with ethics and swissmedic commissions until authorization is obtained.
  • Participate in case management and follow-up.
  • Participate in the development of guidelines and document templates for institutions and researchers.
  • Managing general consent within the HFR and adapting it as legal requirements evolve.
  • Manage clinicaltrials.gov.
  • Holder of a university degree (min. Master's degree or equivalent) in the fields of medicine, pharmacy, life sciences or psychology. A PhD is an asset.
  • To benefit from advanced GCP training and several years of professional experience in a position where you have been directly involved in the development of research and submission protocols to ethics commissions (in particular those in the cantons of Vaud and Bern) within the framework of the LRH and compliance with the relevant requirements.
  • You are fluent in the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language and you are fluent in the English and oral language.
  • The proven ability to work as a team and a strong self-help spirit.
  • The ability to balance the needs of researchers with the regulatory framework
  • Easy communication with the various research stakeholders (researcher, ethics commissions, legal services, IT services).
  • Self-reliance, capacity to manage priorities and initiative.
  • Excellent mastery of common office tools.
  • Some flexibility in the organization of working time.
