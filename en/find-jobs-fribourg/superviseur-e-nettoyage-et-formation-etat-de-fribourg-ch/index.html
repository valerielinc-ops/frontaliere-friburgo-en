Full description

Who are we?

The Buildings Service (SBat) is a central service that acts as a service provider to other cantonal departments, services and establishments. In order to strengthen the supervision of our cleaning teams and to support the development of their skills, we open a new function at the heart of our professionalization of our activities.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Ensure quality, organisation and safety of cleaning activities
  • Supervise cleaning staff by ensuring their ongoing training
  • Coordinate the work of external cleaning companies and the internal services concerned
  • Planning resources according to the needs and availability of employees
  • Organize and follow up introductory and development training for all cleaning staff

Profile desired

  • SWC of cleanliness officer, operating officer (AGEX) or stewardship manager (GEI) or training deemed equivalent
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the field of activity
  • Multi-year experience in team leadership
  • Authorization to train apprentices operating agent (AGEX)
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language
