Full description

Who are we?

The Fritic Skills Centre is responsible for all aspects related to digital education in the canton of Fribourg. For more information, our website www.fritic.ch will give you all the necessary information.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • In coordination with the project leader and the architect solution, contribute to the implementation and operation of IT solutions for Friburg schools
  • Analyze applications and propose solutions adapted to the context, according to the technical-functional needs collected
  • Support schools and local IT managers in the use of services (support N2)
  • Contribute to documentation, procedures and continuous improvement

Profile desired

  • Federal patent in relation to the field, supplemented by certified training in the field of activity, or training deemed equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience in IT, preferably IT education (8-10 years)
  • Teamwork, adaptability
  • Analysis capacity, curiosity and interest in technology
  • Service orientation and ease in collaboration with different stakeholders
  • Adaptability and motivation to change solutions and practices
  • Interest in knowledge transfer and sharing of good practices
  • Experience in Microsoft Administration 365
  • Excellent mastery of French and good knowledge of German (minimum B1)

The position corresponds to an ICT service manager function. Details of the reference function can be found at https://www.fr.ch/sites/default/files/2024-01/5 60 060.pdf
