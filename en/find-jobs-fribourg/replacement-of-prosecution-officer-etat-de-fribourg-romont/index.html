Full description

Who are we?

The Glâne Prosecution Office is located in Romont. It can issue you extracts and inform you in case of prosecution proceedings. The Office also proceeds with the prosecution and enforcement of seizures.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

What We Offer

  • A responsible position in the economic and social fabric of the Glâne district
  • Benefits and job security of a reference public employer (State of Fribourg)
  • Opportunities for further training

Your missions

  • Joint management: You actively assist the officer in the strategic and operational management of the Office and ensure his/her complete replacement in his/her absence
  • Complex Case Management: You deal autonomously with high complexity legal and technical procedures related to the PCA (including real estate seizures, forced realizations and the distribution of money)
  • Guidance and training: You supervise and support the team of case managers, ensuring strict application of cantonal law and directives
  • Representation and advice: You inform users, creditors, debtors and judicial authorities on specific legal issues

Profile desired

  • Training: You are the holder of the Federal Patent of Specialist in Proceedings for Debts and Bankruptcy or of a Master in Law (with strong expertise in LP and law of obligations)
  • Experience: You justify a proven professional experience of several years in an Office of Prosecutions or Bankruptcy in Switzerland
  • Competencies: With a sharp mindset of analysis and high stress resistance, you have proven skills for team leadership and negotiation
  • Judicial case: Impeccable reprimand (a criminal record extract and an extract from the blank prosecution office will be required)
  • Languages : E xcellente mastery of French . Good knowledge of German is an important asset for relations with partner offices and Swiss-German creditors.
