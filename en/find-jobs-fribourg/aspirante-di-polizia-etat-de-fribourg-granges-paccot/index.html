Your missions

Complete two years of on-the-job higher professional training, with the aim of working as a police officer within the Friborg cantonal police. The main missions are as follows: •

  • prevent attacks on public security and order and intervene if necessary;
  • record offenses, gather evidence and identify their perpetrators, in accordance with the provisions of criminal procedure;
  • ensure the execution of administrative and judicial decisions;
  • respond to emergency calls and alarms, carry out rescues and provide first aid in the event of serious danger or accident;
  • prevent offenses.

Desired profile

The formal conditions of engagement appear on the site dedicated to cantonal police recruitment. They are supplemented by the following skills:

  • good physical condition and good resistance to stress;
  • willingness to work irregular hours, evenings, nights and weekends;
  • presence, interpersonal skills and communication skills enabling teamwork and conflict management;
  • good oral and written expression skills;
  • ethical sense and sense of responsibility;
  • good general knowledge;
  • knowledge of the other official language of the canton is an advantage.

Who are we?

Attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport, the Friborg cantonal police have the general mission of ensuring public safety, maintaining order and observing the laws.

It serves the population and the authorities. This means carrying out a large number of varied and demanding tasks 24 hours a day, 365 days a year: emergency interventions, reports of offenses, crime prevention, investigations, etc. The increasingly important role of the police in managing social problems requires tact and know-how from officers.

It is also responsible for ensuring road traffic safety. It provides security support to organizers of public events and supports cantonal and municipal authorities during serious events.

The Friborg Cantonal Police is made up of two main bodies, the gendarmerie and the security police, and three divisions: command, logistics and IT, human resources and training.
