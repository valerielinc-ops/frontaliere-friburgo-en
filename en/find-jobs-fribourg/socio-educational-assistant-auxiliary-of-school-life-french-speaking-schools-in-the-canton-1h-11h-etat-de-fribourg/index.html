Full description

Who are we?

The Service de l'enseignement obligatoire de langue française (SenOF) is responsible for the conduct of teaching and the management of teaching at different levels of compulsory education in the French-speaking part of the canton. It also supervises private schools and home education.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Student support in everyday school life
  • Student support in access to learning activities
  • Student support in social and relational activities
  • Participation in the implementation and follow-up of the individualised educational project (PPI) for pupils' schooling
  • Participation in class and school life
  • Participation in continuing training required by the function

Profile desired

  • Holder of a Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC) for Socio-Educational Assistant (SEA)
  • Experience in accompanying young people of compulsory school age
  • Interest in accompanying young people with disabilities
  • Mastery of the French language
