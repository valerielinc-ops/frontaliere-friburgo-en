Who are we?

The Child and Youth Service SEJ is committed to children and young people in the canton of Fribourg. It is the specialized cantonal department responsible for the implementation of the policy on children and youth and child protection. Its benefits are for children, young people, their families, different authorities and anyone in contact with children or young people.

Why join us?

You join a small, motivated, creative and multidisciplinary team that addresses challenges and seeks innovative solutions in a challenging and demanding environment.

The course is offered for 6 months (extensible).

Those who have been detained will be invited to an interview on Thursday, September 24, 2026.

Your missions

  • You support the management of the Child Protection Service
  • You are in charge of managing a dynamic database and participate in various working groups as rapporteur and active member. In this context, you collaborate with all sectors of our service as well as with different partners of the child protection network.

Profile desired

  • University degree (master's degree) in social sciences or judged in accordance with the function
  • Interest in child protection
  • Excellent synthesis, good analytical skills and ease of writing
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools, especially Excel
  • Ability to work independently, organizational and planning skills
  • Perfect mastery of the French language in both oral and written with knowledge of German
