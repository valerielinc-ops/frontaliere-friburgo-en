Appointment authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs.

The FNPG brings together the canton’s outpatient, day clinic, mobile and inpatient public psychiatric services and ensures high-quality psychosocial care for the population of the bilingual canton of Freiburg.
