Head of electric mobility projects (Southern Region) — Groupe E

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Mission

As Project Manager, you are our clients' preferred contact point for all projects related to electrical mobility. From the identification of needs to the commissioning of facilities, you ensure complete monitoring of projects while actively developing our market presence.

Your main responsibilities

  • Develop and promote our electrical mobility solutions to individuals, businesses, communities and real estate authorities.
  • Advise clients and identify technical solutions tailored to their needs.
  • Carry out technical studies, develop offers and develop project costing in collaboration with the back office.
  • Ensure complete project management from the design phase to final acceptance.
  • Ensure order entries and work reserve for the region.
  • Support clients during commissioning and provide training in infrastructure use.

Profile

  • Basic CFC training for electrical or assembly installers.
  • Project leader's licence in installation and electrical safety or will to obtain it is an asset.
  • Experience confirmed in the management of technical projects and/or in a technical-commercial function in the field of electric mobility.
  • Good knowledge of electrical mobility facilities and ease in reading electrical plans and diagrams.
  • Strong interest in electrical mobility, renewable energy and innovative energy solutions.
  • Relationship, counseling and client orientation.
  • Entrepreneurship, autonomy and responsibility.
  • Mastery of Microsoft Office tools
  • Knowledge of an LES is an advantage.
  • French mother tongue, knowledge of technical English is an asset.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
