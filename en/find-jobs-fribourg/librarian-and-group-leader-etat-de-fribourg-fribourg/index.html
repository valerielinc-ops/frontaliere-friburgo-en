Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser all in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889. The Cantonal and University Library Freiburg (KUB) collects and provides documentary resources on all media types to the cantonal population and the academic community. The KUB is a meeting place for members of the university community as well as for a broad cantonal audience. It is part of the national library network Swisscovery.

Their tasks

  • Participation in the tasks of the Department of University Collections, which specializes in languages, literature and musicology
  • managing a small team responsible for orders and formal cataloguing
  • Coordination of these tasks within the department and with other departments of KUB
  • Participation in drawing up the work plans and communication of the department
  • Acquisition, cataloguing and provision of media services
  • Participation in providing information skills to students
  • Participation in the Information Service for Languages, Literatures and Musicology
  • If necessary, participation in working groups of the Abt. University Collections and the KUB

Desired profile

  • You have a Bachelor in Information Science or equivalent education
  • You have good knowledge of the standards RDA DACH and IdRef and proven experience in formal cataloguing
  • They are characterized by openness to change and are ready to take on further tasks that serve the functioning of the department and the institution
  • They speak French and have good knowledge of German and English. Knowledge of other, e.g. Slavic languages, is an advantage
