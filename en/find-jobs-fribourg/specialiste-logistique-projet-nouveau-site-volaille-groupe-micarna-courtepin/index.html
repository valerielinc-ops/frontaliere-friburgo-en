Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna has set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros.

Mansioni

  • Participate, as a representative of future users, in the planning, implementation, commissioning and operation of the internal logistics facilities of the new poultry site to ensure efficient processes.
  • Collaborate in the planning and coordination of the Early Equipment Management and the ramp-up, contributing to easy-to-use, cleaning, preparation, safety and processes.
  • Put in place proactive measures to achieve the objectives of your field.
  • Ensure timely information in your area of responsibility.
  • Participate in projects related to the current ATV in Courtepin in coordination with Chief ATV and your supervisor.

Requirements

  • Minimum 3 years: experience in planning and/or conducting industrial logistics tools, ideally in the agri-food sector.
  • Federal patent: training in logistics or title deemed equivalent.
  • French (Very good knowledge) German (Very good knowledge): Language skills ideally bilingual French German or otherwise German or French level B2 Technical
  • Strong technical skills in automated internal logistics.
  • Good knowledge of business management.
  • Ability to work as a team.
  • Mastery of Office 365 and good practice of SAP.
  • Systematic method of work, network thinking and ability to prioritize.
  • Ability to work under pressure in a project and ramp-up environment.
  • Willingness to succeed, strong example and proactive attitude.

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Professional planning: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Restaurant: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points Cumulus: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: Reimbursement of 70% of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Offers leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
  • Individual continuing training: Attractive offer of training and development
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
