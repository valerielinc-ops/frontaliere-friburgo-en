Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

Additional information

The environmental analysis research group supports and serves Agroscope researchers in chemical-analytical matters. In addition, it contributes with method development and own research projects to a better understanding of the fate and behavior of substances in the anthroposphere, agriculture and environment. Their target analyses range from heavy metals to organic microcontaminants and microplastics.
