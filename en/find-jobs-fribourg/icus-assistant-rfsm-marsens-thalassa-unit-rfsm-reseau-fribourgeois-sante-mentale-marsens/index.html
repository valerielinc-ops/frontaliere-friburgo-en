Full description

Post "ICUS-Assistant, RFSM Marsens - Thalassa Unit" at RFSM - Fribourg mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For the Thalassa Unit, psychiatry and psychotherapy for adults, taking care of patients with addiction:

  • Participate in the design of care processes and contribute to their implementation
  • Contribute to the improvement of interprofessional clinical practices
  • Promoting measures to develop mental health care and support
  • Alternate ICUS during absences
  • Work closely with ICUS in the management of the care unit
  • Participate in ongoing training of the care team
  • Participate, on behalf of ICUS, in the development of institutional projects
  • Contribute to the deployment of the values and principles set out in the RFSM Ethics Charter

Profile desired

  • Minimum of 2 years of professional experience in the field of mental health in hospitals
  • Expertise in psychiatric problems and addictions
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team
  • Organizational sense
  • Autonomy and responsibility
  • Communication and negotiation
  • Mastery of the French language, knowledge of the partner language, an asset

We offer
