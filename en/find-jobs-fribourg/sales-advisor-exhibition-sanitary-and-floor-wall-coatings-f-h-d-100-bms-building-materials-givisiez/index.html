Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz-Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team based in Givisiez or Bulle, we are looking for a dynamic personality that loves sales from 1 January 2027
