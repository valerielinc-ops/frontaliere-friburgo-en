Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Forest and Nature Office (WNA) is responsible for the management and conservation of forests, habitats, landscape, protection and promotion of flora and fauna, prevention and protection against natural hazards, and training of forest professionals. It has a territorial presence in all regions of the Canton of Freiburg. The various tasks are coordinated at the headquarters, the implementation takes place in the forestry districts and in the supervisory areas of the wild hat.

Become part of our team!

The employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Their tasks

  • Translations from French to German
  • Revision of texts (reports, approvals, instructions, etc.)
  • Writing of correspondence in German and French
  • Telephone and counter services (reception and information)
  • Processing and preparation of internal and external mail
  • Preparation of prefactures in the SAP program
  • Date monitoring and filing
  • Management of office equipment
  • Working days: Wednesday afternoon, Thursday, Friday

Desired profile

  • Commercial employee or equivalent
  • Good knowledge of common computing tools (Word, Excel, Outlook) SAP is an advantage
  • Some years of professional experience
  • Mastery of the German language
