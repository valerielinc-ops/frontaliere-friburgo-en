Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence center specializing in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, more broadly, mental health.

Bilingual FNPG is constantly evolving and keeping up to date in the therapeutic field.

Their tasks

With a view to expanding our psychiatric emergency room and triage:

Tasks related to emergency psychiatry

  • Reception, evaluation and orientation of emergency situations
  • Crisis intervention in the medical-nursing team
  • Coordination of care in the network

Task areas related to triage

  • Clinical evaluation by telephone or physical as well as assessment of the degree of urgency of the situation
  • Depending on the evaluation, forwarding the request to a suitable location in the RFSM and the entire Freiburg healthcare network
  • Management of beds under the supervision of the senior area manager of care
  • Promoting the FNPG brand image while respecting and respecting the values of the FNPG Ethics Charter

Desired profile

  • Ability to work in an interdisciplinary team
  • Autonomy and responsibility
  • Communication and negotiating skills
  • Safety in dealing with complex situations
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in psychiatric care
  • Expertise in the Freiburg Social and Health Network
  • Mastery of the specific computer tools of the station or ease of application
  • Mastery of the German language with very good knowledge of French or vice versa
