Full description

You lead with passion, think networked and negotiated skillfully? Then take responsibility in a key role of the meat industry. With your sense of people, markets and economic contexts, you ensure procurement, develop strong partnerships and actively shape our success.

Mansioni

  • You procure raw materials in the fresh meat sector and ensure a needs-based supply
  • You maintain and develop existing supplier relationships sustainably
  • You conduct price, volume and contract negotiations and achieve economically optimal solutions
  • You observe the market, analyze supply and demand situations and derive suitable measures
  • You ensure the availability of goods taking into account quality, costs and deadlines
  • You work closely with planning, production, sales and supply chain and coordinate the relevant interfaces

Requirements

  • Multiannual experience: in a leadership role in the meat industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): or higher vocational school degree, preferably as a meat specialist, cook or in food technology with a focus on meat products, as well as training in marketing or sales.
  • You have leadership experience and understand how to motivate and develop employees
  • You are a team player and enjoy dealing with people and building sustainable relationships
  • You are characterized by a fast comprehension and networked thinking
  • You perform safely and convince through your negotiating skills
  • You work independently, responsibly and solution-oriented
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office and ideally bring experience in dealing with SAP
  • You also keep an overview in a dynamic environment and set the right priorities
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave / 8 weeks paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages
  • Meals: Balanced lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for max. CHF 7.-
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Holidays: 5 to 7 weeks holidays per year depending on age and service
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Du-Culture: At Migros you smell – because it’s simply more relaxed
