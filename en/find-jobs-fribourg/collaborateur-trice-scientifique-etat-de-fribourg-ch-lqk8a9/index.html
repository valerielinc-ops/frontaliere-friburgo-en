Full description

Who are we?

The SASoc Social Action Service, the competent authority in the canton of Friborg in particular for the monitoring, coordination and harmonization of the social assistance system (LASoc), is looking for entry on January 1, 2027 or to be agreed, a scientific collaborator in this sector at 60-70%. The mission of the position concerns the implementation and monitoring of the law on social assistance (LASoc). Only online applications via the State of Friborg website jobs.fr.ch will be taken into consideration.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages work-life balance and offers an attractive working environment and conditions to its employees. To find out more, follow this link.

Your missions

  • Establish and support the tasks of monitoring the organization and functioning of regional social services and social commissions responsible for the application of the law on social assistance (LASoc)
  • Define monitoring of the social assistance system, statistical indicators, control dashboards, report the results and analyze them
  • Develop quality criteria for services and verify their application
  • Support regional social services and social commissions for the adaptation of processes, the implementation of general measures relating to the information and training of professionals and for the organization of procedures
  • Participate in the development of the social assistance system

Desired profile

  • Master's degree in social work or training deemed equivalent followed by several years of professional experience
  • In-depth organizational knowledge
  • Excellent knowledge of social security and the organization of the cantonal social system
  • Taste for contact with administrations, ease of communication and interest in social issues
  • Dynamic and versatile personality, working precisely and comfortable with numbers, sense of organization and coordination, good ability to integrate into a team
  • Good command of common IT tools
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language
