Who are we?

The Friburg detention facility (EDFR) brings together prison structures in the canton of Fribourg. It has a status under public law and has legal personality. It is administratively attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport (DSJS), under the high supervision of the Council of State. The institution shall ensure the custody, accommodation, care and treatment of prisoners entrusted to it and shall contribute to the achievement of the objectives assigned to the execution of custodial sentences and measures.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Provide custody and ongoing supervision of detainees, taking into account the various detention regimes
  • To operate the kitchen of the Central Prison site in collaboration with other detention officers
  • Monitor the presences, provide safe support during the inmate's travels and ensure that the inmate is adequately looked after by the inmate's successor
  • Collaborating in the resocialization of prisoners through positive action
  • Respecting discipline, order and security among detainees
  • Ensure and respect hygiene and food concepts in accordance with legal requirements
  • Manage bugdet and supply

Profile desired

  • Cook CFC, or equivalent training in a mouth trade, with several years of professional experience
  • Federal Patent of Detention Officer of the Swiss Centre of Competence in Enforcement of Penal Sanctions, or Be Ready to Attend Training
  • Good physical and mental condition, as well as flexibility and stress resistance
  • Meaning of human relations, communication and conflict management
  • Be willing to work in turns (irregular working hours including Saturday, Sunday and holidays)
  • Be willing to intervene outside working hours for cases of force majeure
  • Good knowledge of IT tools (MS Office)
  • Excerpts from prosecutions and blank criminal records
  • Be of Swiss nationality or holder of a licence C
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language
