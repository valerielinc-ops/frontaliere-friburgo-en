Who are we?

The Environmental Service SEn ensures a balance between man and the environment under federal water protection and environmental protection laws. To this end, it monitors the quality of the environment, contributes to the prevention of harmful interference with new activities, ensures the remediation of existing installations causing damage and informs the authorities and the public about the behaviour causing harmful damage to the environment.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

Training course for 6 months

  • Support the Deputy Head of the Service in the coordination of parliamentary affairs, consultation of cantonal and federal laws and directives
  • Support the management of the secretariat of two cantonal commissions and an intercantonal association (Climate Circle)
  • Strategic annalysis of existing work processes, identification of ways to improve and development of suitable solutions
  • Support the conduct of internal projects

Profile desired
