Full description

Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for economic and cultural life in the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

As a member of CoARA, the University of Fribourg attaches particular importance to the qualitative evaluation of academic performance. The University of Fribourg (Switzerland) encourages women's scientific careers and specifically invites qualified women to apply.

Your missions

  • Courses in the discipline of canon law

Profile desired

  • Doctorate: Doctorate (canonical) in Canon Law or Doctorate in Theology on a subject of Canon Law or Doctorate in Law on a subject of Canon Law combined, at least, with a canonical license in Canon Law
  • A job authorization or equivalent scientific publications desired
  • Knowledge of Swiss cantonal and federal legislation is required, or willingness to train in this field
  • Scientific publications in the field of the post
  • Preferably, experience in seeking external funding
  • Good pedagogical and didactic skills and willingness to train
  • Provision and ability to teach online
  • Ability to reflect on the theological dimension of discipline and to integrate it into dialogue with other theological disciplines
  • The position is bilingual French/German. Therefore, the provision of education in both languages on an equal basis is required. If the person is only fluent in French or German, he will have to learn the other language of instruction within two years
