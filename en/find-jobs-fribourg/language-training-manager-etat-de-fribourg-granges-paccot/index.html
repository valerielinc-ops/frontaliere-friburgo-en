Role overview

Who are we?

The Center for Interprofessional Development (CPI), based in Fribourg since 1986, is a public centre for continuing education for adults.

We offer more than 250 courses, prepare for federal certificates and patents, and also manage training arrangements mandated by the Public Employment Service and the Canton AI Office.
