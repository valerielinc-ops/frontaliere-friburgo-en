Full description

Who are we?

The Center for Interprofessional Development (CPI), based in Fribourg since 1986, is a public centre for continuing education for adults. We offer more than 250 courses, prepare for federal certificates and patents, and also manage training arrangements mandated by the Public Employment Service and the Canton AI Office. Certified ISO 21001:2018, we place the quality, adaptability and professional support of adults at the heart of our mission.

For our field of language training, we are looking for a domain manager.

The offer of training in French and German is aimed at various adult audiences and combines several regulations: labour market measures (MMT) mandated by the Public Employment Service (SPE) for job-seekers, French fide and French courses for migrants with a view to integration, as well as courses for individuals and enterprises. The position combines the leadership of a team of 2 training managers and a dozen casual trainers. The driving share is estimated at 30-40%. You also train in either language, which represents 60-70% of the specifications.

Why join us?

Joining the ICC is contributing to the development of the skills that shape the future. You will evolve in a collaborative, stimulating and innovative environment where initiative is encouraged and where everyone can have a concrete impact. Together, we support individuals and organizations in their professional development

Your missions

  • Pilot, develop and represent the Language domain
  • Develop innovative training offering that meets market and partner needs
  • Ensure the quality of services in accordance with the ICC management system (ISO 21001:2018) and the requirements of the applicants
  • Ensure budgetary control, monitoring of indicators and continuous improvement
  • Coach and support training managers, as well as occasional trainers, and support the development of their skills
  • Evaluate teaching practices
  • Design, in collaboration with the team, training devices in presentation, distance and hybrid
  • Provide training in French and/or German and support learners until their objectives are achieved

Profile desired

  • Higher vocational training or equivalent
  • Federal adult trainer's patent (or title deemed equivalent), or willing to obtain it quickly
  • Experience confirmed in adult training, ideally in language teaching
  • Team driving experience or demonstrated ability to perform a management function
  • Strong pedagogical skills and ability to support the professional development of trainers
  • Mastery of digital training tools and common computer tools
  • Knowledge of labour market measures (LMMs), the framework or language qualifications is an asset.
  • Initiative, organizational sense, leadership, team-building and quality guidance
  • Bilingual French / German (oral and written)
