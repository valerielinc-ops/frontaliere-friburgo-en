Full description

A leader in meat, poultry, eggs and fish production, Micarna, a member of Migros Industry, is committed to being "just better". Do you share that vision? Then join us to contribute to our success. Bring your expertise, structure projects, foster collaboration and continuous improvement, while embodying our values of sustainability, transparency and accountability

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Mansioni

  • Pilot strategic projects related to construction, processing or optimization of industrial installations, integrating economic dimensions
  • Ensure the achievement of project objectives in terms of quality, time and cost, while ensuring rigorous implementation and compliance with internal and regulatory standards
  • Contribute to Micarna's industrial transformation by integrating innovative technologies, optimizing existing facilities and supporting organizational developments
  • Work across departments (production, maintenance, quality, security, procurement) and external partners (suppliers, subcontractors) to ensure project coherence and effectiveness
  • Pilot and plan budgets and perform quality control

Requirements

  • Min 5 years: Experience in managing complex industrial projects, many of which in production or processing environment
  • Completed studies (UK/EFP, HES, ES): Diploma of engineer HES, EPF or equivalent in the following fields: mechanical engineering, industrial engineering, agri-food, automation or electrotechnical
  • French (Very good knowledge) German (Good knowledge): French mother tongue with good knowledge in German (niv. B2 min.) or reverse
  • Experience in managing investment projects (CAPEX), coordinating subcontractors and budgetary management
  • Cross-cutting collaborative leadership, ability to unite around common objectives
  • Control of purchasing, negotiating and contracting processes
  • Excellent capacity for analysis, communication and coordination. Strong sense of responsibility, rigour and direction results
  • Further training in project management and/or highly desired management

Cosa offeriamo

  • Flexible workplace/telework: Up to 2 days of telework possible
  • Occupational planning: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Restaurant: Balanced lunch/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: Refund of 70% of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Training and continuing training: Attractive offer of training and further training

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the specialized department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Team knowledge: Defining a person's compatibility with the team is an important factor for both the employee and the employer in the decision that is favourable or unfavourable to engagement. That is why we are offering you the opportunity to get to know each other better in an informal setting.
  • Assessment centre: The Assessment Center is a partial part of the selection process during the postulation process. Candidates' qualifications and behaviour are analysed using different tasks and tests. This process can take one day depending on the file. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Final maintenance prior to contract establishment: Before establishing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify the remaining open issues of both parties and to resolve the contractual details. When all points are clarified, a written offer is submitted. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week
