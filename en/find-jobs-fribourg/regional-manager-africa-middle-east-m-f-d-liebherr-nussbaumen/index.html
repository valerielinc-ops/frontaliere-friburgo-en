Full description

Ready to Make an Impact? We are looking for a motivated and commercially driven Regional Manager Africa & Middle East to join our dynamic and international Earthmoving & Mining sales team. You will develop our business across assigned markets in Africa and the Middle East, working closely with dealers, customers and colleagues across Liebherr. We are looking for a self-driven, entrepreneurial professional who takes ownership, enjoys working independently and wants to make things happen in the market. Tasks:

  • Grow sales and strengthen our market position across your assigned countries.
  • Manage, support and further develop our dealer network.
  • Build strong relationships with dealers, key customers and business partners.
  • Identify and develop new customers, sales opportunities and major projects.
  • Stay close to the market through regular customer and dealer visits.
  • Drive opportunities from first contact through negotiation to order.
  • Manage your sales pipeline, CRM, forecasts and business plans.
  • Monitor market developments, competitors and emerging opportunities.
  • Work closely with colleagues across Sales, Customer Service, Finance and Liebherr production companies. Qualifications:
  • Several years of international B2B sales experience, ideally in construction equipment, earthmoving, mining or capital goods.
  • Experience in dealer or distributor management.
  • A relevant degree or equivalent professional experience.
  • A self-driven, highly motivated and entrepreneurial mindset with a strong sense of ownership.
  • Strong commercial, negotiation and relationship-building skills.
  • The confidence to work independently, set priorities and drive initiatives forward.
  • A hands-on, solution-oriented approach and a genuine interest in customers and markets.
  • Strong intercultural competence and the ability to work successfully across different countries and cultures.
  • Fluent English
  • German, French and/or Arabic would be an advantage.
  • Flexibility to work with customers and colleagues across different countries and time zones.
  • Willingness to travel extensively throughout Africa and the Middle East. Our Offer:
  • A dynamic and international team with a strong hands-on mentality.
  • A high degree of responsibility, independence and room to make an impact.
  • The opportunity to shape and grow our business across Africa and the Middle East.
  • Direct involvement with dealers, key customers and major projects.
  • Close collaboration with colleagues and experts across the Liebherr Group.
  • A varied international role where no two days are the same and opportunities for professional development. Join Our Team If you enjoy working with people, travelling, developing markets and turning opportunities into business, we would like to hear from you.
