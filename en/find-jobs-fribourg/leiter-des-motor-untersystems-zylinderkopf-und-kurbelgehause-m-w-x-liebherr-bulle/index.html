Your Responsibilities

  • You decide on the technical content and timelines for the development of new engine components, as well as improvements to existing components, in accordance with Project Management
  • You define the technical specifications of components to be developed and their key operating parameters
  • You work in a cross-functional team to ensure that the chosen technologies and specifications match project requirements (Design, Engine Tests, Electronics, Project Management)
  • You support FMEA processes and update and follow up on internal and external engine test activities
  • You regularly work with suppliers on technology surveys, component development, and tear-down activities
  • You define maintenance requirements for the subsystem and report its development status to the platform manager
  • You define the master Design and Validation Plan for the subsystem and prepare technical releases for components under development Your Profile
  • You are passionate about combustion engines and motivated to shape the performance and durability of our next‑generation diesel engines
  • You have successfully completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering
  • You have advanced knowledge in the field of combustion engines, DVP&Nr methodology, as well as troubleshooting and problem-solving
  • You have extensive experience in the development of cast iron parts, including manufacturing processes, design-for-casting and failure modes
  • You have a proven background in engine development, including component design, materials, and a solid understanding of thermo‑mechanical behavior of cylinder heads and crank cases, including heat transfer
  • You have an excellent command of English and good knowledge of French or German
  • You have an excellent command of MS Office
  • You define yourself as an analytical thinker with strong leadership skills.
