Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all over the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

The Education Centre for Primary Education (CEDP) is part of the Education Training Department of the University of Fribourg. Its mission is to train primary-level teachers, provide services in continuing training, research and development.
