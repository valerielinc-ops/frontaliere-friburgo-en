Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Directorate of Electrical Energy operates hydraulic and thermal production infrastructure, develops production facilities from renewable energy sources and provides electricity supply on the best conditions.

In order to complete our driving centre within the Electrical Energy Directorate, we are looking for our future colleague.

Mission

  • With a 3x8 7j/7 schedule, ensure with versatility the requirements of the various services of the driving centre after an internal training of more than 6 months.
  • Monitor, control and remotely manoeuvre high and medium voltage distribution facilities according to current reconstruction processes and scenarios.
  • Take the necessary measures in case of disturbances and overloads on the distribution networks. Ensure the restoration of customer feeding.
  • Monitor and optimize the planned commitment to energy production, respecting operational, safety and environmental constraints.
  • Following a real-time analysis, take the necessary corrective measures to optimize the efficient execution of system services for network balance.
  • Monitor the evolution of weather forecasts in order to anticipate the evolution of inputs into the accumulation lakes. Manage spills in real time in case of flooding according to alarm plans.

Profile

  • CFC in a technical trade
  • A few years of practical work experience
  • Being comfortable with computer tools
  • Knowledge of distribution networks and electrical production facilities, an asset
  • High flexibility, adaptability, autonomy
  • Organizational sense, stress management and good analytical capacity.
  • French mother tongue, oral knowledge of German would be a plus

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
