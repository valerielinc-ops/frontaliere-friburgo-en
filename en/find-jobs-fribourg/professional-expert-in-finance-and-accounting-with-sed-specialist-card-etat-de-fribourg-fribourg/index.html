Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office for Municipalities (GemA) is responsible for the financial and administrative supervision of municipal public corporations. Furthermore, the Office is responsible for the implementation of the policy to promote municipal mergers and with inter-municipal financial equalization. It undertakes work in the area of the division of tasks between the state and municipalities and legislative work.

Your tasks

As a finance and accounting specialist at the Office of Municipalities, you will be actively involved in the financial supervision of local public bodies and contribute to the quality, reliability and sustainability of the financial management of the municipalities. Your role will include technical aspects as well as control, advice and support. You will work in a cantonal service that focuses on municipal interests and is in direct contact with the local authorities, the higher offices and the administrative units of the state, in a working environment that promotes personal responsibility, initiative and professional development.

Financial control

  • Carrying out and documenting the controls of budgets and annual financial statements in accordance with the cantonal financial supervision concept
  • Participation in the preparation of financial, accounting and administrative statistics of local public corporations

Support and advice

  • Advising municipal public corporations on the application of public accounting standards
  • Participation in the preparation of accounting guidelines and recommendations of the Office for Corporations and Auditors

Information, communication and training

  • Participation in working groups and commissions as a representative of the office
  • Participation in courses and information events for the municipalities

Desired profile

  • Eidg. Specialist in Finance and Accounting
  • Knowledge of public accounting is an advantage
  • Proficiency in specialized IT tools (especially SAP and FINSTA)
  • Analysis and synthesis capability
  • Accuracy, independence and reliability
  • Social skills and the ability to work with numerous institutional partners
  • Strong interest in municipal issues and public administration
  • Proficiency in the German language or bilingualism (French/German) with excellent French skills
