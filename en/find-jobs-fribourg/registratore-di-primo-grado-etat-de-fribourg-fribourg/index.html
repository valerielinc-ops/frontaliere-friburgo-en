Full description

Who are we?

Justice of the Peace is the Authority for the Protection of Children and Adults. As such, it acts on request or ex officio. At the end of the investigation, it is decided whether or not to introduce a protective measure. Justice of the Peace also has jurisdiction in matters of inheritance, placements for assistance purposes and in the area of bailouts.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Writing in French and German of decisions on civil matters, including child and adult protection
  • Participation in meetings of the Child and Adult Protection Authority and taking minutes of hearings
  • Registry management: contacts with concerned persons and their relatives, Justice of the Peace partners, including legal support to professional and private trustees and other services
  • Information and advice to third parties
  • Legal research
  • Follow-up of files and preparation of administrative letters

Profile desired

  • University degree in law (licence or Master of Laws)
  • Several years of professional experience, preferably in a transplant
  • Writing experience, excellent spelling, autonomy, stress resistance and team spirit
  • Meaning of communication, organization and ease in contacts
  • Good computer skills
  • Mastery of the French or German language, excellent oral and written knowledge of the other language
