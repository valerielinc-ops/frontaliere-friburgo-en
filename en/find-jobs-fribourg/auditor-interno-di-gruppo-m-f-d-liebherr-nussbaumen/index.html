Full description

Liebherr-International AG, headquartered in Nussbaumen, Switzerland, is the holding company of the Liebherr Group. It performs group-wide functions in the areas of Finance, Compliance, Human Resources, Legal Affairs, Internal Audit, Corporate Communications, and Insurance. Responsibilities:

  • Planning and conducting operational, financial, and compliance audits at domestic and international group companies.
  • Analyzing, evaluating, and optimizing business processes, as well as developing practical recommendations for improvement.
  • Following up on audit findings and monitoring the implementation of agreed corrective actions.
  • Advising group companies on matters related to Internal Control Systems (ICS), Corporate Governance, and Compliance.
  • Analyzing and evaluating financial and management reporting of group companies.
  • Preparing clear, concise, and audience-focused audit reports and presenting audit results to management. Your Profile:
  • A successfully completed degree in Economics, Business Administration, or a comparable field from a university or university of applied sciences.
  • At least three years of professional experience in internal auditing, external auditing, public accounting, or a comparable environment.
  • Willingness to obtain the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) qualification or an equivalent professional certification.
  • Strong affinity for information technology, as well as an interest in data-driven audit approaches and digital technologies.
  • Solid understanding of business processes and economic principles, combined with strong analytical thinking skills.
  • A self-reliant, structured, and solution-oriented approach to work.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills, with the confidence to operate effectively in an international environment.
  • Fluency in German and English, both written and spoken; additional languages are advantageous.
  • Willingness to undertake international business travel of approximately 30%. Our Offer:
  • Diverse and responsible tasks within an international corporate environment.
  • Direct collaboration with management and business functions across the globe.
  • Comprehensive insights into a wide range of business processes and companies within the Liebherr Group.
  • Individual training and development opportunities, particularly in the fields of Internal Audit, Governance, and Risk Management.
  • Attractive employment conditions and flexible working arrangements. Become part of our team and actively contribute to the further development of governance, risk management, and internal control systems across the global Liebherr Group. Have we awoken your interest?
