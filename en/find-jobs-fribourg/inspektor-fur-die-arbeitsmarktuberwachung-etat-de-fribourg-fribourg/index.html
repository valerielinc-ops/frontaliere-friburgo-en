Full description

Who are we?

The Public Employment Service (SPE) is the cantonal authority dealing with the labour market in the canton of Fribourg. Its mission is to help ensure that the market is as balanced and prosperous as possible.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Organisation and execution of checks in the field of accompanying measures and the fight against work in the dark
  • Investigation of suspected offences, conduct of criminal hearings
  • Preparation of audit report and hearing minutes
  • Business closure decisions in case of infringement
  • Close cooperation with the cantonal police and the Public Prosecutor ' s Office in the fight against crime, with regard to the fight against undeclared work
  • Collaboration with oversight bodies and other authorities
  • Animation of presentations with organisations and partners
  • Performance of function-related administrative tasks

Profile desired

  • Higher vocational training
  • Criminal record extract
  • Good knowledge of the law of foreigners, social insurance and taxation
  • Flexibility, autonomy and geographical mobility
  • Stress management
  • Writing experience
  • Analysis and synthesis capacity
  • Communication and direction of contact
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language
