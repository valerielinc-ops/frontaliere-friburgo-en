Full description

Reference code: JR130276

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

You will work within Group Supply Chain Execution team to enrich the new Supply Chain Knowledge platform for Watch and Jewelry Maisons, interacting directly with them to build the right approach for publishing and enhancing Group Operating Model knowledge and ensuring W&J business continuity through it.

You will be involved in various ongoing projects to be able to capitalize on documentation and allow the W&J knowledge to spread through the various Group Supply Chain actors in a more structured way and better user accessibility.

You will also deliver the documentation within the appropriate tools, validate each key processes with Maisons and experts, and support the final publication on the platform, looking for a “User buy-in” of this reshuffled and completed documentation platform

WHAT DO YOU NEED TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE?

You will bring

  • your desire to explore the Supply Chain end-to-end business models, procedures, and best practices of our Watch & Jewelry Maisons
  • your appetence for detailed process mapping and for tools associated.
  • your soft skills to interact with the Supply Chain departments of our W&J Maisons, IT department, and Richemont Group technical experts.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCESS WITH US?

You will work with diverse teams and have the opportunity to learn about a wide variety of domains.

You will gain insights into the luxury industry from the perspective of one of the global leaders that is bridging more than two hundred years of heritage with cutting edge innovation.

Working in the close to the Swiss countryside right outside Fribourg, we offer an on-site restaurant, a beautiful setting, and a spacious open-plan working environment. The offices are close to local transport with direct access to the train station.
