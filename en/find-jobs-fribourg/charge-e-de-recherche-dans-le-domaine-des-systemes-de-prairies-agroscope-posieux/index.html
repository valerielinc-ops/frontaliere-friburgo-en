Additional information

The Forage Production and Grassland Systems group works for sustainable grassland management from the valley to the alpine area. We create knowledge for a grassland that provides both high-quality, on-farm meadow fodder and a wide range of ecosystem services. To this end, we are researching site-adapted, efficient management, new artificial meadow mixtures and the management of natural meadows. Knowledge of the ecological processes of meadows and pastures contributes to the sustainable use of their resources and to making the grasslands fit for current challenges such as climate change.
