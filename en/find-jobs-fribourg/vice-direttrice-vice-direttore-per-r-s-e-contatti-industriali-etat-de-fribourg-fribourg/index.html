Their tasks

As a member of the Executive Board, you will bear strategic responsibility for the area of Applied Research & Development (aF&E) as well as for relationships with partners from business and science. In this role, you will participate in determining the institutional orientation, supporting the expansion of research activities, strengthening cooperation and ensuring the broadcasting of the HSW-FR.

  • Participation in the management and strategic development of HSW-FR
  • Defining and implementing the applied research and development strategy and mandates
  • Manage the portfolio of research activities and projects, ensure the procurement of external funding and maintain regional, national and international cooperation
  • The managers of the institutes, centres of competence and departments affiliated to the field of R&D lead and accompany their development
  • Strengthen and consolidate relationships with companies, public bodies and institutional partners to strengthen knowledge transfer and contracting activities
  • Ensuring the scientific quality of research activities and compliance with institutional standards and the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) standards
  • Strengthening synergies between research, teaching and the professional world to increase the impact and visibility of HSW-FR

Additional information

  • Level of employment: 40-50 %
  • This position can be supplemented 80-100% by the second currently advertised position as Deputy Director / Deputy Director of Teaching

Desired profile

  • Doctorate (PhD) or equivalent degree in a relevant field, ideally complemented by further training in management or leadership
  • Demonstrated experience in leadership or team leadership within an academic institution or complex organisation
  • Recognised career in applied research and proven experience in the acquisition and management of projects funded by competitive grants
  • Good knowledge of research funding mechanisms and academic and economic innovation ecosystems
  • Experience in developing partnerships between universities, companies and public or private institutions
  • Leadership, strategic foresight and the ability to motivate teams of experts in a collaborative environment
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language as well as knowledge of English
