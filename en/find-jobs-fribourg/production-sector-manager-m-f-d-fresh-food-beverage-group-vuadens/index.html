Full description

Sushi Mania, Swiss leader in fresh sushi and Asian products ready to consume, is looking for a world leader in food production and guaranteeing quality and safety, while continuously improving operational performance.

Luogo di lavoro: Sushi Mania, Sushi Mania SA - Vuadens, Rue des Artisans 106, 1628 Vuadens

Mansioni

  • Lead your sector's production activities by ensuring timeliness, quality and food security.
  • Framework, support and develop the skills of your teams to achieve the performance objectives set.
  • Planning resources and staff according to activity while ensuring effective management of absences and schedules.
  • Animate shopfloors and follow key performance indicators to optimize productivity and reduce losses.
  • Ensure that hygiene, safety,quality and sustainability standards are applied in your sector.
  • Actively contribute to the continuous improvement of processes and collaborate with the various services to support business growth.

Requirements

  • A proven experience of 3 to 5 years in production team management: Food industry: fresh products, prepared dishes, industrial bakery, food processing, dairy products, meat, etc.
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Vocational training (CFC) in the food, technical or equivalent field, ideally supplemented by higher education (Federal Patent, ES Technician)
  • Experience confirmed in managing production teams, ideally in the food industry or an industrial environment.
  • Ability to work in refrigerated environments, to drive production using performance indicators and to lead continuous improvementprocedures.
  • Excellent relational and communication qualities at all hierarchical levels, autonomy, rigour, ability to manage priorities and make decisions in a dynamic environment.
  • Good knowledge of hygiene, quality, food safety and safety standards at work.
  • Ability to work in a chilled production environment and to collaborate effectively with the company's various services.
  • French (Very good mastery)
  • German (Basic terms)
