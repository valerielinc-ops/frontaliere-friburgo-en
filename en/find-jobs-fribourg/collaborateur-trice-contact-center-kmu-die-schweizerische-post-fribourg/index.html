Full description

Are you looking to make a fresh start and bring a breath of fresh air to our team? As a dedicated customer service professional, you can support our SME customers with their diverse concerns and contribute to their success. With us, you can put your strong communication skills to use and, together with us, professionally support numerous inquiries every year across various channels. With you, no question will go unanswered.

What you can achieve

  • You process written and telephone inquiries and answer questions from our business customers about our diverse range of products and services.
  • With your expertise, you ensure efficient support for our customers by advising them and offering tailored solutions.
  • You handle requests via channels such as email, live chat or social media and work with various systems (e.g. SAP, CRM).
  • If you have never heard of SAP or CRM, don't worry. You can also join us as a career changer or returner.
  • In this case, you will receive several weeks of specialized training and support from our communications professionals. Even after your onboarding, we will always have an open ear for your questions.
  • With us, you work neither at night nor on Sundays - but partly from home! Our attractive working hours are Monday to Friday, 07.30 - 18.00 hours.

What you bring

  • You have completed basic vocational training (EFZ). A career change or return to work is possible.
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of interacting with people help you support our customers in the best possible way.
  • With initiative and a solution-oriented approach, you handle our customers' concerns effectively and successfully.
  • You enjoy working with digital tools, and even if you are not yet familiar with all programs, you learn quickly.
  • You have very good written and spoken German skills as well as very good written and spoken skills in a second national language. English is an advantage.
