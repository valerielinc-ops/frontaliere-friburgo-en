Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Hochschule für Technik und Architektur Freiburg (HTA-FR) is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual training at bachelor and master level.

Their tasks

The degree program Electrical Engineering of the HTA-FR offers a practice-oriented engineering training with the specializations Energy Systems as well as Embedded Electronics and Signal Processing. This in close networking with the applied research institute iSIS, which in cooperation with the regional economic fabric develops innovative solutions in the field of intelligent and secure systems.

Tasks and areas of activity

  • Teaching at bachelor and master level as well as in advanced signal processing, data science and embedded artificial intelligence
  • Acquisition of projects and development of an applied research activity at the Institute iSIS
  • Management and strategic development of the signal processing laboratory
  • Representation of HTA-FR in relevant professional and scientific networks

Desired profile

  • Completed engineering degree in electrical engineering, ideally with doctorate or equivalent scientific and technical qualification
  • Solid professional experience in applied research and in the industrial environment
  • In-depth expertise in signal processing, machine learning and embedded systems for the analysis, use and exploitation of measurement data, including the analysis of complex signals, filtering, data fusion, estimation, detection, information gathering and integration of physical and data-based models
  • High teaching competence and pleasure in teaching as well as in the care of students
  • Experience in the acquisition, management and valorisation of third-party funded aF&D projects
  • Good communication and team skills
  • Mastery of the German or French language as well as good knowledge of the other language and English
