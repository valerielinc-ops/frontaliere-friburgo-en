Your missions

  • You carry out dimensional checks of our components (hydraulics and combustion engines) on Zeiss 3D measuring machines,
  • You analyze and interpret the measurement results,
  • You work closely with production employees,
  • You respect metrology procedures and good practices,
  • You participate in the continuous improvement and optimization of control methods,
  • You are willing to work a 3x8 shift schedule. Your profile
  • You hold a CFC as a polymechanic (m/f/x) or equivalent training,
  • You ideally benefit from initial experience in three-dimensional measurement (Zeiss),
  • You are proficient in reading plans and interpreting the ISO GPS rating,
  • You have good knowledge of French,
  • You are flexible on working hours (3x8),
  • Your personality is characterized by your rigor, your precision, your commitment and your autonomy.
