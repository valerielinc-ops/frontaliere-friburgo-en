Full description

High quality fresh chocolate - This is what the Swiss chocolate factory Läderach represents. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries throughout the group, and the trend is on the rise. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glaris. It is here, in the picturesque setting of the Alps, that we produce our fresh chocolates and constantly develop new and innovative ideas within agile teams. The company's retail sector comprises more than 250 chocolate shops in more than 30 countries. This is where we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to rediscover fresh chocolate constantly. You want to enjoy it too? Then join us.

What's waiting for you

  • You advise clients with professionalism and sell our wide range of products, including by accompanying them in the choice of gifts
  • You will actively meet our customers, identify their needs and propose suitable solutions
  • You perform cash transactions and guarantee accurate and correct accounting
  • You highlight our refined assortment of confectionery and chocolates in a neat and attractive way
  • You also participate in the production activities as well as the restocking and maintenance of sales surfaces
  • You apply our high quality requirements with commitment and thoroughness
  • You ensure constant adherence to hygiene standards and the concept of freshness
  • You work closely with your team and help create a positive working environment

What you bring

  • You have an experience in the retail business and you are passionate about selling and direct contact with customers
  • You quickly identify the needs of the clientele and advise it with friendliness, in a personalized and convincing way
  • You are fluent in German and English; knowledge of French and other languages is an asset
  • You work with a sense of responsibility, reliability and care for quality, while strictly respecting hygiene and cleanliness standards.
  • You appreciate teamwork, willingly assume responsibilities and wish to continue to develop in the areas of sales and service
  • You are flexible and willing to work on weekends and holidays

What we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position full of responsibilities
  • The possibility of bringing new ideas
  • A culture of frank and respectful feedback
  • Attractive sales premium related to achieving common objectives
  • Generous discounts on our assortment
  • Chocolates in the workplace
  • Good accessibility to all sites

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.
