Full description

We operate in Western Switzerland and support our customers in implementing their projects with comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions. Our offer includes the generation and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, building automation, sustainable mobility, air conditioning and water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we promote the development of the skills of our 2,450 employees, including 245 learners, and support the reconciliation of professional and private life.

The Energy Solutions division develops holistic offerings in the areas of consulting, planning, execution, maintenance and operation. It is also the central point of contact for major projects as well as for energy efficiency and automation, electrical engineering, facility management and innovation.

Their tasks

  • Collaboration in the implementation of interdisciplinary energy projects as a project engineer in the field of heating and air conditioning technology – from the preliminary study to commissioning.
  • Responsibility for the planning of major projects, in particular district heating and cooling centres and the associated networks.
  • Overall responsibility for planning, coordinating and allocating resources as well as managing your projects in technical, financial and scheduling terms. Thanks to your in-depth expertise, you will develop optimal technical solutions.
  • Active participation in the further development of the products "Contracting" and "Energy Performance Contracting (EPC)".
  • Close cooperation with public authorities, developers and architectural and engineering firms in the implementation of the projects.
  • Thanks to your sociability and negotiating skills, you maintain good relations with our suppliers.

Your profile

  • Technical higher education (FH) as an engineer for heating, ventilation and air conditioning technology (HLK) or an equivalent qualification.
  • Proven experience in the management, conception and planning of interdisciplinary technical projects.
  • Practice-oriented personality who is able to take responsibility for complex projects from the preliminary study to commissioning and integrate automation and facility management concepts.
  • Strong customer orientation, a high level of initiative and proactivity as well as team spirit and a strong practical understanding.

This is what we offer you

  • A dynamic and modern working environment that promotes the reconciliation of professional and private life.
  • Attractive social benefits of a modern company, fair working conditions and a wide range of development opportunities.
  • A strong team spirit and a committed team that works with pride: “Together we are on the road to success.”
