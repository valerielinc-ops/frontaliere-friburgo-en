Role overview

Your Responsibilities

  • You will define and implement the team's long-term vision, objectives, and testing strategy in alignment with departmental goals.
  • You will plan, coordinate, and oversee functional validation programs on hydraulic component test benches.
  • You will ensure high-quality validation standards and drive innovation by establishing and continuously improving testing methodologies and best practices.
  • You will analyze test results and translate findings into actionable recommendations for product improvement.
  • You will collaborate closely with Design Engineering, Quality, and Production teams to align testing priorities and support product development.
  • You will develop and implement standardized testing procedures and data analysis methods to improve efficiency, reliability, and repeatability of validation activities.
  • You will monitor project progress, resource allocation, and team performance, providing regular updates and recommendations to department management.
  • You will ensure compliance with safety standards across all testing operations and laboratory activities.
