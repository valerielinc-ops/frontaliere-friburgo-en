Full description

Who are we?

At a time when access to reliable and quality information is crucial, the BCU Fribourg offers a learning, discovery and memory space in the heart of the city and canton.

On-line and in its new building, it offers important documentary resources and provides access to scientific information. It also preserves the cantonal cultural heritage and animates rich cultural programming.

Why join us?

We offer a dynamic environment, in the context of the creation of a new library in the city centre providing for the development of modern services. BCU is a founding member of SLSP (national network) and is involved in the development of Swisscovery services.

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • With a team of eight people, you manage the various stages of the circuit of acquisitions of printed and electronic documents and ensure support for the BCU-Centrale and the documentary centres at the University
  • In collaboration with other sectors, you are involved in the development of digital offer, the management of free access and in-store funds, the management of financial resources and the seizure of invoices, the coordination of the Fribourg institutional area (IZ 8) and the library management system.
  • You define the general collections acquisition policy and select documents to be acquired by purchase, subscription or donation for the BCU-Centrale
  • You participate in the strategic conduct of the institution, particularly in your area of competence

Profile desired

  • You have a full university education, as well as additional training in documentary information or a Master in Information Sciences
  • You have professional experience in the field of documentary information; experience in leading a team is an asset
  • You are comfortable with organizational and financial issues
  • You have a broad general culture
  • You speak French or German with good knowledge of the other language and English

Competences

Analysis and synthesis Team leadership Flexibility Strategic thinking Responsibility
