Security Assistant — groupe Micarna

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Security is more than a priority for you, is it a real state of mind? Join Micarna SA, a leading producer of meat products in Switzerland. As a Safety Assistant, you contribute to risk prevention, sensitize teams and actively participate in a strong safety culture.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Advise and support processes on safety and health issues
  • Participate in the identification and assessment of hazards related to the activities, facilities, working environment and behaviour of employees
  • Propose, implement and monitor preventive measures in collaboration with processes to reduce accidents, occupational diseases and health risks
  • Train, sensitize and support staff and managers on safety and health issues
  • Manage and update STPS documentation (instructions, risk analyses, training, controls, action plans)
  • Conduct audits, inspections and controls to verify compliance with legal and internal requirements
  • Support the security officer in developing measures to achieve the objectives set

Required

  • 1-3 years: Experience in a similar position ideally in industry (a good agro or meat sector)
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): CFC-level training completed with STPS Safety Assistant Certificate or willingness to complete training
  • French (Very Good Knowledge) German (Preferably): You have very good knowledge in French with ideally good knowledge in German
  • You have good analytical and synthesis skills while having a sense of rigour
  • You know how to work independently while enjoying teamwork and you have a great sense of responsibility.
  • You're willing to work in 2 shifts from 6am to 3pm or 9am to 7pm.
  • You have very good knowledge of computer tools (Suite Office)
  • Ideally, you get training for chemical risk prevention in business

Contatto

Candidature and contact Sabrina Zimmermann

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Team knowledge: Defining a person's compatibility with the team is an important factor for both the employee and the employer in deciding whether or not to engage. This is why we offer you the opportunity to get to know each other better in an informal setting.
  • Final maintenance before the establishment of the contract: Before establishing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify the remaining open issues of both parties and to resolve the contractual details. When all points are clarified, a written offer is submitted. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we conduct an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 100%
