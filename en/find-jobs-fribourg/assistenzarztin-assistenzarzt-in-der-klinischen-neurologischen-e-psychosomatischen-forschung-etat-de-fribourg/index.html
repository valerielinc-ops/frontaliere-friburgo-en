Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser all in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

A multidisciplinary research group in the field of clinical neuroscience, consisting of neurologists, psychologists and engineers. The research focuses on neuropsychiatric diseases and in particular on functional neurological disorders. Our methods include brain imaging using MRI, transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS), electrophysiology (EEG, recording movements using EMG), biological analyses (genetics, cortisol) and neurobehavioral tasks.

Current research group website: https://www.neuroscience.unibe.ch/research/research groups/functional neurological disorder fnd research group/

Become part of our team!

We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg.

The research group is led by the new Professor of Neurology and offers a national (with the Unibe, Unige, EPFL, ETH) and international network (with the Functional Neurological Disorder Society [FNDS], the American Neuropsychiatric Association [ANPA], the European Academy of Neurology [EAN] and the American Academy of Neurology [AAN]).

Benefits

  • Active participation in the specialised consultation on functional neurological disorders (FNS)
  • Opportunity to actively participate in group research projects
  • Promotion of academic careers through publications in peer-reviewed journals
  • Acquisition of experience in teaching and supervision of master students
  • Participation in congresses and establishment of a research network
  • Possibility to participate in an MD-PhD programme (Swiss National Science Foundation and/or BeNeFri programme or other Swiss graduate schools)

Their tasks

Temporary contract for one year.

  • Participation in the clinical part (examination of patients) of several research projects under the supervision of the Professor of Neurology and a postdoctoral researcher
  • Building databases, learning the basics of data analysis in research
  • Collaboration in publications in peer-reviewed journals
  • Participation in the clinical teaching of neurology for students

Desired profile

  • Swiss medical diploma (or federal recognition)
  • At least 2 years of clinical experience in neurology and/or internal medicine
  • Interest in functional neurological disorders
  • Basic knowledge in clinical research (“Good Clinical Practice” course)
  • Experience and/or strong interest in teaching
  • Mastery of German and French as well as good knowledge of English
