Be part of our team!

Do you like to advise, develop and build relationships of trust? Our front-office team in Belfaux is looking for its future customer advisor.

What are you waiting for?

  • Developing sustainable relationships: acquisition and monitoring of new private clients
  • Valorize your expertise: recovery and development of a portfolio of private clients
  • Be a trusted partner: financial advice, investment and provision
  • Building a lasting relationship: customer support for all types of financial and real estate issues
  • Contributing to our development: collaboration in market research and advocacy
  • Diversify your activity: monitoring and development of an enterprise clientele, in addition to the main activity with private customers

What are you bringing?

  • You benefit from completed banking training or an equivalent level in one of the areas of finance (financing, investments, provisioning and financial planning)
  • You have a solid experience in financial advice and know how to create solutions tailored to customers' needs
  • You know the regional economic fabric well and understand local issues
  • You have an active network of clients and relationships in the region and like to develop it
  • You have a professional presentation and ease in the exchanges that allow you to create relationships of trust
  • Knowledge or experience in consulting with corporate clients is a plus
