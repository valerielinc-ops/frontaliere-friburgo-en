Full description

We give you the opportunity to get involved. With your team, you're helping to get our clients excited and shape the customer advice of the future. We are looking for a personality that anticipates and engages proactively.

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Bank, Rue de Romont 4, 1701 Fribourg

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but we can only consider online applications. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Through your professional advice in the core areas of investment, financing and foresight, you help your clients achieve their financial goals.
  • Your proactive approach allows you to develop business with your clients, with the aim of Migros Bank becoming their main financial partner.
  • You bring your ideas, while supporting Migros Bank's digital transformation, and you help us shape the future of our institution.

Requirements

  • Min. 3-5 years in the global 360° council: Private customers
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Financial Council
  • Good knowledge in the areas of investments, mortgages and foresight.
  • From experience in the 360° global consultancy to individual customers.
  • A personality that corresponds to the human values of Migros Bank, lives them and shares them.
  • A positive attitude, the ability to identify the needs of clients and to energize them through an innate sense of communication.
  • French (Very good mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Centralised workplace: Central workplace, very well accessible
  • Child care: Up to CHF 300 per month per child for external child care
  • Individual continuing training: Attractive contributions to external training
  • Migros Bank: Accounts, mortgages and preferential financial benefits
  • Points: Up to 5 additional points per purchase value franc
  • Offers for employees: Annual generous "well-being" contribution
