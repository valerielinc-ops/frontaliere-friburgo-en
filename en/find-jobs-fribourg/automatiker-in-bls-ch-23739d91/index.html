Full description

According to statistics, rail is one of the safest means of transport in the world. But behind the figures are people – people like you! Every year, 60 million passengers rely on your skills and ambition to keep the vehicles in reliable working order and thereby ensure their safety. This makes you proud as a skilled professional.

What awaits you

  • Carry out fault analyses followed by demanding repair work on vehicles and components
  • Perform a wide range of maintenance work on the passenger vehicle fleet
  • Carry out demanding repairs on the passenger vehicle fleet and its systems
  • Accurately record maintenance and fault data
  • Schedule, process and finalize vehicle stays
  • Opportunity to move to a newly built workshop in Oberburg from 2030

What you bring

  • Successfully completed training as an automotive mechatronics technician, truck mechanic, automation technician or equivalent
  • IT user skills
  • Very good spoken and written German skills
  • Willingness to work regular night and weekend shifts

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning possible for your tours and shifts
  • Receive a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are additional allowances for working at night and on weekends.
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten days of holiday per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal learning and course offering are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. More than that: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What connects us in all this? Pride in what we do every day. For us, mobility has many facets. And many exciting jobs.
