Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for profiles of (M/F) SAV Terminator to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Perform termination operations on watch components in compliance with manufacturing standards
  • Ensure the polishing and satining of the cases and bracelets of the watches returned in VAS, in accordance with their original finish
  • Perform washing and cleaning of components
  • Perform the necessary self-checks and inspections to ensure the aesthetic and functional conformity of the parts
  • Identify quality gaps and contribute to necessary corrective actions
  • Participate in continuous improvement of the workshop's processes and performance indicators

Profile

  • Terminator CFC in watchmaking dressing or equivalent formation
  • Experience confirmed in a similar function, ideally in a VAS workshop
  • Mastery of a computer system related to the trade
  • Manual skill, precision, thoroughness, finesse and dexterity
  • Ability to analyse and work independently
  • Customer orientation, listening, open mind and team spirit
  • Rigor, sense of organization, consistency, attendance and flexibility

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
