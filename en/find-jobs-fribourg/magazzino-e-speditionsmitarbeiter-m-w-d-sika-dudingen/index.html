About the role For our production site in Düdingen/FR we are looking from 01. January 2027 for freight forwarding Sealing&Bonding/Industry

Warehouse and forwarding staff (m/f).

The following exciting tasks await you

  • Carriage of goods incoming and outgoing packaging, finished material and raw material
  • Correct data maintenance in SAP and other systems
  • Close cooperation with production and materials management
  • Support of the team leader in his tasks and, if necessary, in projects

Your skills and experience

  • Versatile and thinking person with a completed vocational training – ideally in the field of logistics or with experience in the field of logistics
  • SUVA recognised forklift certificate
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, additional language skills are advantageous
  • Flair for mechanical and electrical automation systems is an advantage
  • Good PC knowledge is required and SAP knowledge is an advantage

Why you should come to us The Sika is a kind of "world SMEs": locally anchored in over 103 countries, at home all over the world. This opens up exciting prospects in Switzerland and beyond the borders Uncomplicated leaders who trust, promote and actively support. A family atmosphere makes working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit If you have any questions, Jacqueline Zbinden, HR Manager, will be happy to provide you with further information (tel. 058 436 6105). Do you feel addressed? Then apply online. We look forward to meeting you!

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemicals company with a leading position in the development and manufacture of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in the construction and automotive industry. Sika has subsidiaries in 103 countries around the world and produces in over 400 factories. The more than 33,700 employees generated an annual turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
