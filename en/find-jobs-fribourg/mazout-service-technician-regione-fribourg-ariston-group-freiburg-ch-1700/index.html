Full description

ELCO is the leading provider of high-end heating solutions and a first-class service to meet the needs of consumers throughout the lifecycle of its systems and products. High quality advice, products, systems and services. ELCO has been at the cutting edge of burner, condensing boiler and solar energy technology for decades, thanks to over 1.7 million heating systems installed throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering both smart solutions and technologies, designed to meet the needs of all types of domestic or commercial projects, guaranteeing maximum energy saving.

To strengthen our service team in the Fribourg region , we are looking

MAZOUT SERVICE TECHNICIAN

Your tasks – varied and responsible

  • Meeting the expectations of our customers both technically and through the maintenance of a quality customer relationship by effectively contributing to their satisfaction
  • After a period of training and introduction, you will be responsible for the commissioning, maintenance and troubleshooting of facilities in the geographical area.
  • Your professionalism and rigor will enable you to carry out your activities in full compliance with contractual commitments and rules relating to the safety of property and persons
  • Ambassador of the image of our company, you advise your customers, including on sanitation
  • You are also a support to your colleagues in external sales in connection with the sale of facilities
  • You also periodically ensure the picket service

Your profile – technically competent, convincing personally

  • Attached to the quality of the service and endowed with an excellent relationship with customers, you benefit from vocational training (with obtaining a CFC) in the field of electricity of preference or other technical profession; combustion controller training (ARPEA) or federal patent is an advantage
  • Your team spirit, your commitment and your taste for the challenge are all the strengths that will allow you to succeed in your role and evolve within our company

Our offer – your chance to help shape our service

We offer you the opportunity to join a leading company in its field where customer experience represents every day an opportunity to make a difference to our customers. You will integrate a team of professionals and a pleasant working environment in which you will benefit from quality training on our heating solutions, a fully equipped service vehicle and modern working tools.
