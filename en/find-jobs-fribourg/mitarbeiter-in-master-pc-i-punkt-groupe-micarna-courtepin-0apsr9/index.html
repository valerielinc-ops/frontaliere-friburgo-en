Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros.

Mansioni

  • You weigh the goods (pork, sow, beef, cow and veal) at each cutting stage. You manage weighings according to the requirements of the labels while ensuring the traceability of the products.
  • You prepare and process fresh meat orders according to internal needs. You separate labels and manage orders via SAP.
  • You ensure rigorous product monitoring to ensure traceability at every stage of the process.
  • You provide support in different sectors for tasks related to cutting, preparing and managing products.

Required

  • Professional experience: 3-5 years of experience in the meat field.
  • Initial or continuing training completed: In the food field.
  • Good knowledge of meat pieces.
  • Use with computer tools (SAP, etc.).
  • Increased sense of hygiene and compliance with current standards.
  • Ability to work under pressure and manage stress.
  • Team spirit and autonomy in work.
  • Physical resistance (manipulation of boxes from 15 to 30 kg).
  • Personal means of transport recommended for access to the workplace.
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Professional provision: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Restaurant: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Migros Club School: 70% reimbursement of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
