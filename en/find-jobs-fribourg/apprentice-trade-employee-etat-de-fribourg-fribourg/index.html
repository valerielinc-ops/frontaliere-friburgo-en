Full description

Who are we?

The Child and Youth Service SEJ is committed to children and young people in the canton of Fribourg. It is the specialized cantonal department responsible for the implementation of the policy on children and youth and child protection.

Why join us?

You are interested in the profession of business employee and you want to give additional meaning to your training by working for children and young people. Within a small, cohesive and motivated administrative team and in a pleasant atmosphere, you will learn a demanding profession that offers you many future possibilities. We also encourage the choice of integrated professional maturity.

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

You will be trained in the work and administrative processes of our secretariat by working with IT tools and specific programs, you will participate in the tasks of our accounting sector and will be required to hold our reception. You will also learn how to manage inventory of equipment. You will develop both organizational and relational skills. You will be accompanied during the 3 years of your apprenticeship in order to gain autonomy and then reach the labour market with the best possible assets.

Profile desired

  • Mandatory: minimum 20 years in 2027
  • Successful completion of compulsory education
  • Other professional experiences welcome

Applications will be processed in the order of arrival. Please apply only online by sending your complete application package with cover letter.
