Full description

Position "Management Controller, RFSM Marsens" at RFSM - Friborg Mental Health Network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health center for the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friborg Mental Health Network (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a medical, nursing and psychosocial skills center specializing in mental health.

Your missions

To complete the finance department based in Marsens

  • Support and advise budget managers in the establishment and monitoring of their budgets and provide them with the analyzes necessary for the financial management of their activities and the implementation of financial monitoring and reporting tools.
  • Participate in the establishment of analytical accounting by work center according to REKOLE® standards and in the establishment of dashboards and statistics
  • Manage and monitor projects according to the needs of the institution and department
  • Ensure replacement of colleagues within the controlling team

Desired profile

  • Higher education in finance, management control or business economics (HEC, HEG, University or training deemed equivalent)
  • Proven professional experience in management control, ideally acquired within a hospital or health institution
  • Excellent command of Excel and very good comfort with usual IT tools
  • Analytical mind, rigor and “service” orientation
  • Excellent interpersonal skills and ease in collaborating with department managers
  • Excellent oral and written command of French, with good knowledge of the German language
