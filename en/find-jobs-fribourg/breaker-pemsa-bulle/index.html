Your assignment

  • Read and interpret the designs of the armor and technical documents.
  • Prepare, cut, model and assemble the metal armor.
  • Place the bars, networks and armor cages in the formwork.
  • Ensure the binding and fixing of the elements according to the applicable standards.
  • Check the conformity of the works before the concrete casting.
  • Make sure the safety standards at the site are respected.

And now we're talking about you?
