CONTEXT

As Master Data Administrator, you will maintain customer, vendor and pricing-condition master data for EMEIAT (Europe, the Middle East, India, Africa and Turkey) You will join our dynamic and specialized European Regional team and contribute to master data management and operational data excellence in a multicultural environment. The position is an 18-month fixed-term contract based at our Villars-sur-Glâne site, with occasional travel to our Meyrin Campus.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

In this role, you will be responsible for

  • Maintaining customer, vendor, and pricing-condition master data, ensuring accuracy and consistency across the EMEIAT region
  • Reviewing master data requests for completeness and consistency, clarifying missing or conflicting information with business stakeholders
  • Performing data quality checks to identify duplicate records, incomplete fields and inconsistencies, and coordinating corrections with the relevant teams
  • Supporting global stakeholders with master data queries, updates, and priorities
  • Ensuring timely master data delivery and escalating issues
  • Addressing recurring data issues to improve processes

YOUR COMPETENCIES

  • Proficient in MDM principles and data quality standards
