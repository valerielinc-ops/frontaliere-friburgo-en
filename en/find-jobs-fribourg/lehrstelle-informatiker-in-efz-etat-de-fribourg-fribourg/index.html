Who are we ?

The Friborg University of Engineering and Architecture (HEIA-FR) is a member of the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual training at bachelor's and master's level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy in favor of equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of backgrounds and profiles that makes the strength of the State of Friborg. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To find out more, follow this link.

Your missions

  • The training, lasting 4 years, offered at HEIA-FR is offered for the two career orientations: Application Development or Operations and Infrastructure. You can indicate your preference when submitting your application (for example in your cover letter).
  • Apprenticeship takes place in dual mode (work in a company and professional courses at EPAI Friborg alternating).
  • We ask apprentices to follow the integrated technical professional maturity program.
  • We offer the opportunity to evolve in an environment conducive to professional training.
  • You will be accompanied and supported by experienced trainers, specialists in the profession.
  • You will develop knowledge and skills in line with the needs of the job market.
  • At the end of your apprenticeship, you will have all the skills to find a job or start higher education.

Additional information

  • Duration of apprenticeship: 4 years
  • Complete application file: Cover letter, CV, and depending on your situation, report cards (for students) or copies of diplomas and work certificates (for people already in employment)

Desired profile

  • Compulsory education completed (pre-gymnasial or general section) with very good results in mathematics and languages. The final diploma is required to start the apprenticeship
  • Motivation, desire to learn, patience and perseverance
  • Interest in IT and new technologies
  • Sense of organization, responsibility and precision
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language. Knowledge of English would be an asset
