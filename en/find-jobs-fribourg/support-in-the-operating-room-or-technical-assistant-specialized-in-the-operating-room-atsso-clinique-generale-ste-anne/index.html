Founded in 1932, the Clinique Générale has given itself the mission to provide excellence, accessible to all, in a human-sized facility. The Clinique Générale and its staff welcome patients in a modern and pleasant environment in the heart of the city of Fribourg. The Clinique Générale offers high-quality medical care and personalized follow-up in the following areas: orthopaedics, musculoskeletal trauma, hand surgery, neurosurgery, surgery, gynaecology as well as oto-rhino-laryngology (ORL), oncology and pain treatment.
