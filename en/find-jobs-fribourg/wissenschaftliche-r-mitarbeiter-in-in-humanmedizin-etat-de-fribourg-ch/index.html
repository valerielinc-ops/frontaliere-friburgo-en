Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Directorate for Spatial Development, Infrastructure, Mobility and Environment (RIMU), through its offices, ensures responsible spatial development, mobility and adequate protection of the environment, provides efficient, secure and needs-based transport infrastructure and ensures efficient management of state-owned properties. Sustainable development and agglomeration policy are also subordinate to it.

As a staff unit, the General Secretariat assists and advises the Head of the Directorate in his daily management tasks. The tasks of the General Secretariat shall include, in particular, the coordination of the activities of the Directorate or departments associated with the Directorate.

Their tasks

As a research assistant in the General Secretariat of RIMU, you participate in the development and implementation of the strategic activities of the Directorate. In this role, you are particularly responsible for:

  • assisting and advising the Director
  • Analysis and evaluation of data
  • the preparation, analysis and follow-up of strategic dossiers
  • the management and coordination of institutional and cross-cutting projects, in particular in the area of mobility plans;
  • the preparation of reports, analysis papers and the basis for decision-making;
  • Participation in the preparation of responses to parliamentary initiatives

Desired profile

  • Master’s degree in Economics, Political Science or an equivalent field
  • Proven experience in managing, developing and coordinating projects
  • Strong analysis, synthesis and editing capabilities
  • Ability to work independently and process several dossiers simultaneously
  • Strong social skills, ability to represent positions and a secure presence in an institutional environment
  • Consulting competence, team spirit and strong communication and service orientation
  • Sense of public interest and good understanding of public administration challenges
  • Mastery of the French language as well as very good knowledge of the German language – or vice versa
