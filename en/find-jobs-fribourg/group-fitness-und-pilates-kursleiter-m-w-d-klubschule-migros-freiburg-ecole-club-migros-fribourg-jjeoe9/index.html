Full description

Under the motto "As diverse as you", the Club School is a meeting place to get inspired and experience meaningful leisure activities. The Club School offers an attractive range of language, movement, and creative courses, thanks to the support of the Migros Culture Percentage. You will support our participants in developing their talents and achieving their goals.

Duties

  • Preparation, execution, and supervision of Group Fitness and/or Pilates courses
  • Practice-oriented teaching according to adult education principles
  • Course design taking into account the prior knowledge of each participant
  • Regular exchanges with the Club School administration
  • Active participation in continuous training

Requirements

  • Experience in teaching adults: in the relevant field or a comparable function
  • Completed initial or continuous training: Group Fitness and/or Pilates diploma
  • Neat and warm attitude towards customers
  • Independent, flexible, and reliable personality
  • Knowledge transfer in a diverse, targeted, and participant-oriented manner while sparking enthusiasm
  • Enjoyment in teaching adult audiences in small to medium-sized groups
  • French (good knowledge)
