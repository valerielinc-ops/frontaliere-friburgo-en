Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

# Mansioni

  • Active participation in the definition and implementation of commodity group purchasing strategies in collaboration with MIGROS Industry, ensuring that company needs are taken into account
  • Proactive risk management Procurement and implementation of appropriate measures
  • Development of market studies for assigned commodity groups
  • Evaluation and management of supplier performance as a whole, in collaboration with the stakeholders involved (Quality, R&D, Logistics etc.)
  • Preparation of tenders to optimize the total costs of the company
  • Representation of the purchasing function in product development projects: responsibility for setting up and respecting the process of purchasing from definition of functional need to contracting
  • Continuous improvement of the procurement process with the aim of optimizing total cost of possession (TCO), quality and timeliness

Required

  • Experience of at least 5 years: in a similar position, in project management and in negotiation
  • Higher school: in the technical or commercial field with specialization Purchases or Supply Chain
  • French (Very good knowledge) German (Very good knowledge) English (Good knowledge): French or German mother tongue with good knowledge of the other language
  • Relationship strength, very good communication and teamwork skills
  • Initiative, leadership, sense of responsibility and organization
  • High level of priority management and emergency management
  • Good knowledge of ERP systems (SAP one plus)
