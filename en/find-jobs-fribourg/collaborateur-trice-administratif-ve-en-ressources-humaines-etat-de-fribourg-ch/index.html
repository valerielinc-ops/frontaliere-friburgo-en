Full description

Who are we?

The Buildings Service (SBat) is a central service of the state of Fribourg whose mission is to build, acquire, rent and maintain the real estate stock of the state of Fribourg, about 700 objects. The service is organized into four sections including the Finance and Administration section, which includes the human resources sector. In order to complement the team in this area, we are looking for an administrative employee with a strong HR orientation.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Support the HR Sector Leader in Service HR Management (approximately 115 EFAs) throughout the life cycle of employees
  • Support the HR Sector Leader in the conceptualization and development of specific HR management processes and activities in Buildings Service
  • Provide the entire HR administrative process for service employees
  • Prepare and submit statistical reports relevant to the HR management of the service
  • Ensure a reference role for all applications related to the working time management tool, absences and social benefits in consultation with the Directorate of Territorial Development, Infrastructure, Mobility and Environment (DIME) and the Personnel and Organisation Service (SPO)
  • Contribute to the implementation of staff activities, activities and services
  • Participate in identifying key skills, development needs and monitoring training plans

Profile desired

  • SWC Trade Employee, or Federal Patent Specialist in Human Resources or Diploma ES
  • 3-5 years of professional experience in human resources
  • Efficient and autonomous in your work, you are comfortable with the usual computer tools of the Office 365 and PowerBI suite
  • You speak French or German, with good knowledge of the other language
