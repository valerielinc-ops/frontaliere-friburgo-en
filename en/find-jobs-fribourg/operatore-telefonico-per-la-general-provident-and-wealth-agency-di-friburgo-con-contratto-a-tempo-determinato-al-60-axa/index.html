60%, workplace: Friborg

Shape your future with us!

Do you live in the Friborg region? Do you want to get involved in a part-time professional activity with mainly customer contact? Are you comfortable on the phone, do you have a strong sense of service and customer relations as well as strong sales and negotiation skills? Perhaps you are our General Agency and we can’t wait to receive your application!

What awaits you

  • You trigger the interest of our customers / prospects by telephone
  • You manage to convert your telephone contact into an appointment and directly arrange a meeting with one of the Pension and Heritage advisors in your region
  • You suggest marketing and business development ideas remotely, you are proactive and bring dynamism and creativity to your ideas

What you bring

  • Your experience in the field of remote commercial relations (sales, telemarketing, call center)
  • Your knowledge of subjects related to Foresight or note according to the needs of the position
  • Your open, positive and engaged personality
  • Your relational ease
  • Your attentiveness and your strength of proposal both for our clients and within our Agency
  • You are fluent in French both orally and in writing, your knowledge of German is a complementary asset

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to flourish and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% off AXA products
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare subscription/contribution to general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and independent work
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Robust and professional training and development opportunities
