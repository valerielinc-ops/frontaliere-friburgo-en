Be part of our team!

For the Raiffeisen Moléson Bank, a very well-established bank in the southern canton of Fribourg, we are looking for a person like Credit Officer Senior (m/f), a corporate customer sector.

What are you waiting for?

Key responsibilities

  • Evaluate, approve or reject applications for credit within the jurisdiction assigned.
  • Provide the "Back" role in Front/Back separation for non-standard cases.
  • To act as a technical partner ("sparring partner") for client advisors on financing issues.
  • Identify and explain risks, propose alternative solutions.
  • Monitor and enforce internal regulations and directives.
  • Deal with real estate promotion financing cases and support Front teams in risk analysis.

Environment

  • Close collaboration with Front units and Risk teams.
  • Participation in projects related to the supervision of credit operations.

What are you bringing?

Experience confirmed Minimum 5 years in the analysis and validation of corporate customer credits (credit in white, real estate financing) Technical expertise Control of banking regulations and risk standards. Expertise in complex risk assessment. Skills in Recovery, an asset. Training & Certifications Higher vocational training or HES diploma in banking/finance Soft skills Rigor, analytical mind, critical sense. Excellent communication and collaboration capabilities. Solution orientation and priority management. Be part of our team! Nathalie Gremaud, Human Resources Officer (f) Integrate an institution firmly rooted in its local economic fabric.
