Missions

  • You plan and manage new construction, overhaul and upgrade projects for internal combustion engine test benches as well as hydraulic and aeronautical components (from design to reception), in collaboration with production and development services
  • You identify the requirements and develop pre-projects and specifications
  • You supervise the implementation and commissioning phases
  • You collaborate with other internal services, as well as with suppliers, and find innovative solutions to optimize the start-up phase
  • You provide support for maintenance interventions and their preparation.
  • You benefit from a professional of at least 5 years in the field of commissioning production facilities, ideally engine test benches or engine components.
  • Experience in project management and in-depth knowledge of combustion engine or hydraulic technology is a considerable asset.
  • You have engineering training in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering or process engineering. Technical training with relevant experience in the construction and operation of engine test benches or engine components will also be considered.
  • You have a very good knowledge of Windows operating systems and MS Office applications.
  • You have good language skills in German or English, French being an advantage.
  • You are independent, structured and autonomous in your work and have good analytical skills.
  • You like to take initiatives, you are innovative and customer oriented. You have a high level of commitment and you are able to work as a team.
