About the role For our production site in Düdingen we are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement in the area of adhesive production (PUC LV) a plant operator. The following exciting works await you:

  • They are part of the security culture in the company
  • They are responsible for ensuring trouble-free operation on the allocated facilities in compliance with the production programme
  • They support the minimization of false batches, waste, etc. within the process team.
  • They are jointly responsible for achieving goals and eliminating losses with regard to the overall process
  • They comply with standards as well as work instructions, laws and regulations, in particular occupational safety regulations
  • They actively promote improvement measures within the entire processes

Your skills and experience

  • Completed vocational training and proven experience in industrial production
  • A good technical flair as well as manual skill and an independent, self-responsible, reliable working method
  • Very good knowledge of German (m/s)
  • Willingness to work in a 3-shift operation
  • Good knowledge of common office software programs; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience in handling chemical raw materials is an advantage
