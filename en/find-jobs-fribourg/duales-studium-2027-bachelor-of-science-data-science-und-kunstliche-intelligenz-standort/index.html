The Position Looking for a diverse and future-oriented dual study course? Then join us in reinventing the future of healthcare. At Roche, we are developing innovative diagnostics and medicines to enable people to live longer and better lives. Join this mission and make a decisive contribution to patients worldwide from the very beginning.

Tasks

  • You get to know Roche in all its diversity: You rotate through exciting areas – from IT to logistics and finance to marketing – and see how everything is connected.
  • You make big data usable: You sammel, structure and analyze complex data sets and thus create the basis for important decisions.
  • You tell the story behind the figures: You generate real business knowledge and valuable data from confusing raw data and you generate valuable data of up to 130s.
  • You put AI into practice: You develop smart algorithms and mathematical models that enable us to accurately predict trends and business processes. - Hands-on of Day 1: You are not only in the process, but take responsibility in IT projects – from the first idea to the finished programming of your own applications.

The practical training is held at Roche Diagnostics in Mannheim. The lectures are held at the Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg in Mannheim.

We offer you the following training: You actively design your training content in close consultation with your instructors. We also offer you this:

  • Finance: Attractive remuneration as well as holiday and Christmas money.
  • Work-Life Balance: 30 days of holiday, company sports and comprehensive health care.
  • Development: intensive exam preparation, university entrance qualification (optional) and a wide range of further training.
  • Extras : International work environment, foreign missions and discounted canteen catering.

Requirements

  • General university entrance qualification
  • Specialized university entrance qualification
  • Fachhochschulreife and proof of the passed suitability test of the Dual University Baden-Württemberg

Key data How long does dual study take? 3 years. One-month, company-specific internship.
