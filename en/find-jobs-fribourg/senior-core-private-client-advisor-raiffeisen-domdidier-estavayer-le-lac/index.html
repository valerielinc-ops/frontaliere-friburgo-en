The Bank Raiffeisen Broye Vully Lacs, a bank very well established at the local level and with agencies in Domdidier and Estavayer-le-Lac, is looking for one or more of its core private clients.

What are you waiting for?

  • Detection and exploitation of customer potential through active and continuous monitoring of a client portfolio (jointly)
  • Customer advice on basic products "Safety, Payment, Investment and Planning" including savings plans in investment funds
  • Current window operations
  • Consultant support in case management

What are you bringing?

  • Banking training completed (CFC or equivalent)
  • Experience in a similar function desired
  • Sales orientation and customer solutions
