Who are we?

The University of Management (HEG-FR) in Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual and trilingual training at the Bachelor and Master level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

As a member of management, you ensure strategic responsibility for the Teaching area. As such, you contribute to the definition of institutional guidelines and ensure the excellence, consistency and evolution of the training offer of the HEG-FR.

  • Participate in the governance and strategic development of the HEG-FR
  • Conduct academic leaders and coordinate training programmes and related services (bachelor, master, continuing training, EMBA, Language and Career Centers)
  • Ensuring academic quality of programmes and continuous improvement of training
  • Supervise accreditation procedures and implementation of AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business) and Insurance of Learning (AoL) processes
  • Encourage pedagogical innovation, digitalisation and integration of artificial intelligence into teaching
  • Develop and develop the training portfolio by strengthening the attractiveness and positioning of the HEG-FR
  • Promoting synergies between training programmes, applied research and economic environments

Additional information

  • Activity rate: 40 - 50 %
  • This job can be completed by 80 – 100% with the second position currently being awarded to Deputy Director-e-Ra&D

Profile desired

  • Doctorate holder in a relevant field or equivalent training, ideally supplemented by continuing training in management, leadership or higher education
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss system of higher education and the landscape of higher education, particularly in the field of education, as well as the ability to appropriate international standards of quality assurance and accreditation, notably AACSB
  • Experience in managing and developing training programmes
  • High interest in educational innovations, digital transformation and the use of artificial intelligence in education
  • Federated leadership, sense of collaboration and ability to evolve in an expert environment
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language, as well as English
