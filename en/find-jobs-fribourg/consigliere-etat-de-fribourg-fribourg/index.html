Full description

Who are we?

The Public Employment Service (SPE) is the cantonal authority dealing with the labour market in the canton of Fribourg. Its mission is to help ensure that this market is as balanced and prosperous as possible.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • You are accompanying jobseekers to reintegrate them into the labour market
  • You detect barriers to reintegration and implement strategies to return to employment
  • You coach job seekers to develop their employability
  • You monitor compliance with legal obligations
  • You collaborate with internal and external partners and develop appropriate synergies

Profile desired

  • 3-year SWC or tertiary higher vocational training and a federal HR or social insurance certificate (possible employment training)
  • Several years of professional experience in counselling and placement, vocational rehabilitation or human resources
  • Specific knowledge in the fields of social insurance and labour market
  • Ability to manage counselling interviews in different forms (face-to-face, telephone or video), leadership and coaching skills
  • Adaptability, listening and stress resistance
  • Excellent mastery of digital tools and platforms

• Mastery of the German or French language, with very good knowledge of the other language (bilingual)
