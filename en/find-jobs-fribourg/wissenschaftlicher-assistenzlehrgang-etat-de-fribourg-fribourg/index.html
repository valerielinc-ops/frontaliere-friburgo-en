Who are we?

The Children and Youth Service (SEJ) is committed to children and young people in the canton of Fribourg. It is the specialized cantonal department responsible for the implementation of the policy on children and youth and child protection. Its benefits are for children, young people, their families, different authorities and anyone in contact with children or young people.

Why join us?

You join a small, motivated, creative and multidisciplinary team that meets the challenges and seeks innovative solutions in a challenging and demanding environment. This internship allows you to exercise your skills of collaboration and analysis and to discover the organisation and activity of a state service while making your contribution to its proper functioning.

The course is offered for 6 months.

Your missions

You work with the human resources and management of our department. You are responsible for maintaining our HR databases and tracking our HR processes. You are involved in the drafting of internal directives and in policy impact assessments in our business areas. Based on the information provided, you will be responsible for preparing arguments to explain the position of the service. To accomplish your tasks, you work with all sectors of our service as well as with different partners in the child protection network. You have contacts with the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs as well as with the Personnel and Organization Department of the State and carry out administrative work related to the assigned dossiers.

Profile desired

  • Master's degree in social/political sciences, public management, work psychology or diploma judged to be in line with the function
  • Interest in human resources, social policies and child protection
  • Excellent synthesis, good analytical skills and editorial convenience
  • Ability to work independently, organizational and planning skills
  • Confidentiality
  • Perfect mastery of the French language in both oral and written with knowledge of German
