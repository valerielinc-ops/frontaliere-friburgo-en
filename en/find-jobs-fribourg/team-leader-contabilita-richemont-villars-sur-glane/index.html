Full description

Reference code: JR129826

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO MAKE DIFFERENCE?

Attached to an Accounting Manager and based in Fribourg, you will be in charge of the following main tasks:

Accounting management and closure •

Pilot and coordinate the accounting operations of entities under the scope to ensure quality, accuracy and timeliness

Prepare and record closing entries

Ensure critical review of tasks performed by GL and Senior GL Accountants

Prepare, explain and present the monthly reporting to the Director of the Department

Document, monitor and ensure implementation of the existing internal control system

Leadership and team management

Manage and lead a small team of GL and Senior GL Accountants

Provide coaching of the team and support the increase in skills of employees, while supporting their professional development

Provide technical leadership and guide the team in solving complex accounting issues

Setting priorities, coordinating activities and ensuring the achievement of departmental objectives

Internal Relationship and Projects

Be the preferred interlocutor for our internal clients (succursals, houses, functions) for general accounting topics

Piloting and contributing to continuous improvement projects in the department

Promote and ensure the application of the Group's accounting rules

HOW WILL YOU KNOW SUCCESS WITH US?

Passionate about accounting and comfortable with numbers, you stand out for the following skills:

Strong analytical and synthesis capacity with rigour and attention to detail

Proactivity, autonomy and strength of proposal

Excellent organization and management of priorities

Resilience and ability to navigate in complex and demanding environments

At home with complexity

Sense of discretion and confidentiality

Flexibility and adaptability

Experience, Training and Job Skills

Federal accounting certificate or equivalent

Min. 8 years of accounting experience, with GL responsibilities, preferably in the international environment

Excellent knowledge of accounting standards (IFRS, CO)

Computer tool mastery (SAP, advanced Excel)

French mother tongue, you have excellent knowledge of English

Team management experience is a plus
