Full description

Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all over the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

The Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in Health Psychology is a federal-recognized post-graduate course (OFSP). It includes three Certificates of Advanced Studies (CAS), which can also be followed independently of MAS. Its mission is to train health psychologists capable of practising their profession under their own responsibility and leads to the Federal Title of Health Psychologist. It is delivered by the University of Fribourg (leadinghouse) in collaboration with the Universities of Lausanne and Geneva.

To ensure the follow-up and development of training, the Steering Committee (Prof. C. Martin Sölch, President) places the position of Training Coordinator, located at the University of Fribourg, in the competition.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • To organize the MAS programme in health psychology
  • Organize the various committees and manage the follow-up of decisions
  • Oversee administrative and logistical monitoring related to the organisation of teaching activities
  • Assist students in their post-graduate training process
  • Developing and coordinating MAS communication and publicity
  • Monitoring legal documents (e.g. study regulations, programme agreements, directives)
  • Budget monitoring of MAS activities
  • Ensure communication between different partners within the MAS

Profile desired

  • Tertiary training (HES, University) in psychology
  • Professional experience in the field
  • Good knowledge of health psychology
  • Knowledge of the different health sectors and the contexts of the three cantons Fribourg, Geneva and Vaud
  • Interest in administrative tasks, rigour and accuracy
  • Mastery of the French language
