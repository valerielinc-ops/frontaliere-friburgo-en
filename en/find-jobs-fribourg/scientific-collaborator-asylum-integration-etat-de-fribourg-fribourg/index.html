Full description

Who are we?

The Service for Social Action SASoc , competent authority in the canton of Freiburg, in particular for the conduct, coordination and supervision of the asylum and refugee system (LASI), search for an entry on 1 January 2027 or to be agreed, a scientific collaborator in this sector at 60%-70% for a fixed period until 31.12.2027 (renewable). Only online postulations by the website of the state of Fribourg jobs.fr.ch will be taken into consideration.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. For more information, follow this link.

Your missions

  • Participate in the coordination, development and monitoring of cantonal arrangements for the reception, supervision, integration and accommodation of asylum and refugee persons
  • Monitoring asylum and refugee warrants in collaboration with representatives and relevant services
  • Managing complex situations requiring a comprehensive and coordinated approach with multiple actors
  • Participate in the coordination of projects and the conduct of scientific work by the Service, including through the delivery, consultation or processing of parliamentary instruments
  • Participate in the development of the scientific expertise of the service in relation to asylum and refugees

Profile desired

  • Master's degree in social work or law or training deemed equivalent, with good knowledge of the rules of the field of social assistance and their application
  • Knowledge of migration issues and integration
  • Asylum experience, an asset
  • Dynamic and versatile personality, sense of organization and coordination, ease with legal bases, numbers and communication, good integration capacity in a team
  • Good control of common computer tools
  • Excellent command of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language; desired English knowledge
