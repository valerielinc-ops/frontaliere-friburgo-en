Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office of Archaeology of the Canton of Freiburg (AAFR) identifies, inventories and documents archaeological heritage.

It accompanies the administrative processes concerning land monuments located in archaeological zones and provides means for carrying out excavations and, where appropriate, obtaining land monuments.
