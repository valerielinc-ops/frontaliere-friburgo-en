Their tasks

The mission of this position is to support the coordinator of the administration in the monitoring and further development of processes in the areas of finance, quality and infrastructure. They play a key role in the smooth running of internal activities and at the same time ensure the connection to our various partners.

  • Preparation, control and follow-up of accounting documents and invoice orders in accordance with applicable procedures
  • Monitoring of the billing plan
  • Updating of monthly budget monitoring instruments and reporting of discrepancies or inconsistencies
  • Maintain follow-up of recommendations from external financial audits in accordance with the coordinator’s instructions
  • Support for the implementation of quality processes and the updating of documentation in accordance with the ISO 9001 standard
  • Cooperation with internal and external partners to ensure efficient processes
  • Administrative support in the area of infrastructure and close cooperation with the various departments of HSW-FR
