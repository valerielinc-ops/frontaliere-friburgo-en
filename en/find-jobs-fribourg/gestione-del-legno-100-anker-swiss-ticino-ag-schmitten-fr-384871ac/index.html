Wood construction manager 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a permanent job more than 3 years of experience as a timber construction manager

Your challenge

  • Technical planning and work preparation of timber construction projects
  • Preparation and processing of work and execution plans
  • Coordination of materials, resources and deadlines
  • Supporting project management in the implementation of challenging construction projects
  • Contact person for architects, builders and internal specialist departments

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed further training as a timber construction technician HF
  • Experience in planning and implementing timber construction projects
  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Good knowledge of CAD and planning programs
  • Responsibility and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Teamwork and communication strength

What we offer you

  • Diverse and technically demanding timber construction projects
  • High level of independence and personal responsibility
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary infrastructure
  • Collegial, motivated team with short decision paths
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Secure workplace in an established family business
