Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique des Grangettes According to agreement Type of employment: Fixed term

Reference Number: 73950

As Switzerland's largest medical network, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency services. The primary medical quality and well-being of the individual are at the centre of our concerns.

We are looking for a nurse for our continuing care service until 31.05.2027

MAIN TASKS

  • Care for patients specific to continuing care and patients in Ambulatory care.
  • Analyze and efficiently manage specific medical-surgical situations.
  • Provide preventive, curative and educational nursing care to patients.
  • Collaborate in the organization of work and in the smooth running of service activities.
  • Contribute to an interdisciplinary approach to care and teamwork.

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • Be a holder of the ES or Bachelor of Nursing Science designation HES-SO,
  • For a foreign diploma, Red Cross recognition is necessary
  • Confirmed experience in specialized acute care
  • To benefit from strong technical skills and able to work in a multidisciplinary environment
  • Motivated, effective, self-sustaining and with a high sense of responsibility, you know how to anticipate and are very organized.
  • High ability to work team and multidisciplinary
  • Sense of priorities in acute situations and good stress management
  • Relationship qualities, empathy and ease in communication
  • Availability and flexibility

In addition to an attractive workstation, you will integrate a dynamic team that is looking forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contracts. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

YOUR CANDIDATURE

The Human Resources Directorate is pleased to receive your complete application (CV, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates).

Please note that for reasons of data protection, we cannot consider paper files (which will not be returned) or applications sent by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via our recruitment site.
