For the Raiffeisen Moléson Bank, a very well established bank in the south of the canton of Fribourg, we are looking for a person as individual customer advisor (m/f).

What are you waiting for?

  • Global 360° Council and Skills in Financing, Investments and Planning
  • Detection and development of business opportunities through active and targeted customer portfolio monitoring
  • Strengthening the long-term relationship with existing customers by supporting our clients in the implementation of their financial projects
  • Targeted acquisition of new clients related to the company's growth strategy
  • Contribution to overall customer development and acquisition objectives
  • Work in synergy with other segments of the bank to achieve common strategic objectives
  • Active participation in customer events, contribution to the company's external visibility, involvement in local market development

What are you bringing?

  • Banking training
  • SAQ certification, an asset

  • Experience in acquiring new customers
  • Strong market anchor and professional network developed
  • Entrepreneurial spirit
  • Excellent relational skills and acute communication
