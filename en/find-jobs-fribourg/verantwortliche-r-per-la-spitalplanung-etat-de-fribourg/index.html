Their tasks

Within our cantonal administration we are looking for someone responsible for hospital planning. In this strategically important function, you contribute to ensuring high-quality, accessible, efficient and needs-based hospital services for the population. They control the cantonal hospital planning in close cooperation with the health institutions, the authorities and the partners of the health care system. For this purpose, you have excellent knowledge of the current challenges related to public health and hospital policy.

Their main tasks

  • Development, implementation and regular updating of cantonal hospital planning in accordance with the federal and cantonal legal framework
  • Analysis of inpatient hospital needs based on demographic, epidemiological, economic and performance data
  • Examination of the conformity of the stationary services provided by hospitals with the service contracts awarded
  • Monitoring the development of supply, the quality of services and their consistency with the needs of the population
  • Control of procedures related to the cantonal hospital list and the award of service contracts
  • Advising the cantonal authorities on strategic issues concerning the organisation of hospital services
  • Managing or participating in cross-cutting health policy projects

Desired profile

  • Master’s degree in economics or equivalent education
  • Further training in project management is an advantage
  • Professional experience in project management
  • Knowledge of the functioning of a public administration and the Swiss health care system and its financing
  • Strong analysis and synthesis skills and networked thinking
  • Ability to lead projects and shape collaboration with different partners
  • High adaptability and flexibility
  • Excellent written expression ability
  • Perfect command of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other official language
