Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

(all genres) – 100%

You are involved in the construction of heating installations. Thanks to your technical know-how and your mastery of welding, you contribute to the quality of the installations. You will also have the opportunity to pass on your know-how by accompanying an apprentice on a daily basis

Your tasks

  • Complete the installation of heating installations on new construction sites and under renovation.
  • Place the pipes and connect the different heating equipment.
  • Welding work (as required by construction sites).
  • Install heat generators (heat boilers, heat pumps, etc.).
  • Mount and connect hydraulic networks.
  • Conduct tests, leak checks and watering of installations.
  • Read and interpret implementation plans.
  • Collaborate with project managers and other trades and crafts on site.
  • To accompany and train an apprentice on construction sites by transmitting the best practices of the trade.
  • Ensure safety, quality and timeliness.

Your profile

  • Installation CFC in heating or equivalent formation
  • Experience confirmed in the installation of heating installations.
  • Mastery of the indispensable welding.
  • Good reading of plans and understanding of technical diagrams.
  • Interest in transmitting knowledge and accompanying an apprentice.
  • Team spirit, autonomy and sense of responsibility.
  • Driver's licence category B.
