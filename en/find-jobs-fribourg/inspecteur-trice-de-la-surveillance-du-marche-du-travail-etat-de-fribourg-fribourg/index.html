Appointing authority / department / institution

The Office for the Labor Market (AMA) is the cantonal authority that deals with the labor market in the canton of Friborg. Its task is to contribute to a market that is as balanced and flourishing as possible.

Become part of our team!

The state employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Your tasks

  • Organization and implementation of controls in the area of ​​accompanying measures and the fight against undeclared work
  • Investigating suspected violations and conducting interviews by the criminal authorities
  • Writing control reports and interview protocols
  • Business closures in the event of violations
  • Close cooperation with the cantonal police and the public prosecutor's office in combating undeclared work as part of the fight against crime
  • Cooperation with the control bodies and other authorities
  • Leading training courses for organizations and partners
  • Carrying out administrative tasks

  • Communication and outgoing personality
  • Mastering common IT applications
  • Fluent in German or French with very good knowledge of the other language
