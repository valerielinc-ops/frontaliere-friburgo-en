Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

In this function, you are the HR support point of a diverse and demanding technical population. Your role: to support managers, strengthen skills, manage sensitive situations and ensure consistent application of HR policies across multiple sites. You are expected for your reliability, clarity and ability to decide.

Missions

  • Talent development and performance: support managers in skills assessment, development and management. Advise and support career development decisions (development plans, training, coaching, skills assessments, promotions, internal mobility and talent management).
  • Support for employees and apprentices: follow up on the main stages of the journey (internal recruitment, onboarding, internal mobility, departures, pensions, long-term absences, etc.). Advise managers in managing individual situations and help maintain a healthy and constructive social climate.
  • HR Partnership and Consulting for Managers: acting as a trusted partner by providing pragmatic recommendations on organizational, managerial and human issues. Support decision-making and ensure consistent application of HR policies and processes.
  • Pay Policy and HR Governance: Ensure internal fairness and organizational coherence. Contribute to job evaluation, salary surveys and compensation processes. Participate in budget exercises, salary revisions and organizational reflections.
  • Proximity: develop a regular presence with teams and managers throughout the assigned perimeter. Listen to the needs of the field and ensure strong HR visibility on several sites.
  • HR collaboration and projects: work closely with the various HR teams (Talent Acquisition, HR Administration, Salaries, Training and Development, HR Projects) to ensure consistent collaborative experience and contribute to continuous improvement of HR processes.
  • HR management and reporting: analyzing HR indicators (staff, absenteeism, turnover, performance, compensation, etc.), identifying trends and making recommendations to support the company's objectives.

Profile

  • University degree, Federal Patent of Specialist in Human Resources or equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed as HRBP or similar.
  • Very good mastery of MS Office and ease with LES.
  • Ability to take position, manage pressure, remain factual. Strong analysis, direct communication, sense of responsibility.
  • Easy relationship, able to maintain direct and professional communication.
  • French mother tongue.
  • Driving licence and adapted mobility essential.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
