Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For the German-speaking outpatient clinic and the German-speaking day clinic of the RFSM in Villars-sur-Glâne:

  • Outpatient and outpatient consultations for German-speaking patients
  • Networking and networking
  • Participation in the day clinic as a referral for patients
  • Animation and co-animation of therapeutic groups within the framework of the therapeutic programme
  • Nursing activities such as nursing interviews, preparation of medicines and deposit medicines

Profile desired

  • Diploma in nursing HES/ES
  • Work experience of at least two years in hospital psychiatry and/or day clinic
  • Good knowledge of the psychiatric network of the canton of Fribourg
  • Autonomy, flexibility and initiative
  • Planning and organizational competencies
  • Experience in conducting interviews
  • Ability and interest to animate or co-animate therapeutic groups
  • Active participation in the development of therapeutic supply
  • Ability to collaborate within an interprofessional team
  • Mastery of the German or French language, with a B2/C1 level in the other language (oral and written)
