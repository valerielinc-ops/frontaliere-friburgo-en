Who are we?

The Museum of Art and History Fribourg (MAHF) is composed of three parts. The Ratzé Hotel, the former slaughterhouse, the temporary exhibition building and a garden for monumental sculptures. The Espace Jean Tinguely - Niki de Saint Phalle (Espace JTNSP) is a magical place that pays tribute to the creativity of two great artists of the twentieth century. The works gathered in both institutions document the canton's cultural history. With the permanent presentation of the collection and temporary exhibitions, they offer reading keys to the past and the present, while raising awareness of the creation of Fribourgeois artists. Many public events take place throughout the year.

Your missions

Technical assistance

  • Participate, under the direction of the technical manager, in agreement with the exhibition commissioners and management, in the assembly and dismantling of exhibitions, including packaging and transport operations
  • Appropriately assist in the handling of cultural objects in exhibitions, depots and during transport operations
  • Collaborate in the manufacture of equipment for transport, packaging, exposure and common use and maintenance of the machinery fleet
  • Support the implementation of actions that ensure the safety of staff, visitors, works and premises
  • Participate in the installation and maintenance of lighting, sound and multimedia devices
  • Under the supervision of the technical manager, manage the locations of works in the centralized reserve for the maintenance and order of the State collections (updating the movements of works and objects)

  • SWC of a building trade (carpentry, electricity, etc.) or training deemed equivalent
  • Experiences in cultural or museum institutions
  • Rigor, versatility, flexibility, and sense of responsibility. You are able to handle multiple tasks in parallel and are stress resistant
  • You know how to work as a team
  • Information technology knowledge and development capacity
  • Knowledge in the field of multimedia and video welcome
  • Driving licence B
  • Mastery of French or German with good knowledge of the other language
