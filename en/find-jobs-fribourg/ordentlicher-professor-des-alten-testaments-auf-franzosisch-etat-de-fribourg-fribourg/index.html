Who are we? Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for economic and cultural life in the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889. The Faculty of Theology at the University of Freiburg is characterized by its bilingualism and its roots in both the French and German speaking world. Thanks to its European network and its doctoral students from all over the world, it enjoys international visibility. We are looking for a personality ready to consider cultural and religious changes in Switzerland and Europe as an opportunity, to advance exegetical research within the Department of Bible Studies in accordance with its tradition, while exploring new ways in a creative way.
