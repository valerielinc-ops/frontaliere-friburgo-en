Full description

Reference code: JR135886

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Following an internal promotion, a great opportunity opens up within our Finance department in Villars-sur-Glâne, composed of about 80 employees.

As a Company Accountant, attached to our entity Richemont International SA, you will be responsible for the maintenance of the accounts of our central entity, from the collection of information from our various entities under management to the complete establishment of the Group and statutory accounts. You will also ensure compliance with Group and IFRS standards.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE DIFFERENCE?

Your main tasks will be, but not limited to

  • Actively participate in the closures and reporting of statutory and Group accounts of our central entity, and ensure their reliability
  • Pilot the accounting closing schedule of branches, coordinate the various contributors and ensure that Group deadlines are met.
  • Ensure the quality and consistency of accounting processes within the RISA perimeter, as well as the proper application of IFRS and Group rules.
  • Acting back-up on VAT and Tax topics
  • Review and analyse key HR flows and accounts and coordinate reconciliation with HR and social insurance teams.
  • Contribute to external financial audits, from preparation of analyses to follow-up on identified issues.
  • Actively participate in the transformation of RISA Accounting by identifying opportunities for simplification, standardization, automation and strengthening of controls.

HOW WILL YOU KNOW SUCCESS WITH US?

Passionate about accounting, you stand out for the following cross-cutting skills:

  • Rigor, reliability and accuracy
  • Initiative and the driver of change
  • Curiosity and willingness to learn
  • Practical approach and orientation solutions
  • Autonomy and responsibility
  • Organization and management of priorities
  • Team spirit and sense of collaboration
  • Interpersonal agility and adaptability to a complex environment

Experience, Training and Job Skills

  • University degree in finance, economics, management or similar, ideally supplemented by an accounting certificate
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in general accounting, ideally in the international environment
  • General accounting (CO and IFRS)
  • Excellent mastery of Excel and other office tools
  • Knowledge of integrated financial software, ideally SAP
  • French and English essential currents. German is an asset.

WHAT DIFFERENCES OUR GROUP?

Our real power lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our specific ability to foster untapped potential.

  • We value collegiality, curiosity and humility.
  • We encourage empathy, courage and integrity.
  • We take care of the world we live in.
