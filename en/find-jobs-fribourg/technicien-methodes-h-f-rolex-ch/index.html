Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for profiles of (M/F) Methods Technician to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Develop, develop and optimize production or control ranges, programmes and means
  • Participate in the industrialization of new products and processes, from feasibility study to production qualification
  • Conduct testing, establish experience plans and formalize results and related reports
  • Formalize, make reliable and make available the technical data necessary for operations
  • Streamline production lines and contribute to continuous improvement of industrial processes and means
  • Providing technical support to operating teams in the face of complex issues
  • Collaborate with internal partners to ensure compliance with business standards and quality of operations

Profile

• Technical training of CFC type of polymer engineer, HES engineer or ES technician in microtechnics, mechanics or equivalent • Experience in industrial methods, ideally in the fields of machining, automatic termination and/or control • Knowledge of automated means of production, digitally controlled machines, programming or control processes according to the field of specialization • Mastery of a computer-assisted design tool, ERP system and common IT tools • Ability to conduct tests, analyze results and solve technical problems • Communication skills, teamwork, autonomy and rigour • Taste for the field, spirit of analysis and synthesis, sense of organization • Availability for initial training in Geneva before continuing the mission in the canton of Fribourg

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
