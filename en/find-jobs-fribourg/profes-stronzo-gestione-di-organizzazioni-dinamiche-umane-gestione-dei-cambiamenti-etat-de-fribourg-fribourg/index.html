Who are we?

The University of Management (HEG-FR) in Fribourg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO). Accredited by AACSB, it offers bilingual and trilingual training at the bachelor and master levels.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Initiate, acquire and pilot applied projects with external partners, including third-party funding
  • Develop applied research, service delivery and teaching activities within its area of competence
  • Strengthening the link between teaching, applied research and field needs in bachelor, master and continuing training
  • Contribute to the scientific quality, visibility and impact of the Institute's activities
  • Designing and delivering practice-oriented teaching in bachelor's, master's and continuing education programmes

Profile desired

  • Doctorate in a relevant field, including organization management, work psychology or change management
  • Experience in acquiring and managing third-party funds
  • Experience in applied research, project conduct and collaboration with external partners and higher education
  • Expertise in one or more areas related to human dynamics at work and to the development of organizations, such as organizational behaviour, innovation, human resources, occupational health, transformation or entrepreneurship
  • Good knowledge of the issues related to entrepreneurship, innovation and the development of organisations, in various contexts: companies, public, social or umbrella organisations
  • Ability to work interdisciplinaryly and contribute to the collective activities of the Institute
  • Mastery of the French or German language, with good knowledge of the other language and English

You stand out for your field orientation and impact, your initiative, your sense of collaboration, your integrity and openness
