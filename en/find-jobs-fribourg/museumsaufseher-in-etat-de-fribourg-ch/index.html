Appointment authority / department / institution

The Museum of Art and History (MAHF) consists of three main areas: the Hôtel Ratzé, the former slaughterhouse and the temporary exhibition building. It also has a garden that serves as a frame for monumental sculptures.

The Espace Jean Tinguely – Niki de Saint Phalle (Espace JTNSP) is a unique place dedicated to the creative work of two important artists of the 20th century. The century is dedicated.

Together, the two institutions present works that bear witness to the cultural history of the Canton of Freiburg. With the permanent presentation of the collection and the temporary exhibitions, they offer insights into the past and present and at the same time draw attention to the work of the Freiburg artists. In addition, numerous public events and activities take place throughout the year.
