Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Résidence Beaulieu in Murten, we are looking for a mindful and team-oriented person as soon as possible. Professional Health EFZ 40-80 % Your tasks

  • Professional, individual care and care according to established care guidelines
  • Respectful and loving accompaniment of the inhabitants, focusing on their dignity and self-determination
  • Participation in reports, team meetings and training
  • Ensure full, professional and differentiated care
  • Care documentation in the Care Coach
  • Active participation in the RAI survey
  • Assuming daily or shift responsibility

You bring that with you

  • Training as a specialist in health and professional experience, beneficial in long-term care
  • You are committed and motivated in the nursing profession and are happy to pass on your knowledge to colleagues and learners
  • You are independent, competent and active thinking is important to you
  • An empathetic accompaniment of the residents is just as obvious to you as an effective work organization and an appreciative cooperation in the team.
  • Ideally, you already have knowledge of RAI/RUG and CareCoach
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German

We offer you

  • Extensive further education offer of the Senevita Academy can be visited free of charge
  • Unique culture and various generous employee events
  • Discounted food and lunch in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various perks (fuels, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • After the trial period, Senevita gives you the half-tax subscription
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
