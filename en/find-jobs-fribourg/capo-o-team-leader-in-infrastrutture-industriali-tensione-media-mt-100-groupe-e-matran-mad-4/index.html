Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations.

Mission

As a team leader, you carry out and have carried out the work related to medium voltage (MT) installations within the industrial infrastructure. You manage your team operationally to ensure a secure, qualitative and consistent delivery. Your responsibilities:

  • Actively participate in MT work: installation of MT cells, installation and connection of MT lines, installation of MT/BT transformers and associated equipment.
  • Supervise, motivate and distribute tasks within your team while ensuring an efficient workflow.
  • Adapt the interventions to the constraints of the field and check the availability, condition and proper use of the specialized MT equipment.
  • Control the conformity of electrical installations, optimize the use of materials and maintain a clean and secure working environment.
  • Strictly enforce safety rules, verify PPE, identify risks and immediately stop work in case of danger.
  • Carry out the survey, document key information and trace needs and gaps to the construction manager.
  • Train, accompany and evaluate employees, while ensuring the quality of the work and the timeliness.

Profile

  • Network electrician CFC, electrician installer or equivalent recognized training.
  • Confirmed practical experience (5 years) in medium voltage installations (cells, transformers, lines, wiring).
  • Proven ability to frame a team, transmit technical instructions and maintain cohesion.
  • Strong sense of security, rigour, organization and responsiveness to unforeseen situations.
  • Dynamic, careful, responsible, solutions oriented person.
  • Driver's license.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
