Full description

Are you looking forward to starting your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our international program to launch your career with us and benefit from our hands-on approach. We are looking for candidates available for 12 months internship as of February 2027. Your Responsibilities As a Young Talent in Test Infrastructure Development within the Control Systems Testing team, you will actively contribute to the design, development, and continuous improvement of test systems for embedded electronic applications.

  • You develop HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) infrastructure according to project and product specifications for the validation of electronic control units (ECU) used in diesel and hydrogen combustion engines.
  • You design system concepts and select appropriate components.
  • You perform electronic routing and spinning diagram design.
  • You research and evaluate requests for system implementation.
  • You develop control software using tools such as MATLAB Simulink.
  • You carry out unit and integration tests to ensure system reliability and performance.
  • You prepare technical documentation and contribute to knowledge transfer.
  • You identify and propose potential improvements for future HIL infrastructures.
  • You troubleshoot spinning and system issues in existing test benches. Your Profile
  • You are currently a student or a recent graduate with a Bachelor
  • You have initial experience or a strong interest in embedded electronic system development.
  • You possess solid knowledge in general electricity, wire, and basic electronic schematics.
  • You are fluent in French and English
  • German is an asset.
  • You are proficient in software development tools such as MATLAB Simulink.
  • You are familiar with instrumentation, technical measurement, and electronic components (sensors, actuators, etc.).
  • You are comfortable using MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and have basic knowledge of electrical design software.
  • You work perfectly and independently, while demonstrating excellent team spirit in a multidisciplinary environment. Have we raised your interest? If so, we would be happy to receive your application online. If you have any questions, please contact Lisa SCHNEIDER. One Passion. Many Opportunities. *A common passion. So many opportunities.
