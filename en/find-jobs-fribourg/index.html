Cost of living vs Ticino

The median gross salary in Fribourg is about 22% higher than in Ticino (BFS LSE 2024 regional wage-level data). The median LAMal health-insurance premium is also lower: CHF 367/month vs CHF 425/month in Ticino (14% lower, BAG/UFSP data) — a real extra cost to factor into your net income.

G permit and border zone

The G cross-border permit requires residency in a border municipality of a country neighbouring Switzerland and a daily return home. Fribourg does not border Italy: Italian residents considering a role here face a much longer commute than in Ticino and in practice opt for a B residence permit or relocation to Switzerland, not cross-border-commuter status.
