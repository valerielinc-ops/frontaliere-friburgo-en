Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Mission

  • Plan, organize, coordinate and supervise heating projects, mainly through energy clean-ups and the replacement of existing plants with heat pumps.
  • Ensure the preparation and proper execution of construction sites within the deadlines, quality, costs and safety requirements.
  • Coordinate human, material and various stakeholders (technical, technical, supplier, subcontractor and assembly teams) to ensure the smooth running of projects.
  • Ensure the administrative management and financial monitoring of construction sites (reports, accounts, governance notes, cost control, technical documentation, etc.).
  • Be the contact point for clients, architects, managers, contractors and partners throughout the implementation phase.
  • Provide technical expertise in the fields of heat pumps, heat distribution systems and modern energy solutions, offering solutions adapted to the constraints of renovations.
  • Participate in the training, supervision and development of the skills of employees and apprentices.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of working methods and ensure compliance with standards and health and safety rules.

Profile

  • SWC installer in heating or equivalent training, advanced training (federal project leader patent, foreman or equivalent training) is an asset.
  • Experience in the management and operation of heating plants, ideally in the field of heat pumps and energy sanitation.
  • Strong technical knowledge of modern heating systems, especially heat pumps and their integration into existing buildings.
  • Assistance in financial monitoring of construction sites and resource management.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple projects simultaneously and effectively manage priorities.
  • Natural leadership, sense of responsibility and ability to unite teams around common goals.
  • Customer orientation, excellent relational skills and taste for sustainable technical solutions.
  • Sensitivity to energy transition issues and strong interest in new heating technologies.
  • Mastery of common computer tools.
  • Driver's licence required.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
