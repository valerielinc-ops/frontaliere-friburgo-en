Full description

Micarna SA, a company of the Migros group specializing in the processing of meat, poultry, eggs and seafood products, is looking for a team leader in internal transport. A key role to coordinate operations, mentor the team and ensure an effective logistics flow on a daily basis.

Mansioni

  • Manage a team of 7 to 10 people divided into different schedules
  • Ensure coordination of activities within the internal transport sector
  • Develop and update employee work schedules and train new entrants in the sector.
  • Working on continuous improvement within the department
  • Provide support in administrative tasks related to personnel management

Required

  • 1-2 years: experience in a similar position
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Logistician CFC or equivalent training
  • You have a first successful experience in team driving
  • Sociable, flexible and organized, you are also a conscientious and autonomous person.
  • You master the standard IT tools (MS Office, SAP)
  • You are available for 2x8 team work and Saturdays with turns
  • You have a good physical constitution (temperature of 3 to 15 degrees)
  • French (Good Mastery)
  • German (preferably)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Professional provision: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Restaurant: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points Cumulus: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: 70% reimbursement of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
