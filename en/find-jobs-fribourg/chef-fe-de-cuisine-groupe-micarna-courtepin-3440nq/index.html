Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna has set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but we can only consider online applications. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Diverse, creative and economical planning of the supply according to the quality standards defined. Creation and development of our kitchen recipes
  • Personal and organizational conduct and development of the kitchen team (6-7 employees)
  • Conduct, supervision and support of all workplaces at different stages of production and manufacturing
  • Guarantee and respect of hygiene concepts and food according to legal requirements / HACCP standards
  • Responsibility for budget and supply
  • Guarantee of a balanced and diversified diet and good quality of our products
  • Monitoring of self-monitoring procedures and compliance with the relevant official guidelines. Strict compliance with safety standards
  • Work closely with service and event staff
  • Working hours Monday to Friday (tournus 5h-16h)

Requirements

  • 5-10 years: Experience as a Chef*fe
  • Federal patent: Chef*fe de cuisine CFC with a federal patent
  • Successful experience in leadership and team development
  • Person passionate about the profession, creative and offering innovative and healthy cuisine
  • Rigorous maintenance of HACCP standards, high individual responsibility, proven professional skills
  • Good sense of communication, open and charismatic personality able to work in a structured and daring way taking initiatives
  • High priority and ability to keep an overview in demanding situations
  • At ease with the Suite Office, knowledge of an ERP (ideally SAP) and affinity with artificial intelligence tools
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (preferably)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paid paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational planning: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Restaurant: Balanced lunch/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Points: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Club School: Refund of 70% of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
  • Training and continuing training: Attractive offer of training and further training
  • Flexible workplace/telework: Up to 2 days of telework possible

Contatto

Candidature and contact Sabrina Zimmermann

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the specialized department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personality test/performance: Under certain conditions, we take an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for the continuation of the selection process. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Final maintenance prior to contract establishment: Before establishing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify the remaining open issues of both parties and to resolve the contractual details. When all points are clarified, a written offer is submitted. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Offer and establishment of the contract: The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by post. Return time: up to 3 weeks

Grado di occupazione: 100%
