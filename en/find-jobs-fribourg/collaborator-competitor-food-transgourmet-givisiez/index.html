Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive own brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service business in Switzerland and has around 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • Proactive sale and advice
  • Compliance with prescribed internal and legal QA and workplace safety directives in the company
  • Ensure clean workplace
  • Recognize, understand and communicate client needs
  • Ensure the freshness and presentation of the goods
  • Ensure labelling complies with labelling concept
  • Ensure that the goods are 100% available (shelving, 2nd point of sale,
  • promotional location)
  • Inventory according to specifications
  • Take note of the claims and transmit them accordingly
  • Scan goods at check-out

Qualifications and qualifications

  • Completed training in the food sector (cooker, baker, retail, butcher)
  • Reliable, efficient and correct work
  • Careful presentation and pleasure of customer contact
  • Enthusiasm for new challenges
  • Physical resistance
  • Pleasure working as a team
  • Impeccable knowledge of French
  • Training in forklift and/or drawbar handling (SUVA standard) would be an asset.
