CONTEXT

Richemont International Distribution recruits for its distribution centre in Villars-sur-Glâne, a CFC Automatic Apprenti for the beginning of August 2027.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

As an apprentice, you design, install, commission and maintain electromechanical controls and automated systems (such as robots, production equipment, building engineering installations, etc.) by mobilizing your knowledge of electricity, electronics and pneumatics.

You plan and perform the preventive maintenance of the facilities, while ensuring that the associated documentation is updated. Furthermore, you use the computer to develop the programs of the controllers that drive these systems.

This learning path is for anyone who is passionate about machine work, has good abstraction skills and appreciates both workshop work and interactions with clients.

HOW ARE YOU TO USE US?

You have proven skills in mathematics, technical design and physics, and show a keen interest in new technologies, electrical engineering and technical contexts. With an excellent understanding of abstract concepts and a high capacity for visualization in space, your manual skill allows you to perform precise and neat work. Enjoying teamwork, you are also recognized for your autonomy.
