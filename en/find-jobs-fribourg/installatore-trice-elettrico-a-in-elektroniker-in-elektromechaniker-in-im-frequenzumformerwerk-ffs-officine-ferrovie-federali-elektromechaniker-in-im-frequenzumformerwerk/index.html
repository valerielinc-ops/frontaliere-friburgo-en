SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. In this function, you can expect an exciting and varied task area with large machines, a lot of power and high voltage. You work in a collegial team together with 8 employees. Together, you ensure a low-interference operation of our frequency converter plant and the associated substations in the region. With your commitment, you make a significant contribution to the security of rail supply in this region and to a sustainable SBB. More information can be found here . You can move that.

  • In this exciting position, you will take over maintenance work in high- and low-voltage systems at the frequency converters Wimmis and Kerzers plants and the associated substations.
  • You carry out inspections, maintenance and repairs, organize and plan them with internal and external employees and suppliers.
  • Disturbances are located, analyzed and fixed by you. In addition, you support project managers in conversions and new buildings.
  • Safety is our top priority. Actively help with compliance and implementation of security requirements – both for your own team and for third parties.

You bring that with you.

  • You also keep the necessary peace in hectic phases and keep the overview. You enjoy working in a small team and are independent and reliable.
  • Knowledge of the operation and maintenance of energy production and/or distribution plants is desired. You are looking forward to completing plant-specific training courses.
  • We require a completed vocational apprenticeship in the electrical sector, preferably as an electrical installer:in, electrical installer:in, electronics technician:in or in a related profession with corresponding experience.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (at least C1), knowledge of a second national language is an advantage. For the piquettes you need the driver's license of category B (ideally also BE).
