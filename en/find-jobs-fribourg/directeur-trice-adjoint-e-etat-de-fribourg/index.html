Full description

Who are we? The Cantonal and University Library of Fribourg (BCU) collects and makes available documentary resources on all types of materials for the cantonal population and the university community. It is part of the national network of Swisscovery libraries. A project to expand and restructure the historic building in the city centre is also under way; the new BCU will open by 2026. The BCU is a meeting place for members of the university community and for a large cantonal public. Why join us? We offer a dynamic environment, in the context of creating a new library in the city centre that provides for the development of modern services. BCU is a founding member of SLSP (national network) and is involved in the development of Swisscovery services. The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link . Your missions In close cooperation with the Director, you are involved in the proper functioning of the Cantonal and University Library.

  • By supporting the Director, you contribute to the overall strategic, financial and human resources direction of the BCU
  • You represent the BCU in various committees and events
  • You ensure the strategic conduct of the institution's library mission, and supervise four sector chiefs
  • You ensure the conduct of coordination with the University of Fribourg
  • You supervise vocational training and lead the engagement of apprentices and trainees. Profile desired
  • You have a full university education and a Master's degree in Information Sciences, or a training deemed equivalent
  • You justify a wide experience in the field of library science
  • You have great managerial and project management experience
  • You stand out for your initiative and analytical spirit, as well as your understanding and sense of communication
  • Your sense of organization allows you to provide structured and efficient work, and contributes to your agility
