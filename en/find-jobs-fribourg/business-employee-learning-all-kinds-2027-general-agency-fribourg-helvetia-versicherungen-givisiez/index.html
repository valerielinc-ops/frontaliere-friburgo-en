Internships: apprenticeship places, internships, trainee Workplace: Experience level: FlexOffice students: on site With us, within #TeamHelvetia, you don't need to know everything from the start. We trust you and accompany you throughout your journey. Ready to start with us? So send us your application with your CV and your school bulletins for the last two years. A cover letter? No need!

What you bring

  • You gradually learn how insurance works and participate in different tasks, for example in the processing of offers, claims or customer requests.
  • You are in contact with our clients as well as your colleagues, by phone, by e-mail or in person.
  • In addition to your daily activities, you participate in exciting projects and bring your own ideas.
  • You are actively involved in your development and discover the tasks and themes you particularly like.

Your profile

  • You are in the final year of compulsory education (medium or higher level) or already frequent a post-compulsory school and have good academic results.
  • You're interested in the insurance world and you want to understand how it works.
  • You like to learn new things, you are curious and you are willing to take initiatives.
  • You like contact with others and like to work as a team.
  • You are actively thinking, asking questions and bringing your own ideas.

Show us what you can do: download your school bulletins directly and complete your application. Here you will find all the information about your apprenticeship at Helvetia, from the discovery day to your entry into professional life.
