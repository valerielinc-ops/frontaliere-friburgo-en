Welcome to Medbase Fribourg

Our modern medical centre with its own laboratory and radiology facilities is located at the Freiburg train station. The team of medical specialists in general internal medicine, gynaecology, obstetrics and paediatrics and medical assistants works in a family spirit and in close interprofessional collaboration, always oriented towards quality. Medbase Fribourg favours a good balance between professional and private life. For doctors, collaboration is possible under employee or independent status, to be agreed upon during the interview.

Your tasks

  • You will provide part of the outpatient emergency consultations in collaboration with the physician responsible for the permanence.
  • You will evolve in a caring and human-sized environment.
  • You will participate actively in internal and external training.
  • Hours: Monday to Friday from 8am to 1 8am.
  • No night guards, no weekends and public holidays.
  • Type of contract, duration: CSD from 6 months to 1 year.

Your profile

  • You are in possession of the federal diploma of doctor or equivalent recognized with MEBEKO.
  • With good communication skills with patients and integration into teamwork, you are passionate, committed and dynamic.
  • This position is suitable for both beginner and advanced physicians.
