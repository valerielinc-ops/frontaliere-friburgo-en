Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG brings together the canton’s outpatient, day clinic, mobile and inpatient public psychiatric services and ensures high-quality psychosocial care for the population of the bilingual canton of Freiburg.

The FNPG is a public-law institution that has six locations in the canton of Freiburg and employs over 800 people in around 40 different professions. As a recognised training and further education institution, it offers more than one hundred students and trainees apprenticeships and internships in various educational programmes every year.

Their tasks

In this important support function, you work closely with the Directorate-General and support them in organizational, administrative and editorial tasks. Their activities include in particular:

  • Thanks to your excellent editorial skills and strong ability to summarize content concisely, you compose various documents, in particular the minutes of the Board of Directors and the Board of Directors, as well as various reports and other documents.
  • They coordinate various meetings and take on the resulting tasks such as correspondence, information sharing and dossier tracking.
  • They perform various administrative tasks at institutional level, in particular in relation to the coordination of education and training in coordination with the management secretariats of the departments concerned
  • They organise various events on behalf of the Directorate-General
  • If necessary, you undertake special orders in connection with the administration of the FNPG

Desired profile

  • You have a higher commercial education and several years of sound professional experience in a comparable function
  • They are interested in the mission and values of the FNPG and understand how to act flexibly and sovereignly in a demanding environment.
  • You are a reliable and trustworthy personality, identify with the objectives of the management and can be entrusted with sensitive dossiers
  • They're well-versed in logging.
  • You master the common IT applications securely
  • You have an excellent verbal and written ability to express yourself in French and a knowledge of German at level B2

We look forward to your application with letter of motivation, CV as well as diplomas and employment certificates.
