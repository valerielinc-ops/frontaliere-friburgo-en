Your tasks

You complete two years of higher vocational training as an employee with the aim of becoming a police officer with the Friborg Cantonal Police. The main tasks include:

  • Prevent disruptions to public safety and order and intervene if necessary;
  • Determine crimes, secure evidence and investigate the perpetrators in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure;
  • carry out administrative and judicial decisions;
  • respond to emergency calls and alarms, carry out rescue operations and provide first aid in the event of serious danger or accidents;
  • Prevent crimes.

Desired profile

The formal employment conditions can be found on the cantonal police recruitment website. In addition, you have the following skills:

  • good physical condition and high resistance to stress;
  • Willingness to work irregular working hours as well as evening, night and weekend work;
  • confident demeanor and strong social and communication skills for working in a team and dealing with conflicts;
  • good oral and written expression skills;
  • strong sense of values ​​and responsibility;
  • good general education;
  • Knowledge of the second official language is an advantage.

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The cantonal police are subordinate to the security, justice and sports directorate. Their mission is generally to ensure public safety, the maintenance of order and compliance with the law.

It serves the population and the authorities. To this end, it carries out a large number of diverse and demanding tasks 24 hours a day and 365 days a year: emergency operations, detection of crimes, crime prevention, investigations, etc. The increasingly important role of the police in solving social problems requires tact and specialist knowledge from officers.

The cantonal police are also responsible for road safety. It offers organizers of public events assistance in ensuring security. It supports the cantonal and municipal authorities in serious events.

The cantonal police consist of two main corps, the gendarmerie and the criminal police, and three divisions: command, logistics and IT, HR and training.
