The Position Join our Global Clinical Operations team as a Global Clinical Operations Intern and play a vital role in transforming clinical trial delivery to improve the lives of patients and caregivers . In this role, you will collaborate across a dynamic, low-hierarchy environment that empowers individuals and prioritizes continuous personal growth . You will work directly with global cross-functional teams to drive high-impact initiatives in pharmaceutical product development .

The Opportunity

  • Provide strategic and operational support to deliver global clinical studies across multiple therapeutic areas .
  • Engage with global cross-functional stakeholders to facilitate smooth portfolio execution and trial delivery .
  • Prioritize patient, investigator, and site needs by building peer-to-peer relationships to foster efficient, patient-centric operations .
  • Implement simplified, high-impact processes to advance personalized healthcare solutions .
  • Drive cross-functional communication and identify new opportunities for innovation in a global matrixed environment .
  • Collaborate with internal partners and external bodies while maintaining compliance with clinical and safety guidelines using a risk-based approach .

Who You Are

  • You are currently pursuing a Master’s program in Biomedical Discovery and Commercialization, Biotechnology or closely related field.
  • You thrive in collaborative, complex, and matrixed global environments where authority is shared .
  • You possess strong organizational and problem-solving skills to manage clinical operations projects effectively .
  • You are fluent in both written and spoken English .
  • You demonstrate strong interpersonal skills and embody core values of integrity, courage, and passion .

Additional Information

  • Term & Location: 12-month full-time internship starting January 2027 in Mississauga, ON (Hybrid model: minimum 3 days in office)
  • Application: Please submit applications directly via our Workday Portal .
