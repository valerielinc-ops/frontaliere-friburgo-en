For Raiffeisenbank Sense-Oberland, a successful and locally oriented bank with branches in Giffers, Jaun, Plaffeien, Plasselb, Rechthalten, St. Ursen and St. Silvester, we are looking for a personality as a corporate customer advisor.

Tasks

  • Experience in advising corporate customers or motivation to develop further in corporate banking

  • Commitment, ability to work in a team and a structured way of working
  • Regional connection to the business community is an advantage
