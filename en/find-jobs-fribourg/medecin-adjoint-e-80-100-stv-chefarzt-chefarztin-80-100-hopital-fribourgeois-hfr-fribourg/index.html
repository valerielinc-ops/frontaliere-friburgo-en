To complement its team, the Medical Directorate is looking for a Deputy Medical Officer (in relation to the service of orthopaedics and traumatology) 80-100% Your mission: You will have a key role in developing an innovative clinical activity, at the interface of geriatrics, perioperative internal medicine and orthopaedic surgery. You are engaged in clinical, academic and organizational activity based on three main axes. 1. Development of the orthogeriatric unit:

  • To ensure medical responsibility and contribute to the development of the orthogeriatric unit according to a model of co-management with the orthopaedic teams.
  • To carry out complete geriatric assessments and to participate in the development of individualized care plans.
  • To participate in multidisciplinary visits and weekly symposia with medical, nursing and rehabilitation teams.
  • To develop treatment pathways, protocols and quality indicators specific to orthogeriatry. 2. Perioperative internal medicine
  • Ensure internistic management of patients hospitalized or operated in orthopaedic surgery.
  • Optimize preoperative evaluation, co-morbidity management and postoperative follow-up.
  • Collaborate with orthopaedic teams in the development of integrated perioperative medicine. 3. Liaison geriatrics
  • Provide inpatient geriatric liaison consultations
  • Issue diagnostic and therapeutic recommendations in collaboration with medical and health teams. Cross-cutting areas: You will also participate in:
  • Supervision and training of clinical chiefs, assistant doctors and doctors in training;
  • Pre-graded teaching of students in the University of Fribourg's Master's degree in Medicine;
  • Post-graded teaching and further training.
  • Institutional projects, clinical research and quality improvement in the fields of orthogeriatry, perioperative medicine and geriatric liaison. Your profile:
