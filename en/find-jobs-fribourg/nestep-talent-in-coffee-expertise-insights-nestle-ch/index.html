Be a force for good and take your NeStep with Nespresso

NeStep is a unique 12-month immersive experience at Nespresso in Switzerland for recent graduates who want to learn quickly, grow with purpose and make an impact. From day one, you will spend at least half of your time owning an impactful project, combined with structured learning and continuous mentoring to deliver meaningful results.

As a NeStep Talent in Coffee Expertise Insights at Nespresso, your mission will be to help the Coffee Expertise team become more data-driven and make its impact measurable. You will unlock insights from existing digital tools, dashboards and user feedback, turning them into practical recommendations and targeted actions for Markets and departments.

Working at Nespresso, you will collaborate with Coffee Expertise colleagues, digital and data specialists, Markets, cross-functional partners and the global Coffee Ambassador community. This is an opportunity to learn by doing, grow with support and contribute to how coffee knowledge, capabilities and experiences are shared across the organisation.

How you’ll make an impact as a NeStep Talent in Coffee Expertise Insights

  • Analyse data and user feedback from Coffee Expertise dashboards and digital platforms and turn your findings into actionable recommendations.
  • Support the implementation and continuous improvement of digital assets, from identifying business needs to testing, onboarding and user feedback.
  • Help measure the impact of Coffee Expertise initiatives by tracking adoption, engagement, learning and business contribution against agreed indicators.
  • Support the virtual activation of the global Coffee Ambassador community through training content, polls and surveys, translating feedback into targeted actions.
  • Explore relevant Nestlé-approved AI and GROW use cases while supporting project activities and Coffee Expertise initiatives such as masterclasses, tastings and internal contests

What will make you successful

  • Being a recent graduate with a bachelor or master’s degree in data Analytics, Data Science, Business Analytics, Computer Science or related fields
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills and confidence working with dashboards, spreadsheets and digital platforms, turning data into clear recommendations and practical actions. Experience with tools such as Excel, Forms or Power BI, or the ability to learn comparable tools, would be an advantage
  • The ability to understand a business need and translate it into a practical digital solution, asset, toolkit or workflow, with curiosity about responsible, value-adding applications of approved AI tools
  • Clear communication, collaboration, proactivity, learning agility and attention to detail, with the confidence to work across teams, functions and cultures and follow through on agreed actions
  • Fluency English is required
  • French and other languages are a plus
  • You demonstrate courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

Eligibility criteria

  • You recently completed a bachelor’s or master’s degree at a Swiss higher education institution, with the degree completed within the last 12 months.
  • You are a Swiss national, an EU/EFTA national or a C permit holder.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Fribourg?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Fribourg's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Fribourg's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
