100%, Fribourg

Shape your future with us!

Are you a professional insurance professional or from another horizon? Start your career in one of Axa's agencies, the insurance leader. We are the trusted partner of our clients, in all situations of life. You too?

Have you ever been able to apply your skills in service and business support? You're a customer-oriented person and you have a strong commitment to your work? You have the opportunity at home to develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities!

Our general agency Prévoyance & Patrimoine de Fribourg wants to strengthen its internal service team. If you recognize yourself in this description and your interest in joining us is real, we would be delighted to receive your file!

What's waiting for you

  • Advice and assistance to customers by e-mail, telephone and reception upon arrival at the agency
  • Assisting and supporting advisors in planning appointments with their clients
  • Close and permanent contact with the Agency's planning advisory teams
  • Frequent exchanges with the various departments of our Directorate AXA Vie SA and Health, especially in the management of client cases (management of the provisioning contracts)

What you bring

  • Business training and first experience in administration, knowledge of the insurance sector and the field of personal welfare and health planning are a real advantage, AFA is an asset (Life & sickness)
  • A strong interest in advice and sales, a strong motivation to train you on our products and internal processes
  • A service oriented personality and the desire to always find the best equitable solution for all
  • Positive and dynamic temperament, reliability, flexibility and ease of contact with others and excellent teamwork
  • An excellent command of French in both oral and written terms, an ease in German is an advantage, any other language is a complementary asset
  • A place to live in the Fribourg region and a good knowledge of the local economic fabric
  • Easiness with common computer systems and learning new digital tools

What we offer you We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to fully develop and exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-tariff subscription/participation in general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities
