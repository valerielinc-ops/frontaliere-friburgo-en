Full description

Reference code: JR130201 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

As one of the core building block of the Cartier Data Office, Connected Planning is a critical, yet rapidly expending part of our Data ecosystem.

The Anaplan model builder has a key role in the development of the solution within the Maison and the maintenance of the models ensuring operational efficiency and business continuity. As a model builder, you will ensure that models used Maison-wide are matching industry standard best practices and delivering the best level of performance and robustness.

How will you make an impact?

You will have the following responsibilities

  • Model building
  • Implement best practices for model development, including version control, documentation and technical optimizations.
  • Proactively keep a high level of technical expertise, and stay up to date with latest developments of the Solution.
  • Ensure robustness and perennity of the models. Ensure data integrity, accuracy and reliability within the models.
  • Maintain comprehensive documentation including design specifications, formulas, and assumptions.
  • Maintenance
  • Investigate and resolve issues related to Anaplan models, including data inconsistencies, formula errors, and system performance. Provide support to end-users.
  • New projects
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders from different department of the Maison.
  • Work with project teams to understand their challenges and requirements. Translate them into scalable and efficient Anaplan models.
  • Supporting new projects from the beginning to a successful launch.
  • Collaborate and federate
  • Facilitate the exchanges of experience and best practices among domains, while evaluating the appropriate help based on their specific needs.
  • Actively contribute to the Anaplan community by sharing knowledge, collaborating on projects and driving innovation.

Your profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Management, or a related field.
  • Proven experience as an Anaplan Model builder and Anaplan Model builder certification are a must. Anaplan Solution Architect certification is a plus.
  • Experience in Supply Chain
  • Experience working with Agile methodologies and management tools (e.g. Jira).
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Understanding of supply chain processes is a plus.
  • English and French.

How do we keep you smiling?

You will play an active role into our Transformation journey, working on solutions that individually generate immediate value, but that collectively enrich our data platform, opening the door to further value creation.
