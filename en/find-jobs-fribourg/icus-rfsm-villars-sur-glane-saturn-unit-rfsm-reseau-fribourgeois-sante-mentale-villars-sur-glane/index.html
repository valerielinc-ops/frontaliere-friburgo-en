Full description

Post "ICUS, RFSM Villars-sur-Glâne - Saturn Unit" at RFSM - Fribourg mental health network, Villars-sur-Glâne (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For the General Adult and Advanced German/Bilingual Psychiatry Unit Saturn:
