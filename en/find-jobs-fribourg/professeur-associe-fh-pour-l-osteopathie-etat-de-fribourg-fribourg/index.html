The Hochschule für Gesundheit Freiburg offers bilingual degree programmes in nursing (level Bachelor) and osteopathy (level Bachelor and Master). It develops research and training activities and offers its partners a whole range of services.

Their tasks

  • Acquisition of funding and management/leadership of important research projects anchored in osteopathy
  • Regular publication of the results of the funded projects in scientific journals with peer-review procedures and in specialist publications
  • Active participation in national and international congresses through the presentation and exploitation of research results
  • Establishment and consolidation of scientific, clinical and interprofessional cooperation networks in the field of osteopathy
  • Promotion of the development of young academics in osteopathy within the framework of research and innovation activities (e.g. by directing or co-supervising doctoral theses)

Desired profile

  • Doctorate (PhD) in the field of osteopathy or in a related health discipline
  • Broad experience in your own area of expertise
  • Profession as an osteopath with proven clinical and practical experience
  • Demonstrated skills in applied research, in particular in project management and the acquisition of competitive third-party funding
  • Proven experience with publications in scientific journals with peer-review procedures and in specialist publications
  • Mastering the German or French language with good knowledge of the other language as well as English
