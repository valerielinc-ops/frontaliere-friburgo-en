Summary of the post

At the post of Logistics Operator (M/F/D), you will be responsible for the operation of the facilities of the production logistics area (package and pallet distribution) in 4*8 team time.

A day in the life of a Logistics Operator (M/F/D)

  • Conducting the operation of logistics area facilities
  • Perform and document quality controls
  • Managing waste
  • Approvision packing lines
  • Ensure proper application of 5S
  • Bring support to production lines

Your assets for success

  • Carist permit
  • Experience in the food or pharmaceutical industry
