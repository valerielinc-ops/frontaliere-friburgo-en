Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Cantonal Social Office (KSA), which is responsible in particular for the supervision, coordination and harmonization of the social assistance system (SHG) in the canton of Friborg, is looking for an inspector for the regional social services in this area as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment. The workload is 60-70%. The tasks of this position include the implementation and monitoring of the Social Welfare Act (SHG). Only online applications via the State of Freiburg website jobs.fr.ch will be considered.

Become part of our team!

The state employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Your tasks

  • Introduction and support of the supervisory tasks relating to the organization and functioning of the regional social services that are responsible for the implementation of the Social Assistance Act (SHG).
  • Defining the monitoring of the social assistance system, statistical indicators and control dashboards and disseminating and analyzing the results
  • Development of quality criteria for the services and review of their application
  • Supporting the regional social services and the social commissions in adapting processes, implementing general measures to inform and further train specialists and in organizing procedures
  • Participation in the development of the social assistance system

Desired profile

  • Master's degree in social work or equivalent training with several years of professional experience
  • Sound knowledge of organization
  • Excellent knowledge in the area of ​​social security and the organization of the cantonal social system
  • Enjoy dealing with administration, good communication skills and interest in social issues
  • Dynamic and versatile personality, precise way of working and a flair for numbers, organizational and coordination skills, ability to work in a team
  • Good knowledge of common computer applications
  • Fluency in German or French with very good knowledge of the other language
