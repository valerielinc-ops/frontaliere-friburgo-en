Role overview

Who are we?

The State Chancellery CHA offers a stimulating working environment at the heart of the cantonal government's activities and transversal to the cantonal administration.

It serves as the secretariat of the Council of State, organizes its events and is responsible for informing the public, coordinating external relations and relations with the Grand Council.
