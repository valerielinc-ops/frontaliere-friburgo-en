Who are we?

The SPC Bridges and Roadways Service ensures the sustainable development of roads by providing users with a safe and adapted road infrastructure, managing public road areas and carrying out administrative tasks to achieve these objectives.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages reconciliation between work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees.

Your missions

Maintenance work on the cantonal roads

  • Maintenance of operations (green areas maintenance, cleaning, picket service)
  • Winter service (protection, network monitoring and control, day care)
  • Local repair work
  • Maintenance and repair of equipment

Profile

  • CFCs in maintenance, operation, green areas, construction and mechanics
  • Ability to carry out manual work and minor road maintenance and repair
  • Teamwork experience
  • Be able to get to the workplace in 25 minutes during the picket service
  • Driving licence: min Cat. B, BE and C1 would be an advantage
  • Mastery of the French and/or German language with knowledge of the other language
