Who are we?

Attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport, the cantonal police in Freiburg are responsible for ensuring public security, law enforcement and law enforcement.

It is at the service of the population and the authorities. This results in 24 hours a day and 365 days a year in the performance of a large number of varied and demanding tasks: emergency response, crime reports, crime prevention, investigations, etc. The increasingly important role of the police in managing social problems requires tact and know-how.

It is also responsible for ensuring road traffic safety. It provides security support to organizers of public demonstrations and supports cantonal and communal authorities in serious events.

The cantonal police Fribourg consists of two main corps, the gendarmerie and the security police and three divisions: command, logistics and information technology, human resources and training.
