Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

Grangeneuve is a multidisciplinary centre of excellence that is passionate about vocational training, expertise, advice and services for the professions of nature, milk, food and hotel industry. More than 280 employees work in a bilingual environment that promotes excellence, respect, collaboration and innovation as well as the appreciation and transmission of cultural heritage.

Become part of our team!

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Leadership and management of the Education Department with all basic and higher vocational training programmes (80 employees)
  • Ensuring strategic development of the department
  • Management of educational and didactic activities
  • Ensuring and further developing a high quality of teaching
  • Collaboration in various cantonal and federal projects
  • Monitoring and analysis of technological, political and legal developments in basic and higher vocational training
  • Representation of Grangeneuve with external partners

Desired profile

  • Higher vocational education and training
  • Multiannual professional experience in vocational training
  • CAS in Administration and Management of Educational Institutions (FORDIF) or Readiness to Complete This Continuing Education
  • Interest in agricultural, natural, food and domestic professions
  • Proven experience in leading teams
  • Competences in project management and a strong sense of innovation
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the partner language (B2-C1)
