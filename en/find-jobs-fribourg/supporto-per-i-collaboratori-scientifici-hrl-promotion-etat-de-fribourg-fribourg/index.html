Full description

Why join us?

In order to strengthen the quality of research projects submitted to the Federal Human Research Act (HRA) and to provide its assistance and expertise in the development, drafting and submission of research protocols, the HRA and UNIFR are seeking a Scientific Collaborator or Collaborator for the Promotion and Support of Human Research (submitted to the HRA).

Your missions

  • Verify that researchers adhere to the recommendations of good practice when drafting applications for authorisations for research projects and clinical trials submitted to ethics boards, from an ethical, epidemiological and scientific point of view
  • Follow-up of applications to ethics and swissmedic commissions
  • Support researchers in their exchanges with ethics and swissmedic commissions until authorization is obtained
  • Participate in case management and monitoring
  • Participate in the development of guidelines and document templates for institutions and researchers
  • Managing general consent within the HFR and adapting it as legal requirements evolve
  • Manage clinicaltrials.gov.

Profile desired

  • Holder of a university degree (min. Master's degree or equivalent) in the fields of medicine, pharmacy, life sciences or psychology. A PhD is an asset
  • To benefit from advanced GCP training and several years of professional experience in a position where you have been directly involved in the development of research and submission protocols to ethics committees (in particular those of the cantons of Vaud and Bern) within the framework of the LRH and compliance with the relevant requirements
  • Proven ability to work as a team and a strong self-help spirit
  • The ability to balance the needs of researchers with the regulatory framework
  • The ease of communication with the various research stakeholders (researcher-s, ethics commissions, legal services, IT services)
  • Self-reliance, capacity to manage priorities and initiative
  • Excellent mastery of common office tools
  • Flexibility in the organization of working time
  • You are fluent in the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language and you are fluent in the English and oral language.
