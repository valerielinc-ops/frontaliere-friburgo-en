Who are we?

The Museum of Art and History Fribourg (MAHF) is composed of three parts. The Ratzé Hotel, the former slaughterhouse, the temporary exhibition building and a garden for monumental sculptures. The Jean Tinguely Space - Niki de Saint Phalle (Espace JTNSP) is a magical place that pays tribute to the creativity of two great artists of the twentieth century. The works gathered in both institutions document the canton's cultural history. With the permanent presentation of the collection and temporary exhibitions, they offer reading keys to the past and the present, while raising awareness of the creation of Fribourgeois artists. Many public events take place throughout the year.
