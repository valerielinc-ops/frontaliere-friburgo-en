Who are we?

The Commercial Police Service (SPoCo) issues authorizations, monitors and enforces legislation in various economic sectors, including public institutions, the sale of alcohol, taxis, gambling and mobile commerce. Through its work, it helps to ensure a reliable legal framework for the professionals and the population of the canton.

Why join us?

Join a team of people and benefit from a variety of activities, combining human contacts, administrative management and enforcement. We attach particular importance to reconciling work and private life and propose flexible working conditions.

To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Inform, advise and inform public officials, operators and municipalities by telephone, in writing and at the counter about procedures under the Commercial Police Service
  • Ensure administrative processing of records of public institutions, mainly for the Singine and Lake districts
  • Monitor compliance of applications, record records and follow up until authorisations are issued
  • Apply legal and regulatory bases for public institutions and other service areas
  • Collaborate with the municipal authorities, cantonal services and the various partners involved in the procedures
  • Perform various administrative tasks related to the activities of the service and contribute to its proper functioning

Profile desired

  • SWCs or training deemed equivalent
  • Interest in administrative matters and the application of legal bases
  • Work experience of several years
  • Human contact and customer service
  • Excellent organizational capacity, rigour and accountability
  • Proven ability to work independently while fostering collaboration within a team
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Mastery of the German language with very good knowledge of the French language both orally and in writing
