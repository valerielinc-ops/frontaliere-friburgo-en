Put to the point

Are you a motivated, well-organised person interested in technology and vehicle logistics? Would you like to actively contribute to ensuring the availability of a versatile vehicle park and its readiness to use? Then you are exactly right for us!

  • Date of entry: immediately
  • Employment type: indefinite
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20430

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the operational readiness of the vehicles, in particular their loading condition, plan the loading and equip them for the next operation
  • Check the technical condition and proper functioning of the vehicles, trailers and/or the materials stored
  • Manage vehicle inventory and logistics independently and according to the rules
  • Actively help with the delivery of systems and general army materials to the troops and with the withdrawal
  • Edit orders and work orders independently with SAP
  • Administrative control and monitoring tasks

This makes you unique

  • EFZ as a logistics agent, training as a material manager or equivalent training
  • Category B and forklift card; category C and CE driver card
  • Training or professional experience in mechanics
  • A strong interest in vehicle technology and the ability to carry out basic technical checks and identify deficiencies
  • A sense of responsibility, customer orientation, team spirit, flexibility, organizational talent as well as independent, conscientious and efficient working methods
  • Good knowledge of at least one second official language and good knowledge of computer science, especially SAP

Your commitment to security and freedom The Swiss Army Logistics Base (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Army and, with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Army. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors, the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs over 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and this is done at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Young people are also provided for: The Swiss Army trains students in over 30 professions.
