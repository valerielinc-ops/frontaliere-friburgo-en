Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution The Institute for Federalism (IFF) is a nationally and internationally recognized centre of excellence for multi-level governance, democracy, the rule of law and human rights. Its International Research and Advisory Centre (IRCC) aims to contribute to the peaceful coexistence of peoples and the respect and promotion of human rights, democracy, good governance and development through research, teaching, capacity building and advice. Become part of our team! We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link. Their tasks

  • Management of international consulting projects independently or in a team
  • Independent or team-related implementation of training and capacity-building activities
  • Management and coordination of internal and international activities, including budget planning and reporting
  • Support and further development of outreach and communication activities
  • Participation in the development and maintenance of the global IRCC network
  • Supporting the IRCC research agenda in the areas of federalism, decentralisation, local self-government, human and minority rights, democratisation and constitutional development, in particular (but not exclusively) with a focus on the Global South The job is temporary for 4 years (with the possibility of extension, if the financing is ensured). Desired profile
  • Doctorate (PhD) in Law, Political Science, Economics or a related discipline
  • Research experience in the priority areas of the IRCC, in particular in the field of comparative federalism
  • Relevant experience in international consulting, training and capacity building
  • Extensive experience in international project management
  • Sound experience in research and in the acquisition of research funds
  • Willingness to travel within Europe and worldwide
