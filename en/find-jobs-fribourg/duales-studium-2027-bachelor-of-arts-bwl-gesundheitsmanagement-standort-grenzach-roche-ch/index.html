The Position Looking for a diverse and future-oriented dual study course? Then join us in reinventing the future of healthcare. At Roche, we are developing innovative diagnostics and medicines to enable people to live longer and better lives. Join this mission and make a decisive contribution to patients worldwide from the very beginning.

What tasks await you in Daily Business?

  • Market Analysis & Strategy: You analyze the health market, scan trends and help our products to be placed perfectly in doctors, pharmacies and clinics.
  • Health system interface: You learn how the health system and the insurance world work and apply this knowledge directly in business processes.
  • Competent customer advice: You support our customers (from laboratories to medical professionals) and provide the right questions and clear communication for a top cooperation.
  • Project management: You work independently on projects in the field of health economics and actively shape what the medical care of tomorrow looks like.
  • Medical expertise: You acquire a profound knowledge of anatomy and physiology. Highlight of your education: In addition to your Bachelor's degree, you can also qualify for the certified pharma lecturer.

Your practical training will be held at Roche Pharma in Grenzach. The lectures will be held at the Baden-Württemberg Dual University in Lörrach.

Your advantages: We rely on the model of Personalized Training: You actively shape your training content in close consultation with your instructors. We offer you this additional:

  • Finance: Attractive remuneration, holiday and Christmas money.
  • Work-life balance: 30 days of holidays, company sports and comprehensive health care.
  • Development : intensive exam preparation, university entrance qualification (optional) and a wide range of further training.
  • Extras : International work environment, foreign missions and discounted canteen catering.

What condition do you have to meet?

  • General university entrance qualification
  • Specialised university entrance qualification
  • Professional university entrance qualification and proof of the passed suitability test of the Dual University Baden-Württemberg

How long does your dual degree course take? 3 years. Advance one month, company-specific internship
