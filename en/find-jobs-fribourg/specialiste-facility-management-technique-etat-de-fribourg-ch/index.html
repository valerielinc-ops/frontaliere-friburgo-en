Full description

Who are we? The Buildings Service (SBat) is a central service that acts as a service provider to other cantonal departments, services and establishments. As part of the development of our Facility Management (FM) competence centres, we are looking for an FM specialist with expertise in the technical and energy management of buildings in order to meet the growing needs of the building stock of the state of Fribourg. Why join us? The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link Your missions As a specialist in Technical Facility Management, you are actively contributing to the management, optimization and sustainability of the technical facilities of the Fribourg State real estate stock. Your main tasks are:

  • Develop and implement a maintenance strategy for the building's technical facilities, in collaboration with the other Buildings Service specialists
  • Manage external contractors and maintenance contracts, from contract preparation and conclusion to operational, administrative and financial monitoring
  • Ensure the management and development of Facility Management tools, including the identification of business needs, data feeding and collaboration with IT specialists during implementation and developments
  • Lead technical and energy projects, from planning to delivery, in coordination with various internal and external stakeholders
  • Monitoring, analysing and optimizing energy consumption in buildings (heating, electricity, water, photovoltaics, etc.), reporting and proposing improvement measures
  • Propose and monitor compliance, remediation and optimization of technical facilities, in conjunction with project managers in other sections Profile desired
  • SWC installer in the CVSE field, supplemented by a projector's training in building technology, or diploma from a profession derived from building technology (ES energy technician or building technology), with several years of experience in the management of works or complex buildings
  • Excellent knowledge in the fields of technical Facility Management, building facilities (CVS, electricity, energy) and energy management
  • Experience with Facility Management tools (GMAO, CAMM, energy tracking systems) is an asset
  • You are autonomous in your work, flexible and easy contact
  • You are fluent in French or German, with good knowledge of the other language Contact details Thorsten Michels, Head of Building Maintenance and Operations, thorsten.michels@fr.ch, T +41 26 305 38 50 Date of publication:
