Full description

Are you passionate about agricultural production and would you like to play a key role in the poultry value chain? Then come to Micarna, a Migros Group company. Your job is to ensure the care, processing and welfare of the animals in compliance with legal requirements and strategic goals. Mansioni

  • You manage the agricultural sector of the "Poulets" value chain (parent animals, hatchery, fattening) and implement the set goals
  • You ensure the supply of live chickens to the slaughterhouse according to sales needs in compliance with legal standards and animal welfare requirements
  • You use the resources made available in accordance with the company strategy to optimize results and yields
  • You build a professional partnership with the fattening farms and are responsible for the welfare of the animals in accordance with the internal guidelines and applicable ones Official standards
  • You set goals that are tailored to the needs of customers and the company
  • Requirements
  • 5-10 years: Proven experience in a similar position required
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): Degree in agricultural and food technology or equivalent training
  • German (fluent) French (fluent): German or French as a native language with excellent knowledge of the other language (at least level C1)
  • Training in the field of agriculture or the food industry relevant experience in production management or a similar position
  • In-depth knowledge of animal husbandry, care and animal welfare processes
  • Ability to achieve strictly defined annual goals and ensure optimal results
  • Natural leadership skills with the ability to motivate and lead a team in a collaborative spirit
  • Sense of responsibility and compliance with occupational safety standards
  • Confident demeanor in leading activities that require coordination of multiple external locations and remote management
