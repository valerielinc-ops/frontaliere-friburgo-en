Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

KSVA is an independent public institution that manages and coordinates the activities of the Equalisation Fund of the Canton of Freiburg, the Cantonal Invalidity Insurance Office and the Central Services. It thus constitutes a unique competence center for the insured persons and companies of the Canton of Freiburg of all social insurances of the first pillar.

Become part of our team!

We offer a pleasant working environment with a modern infrastructure that is ideally connected to public transport. We promote the reconciliation of private and professional life, training and mobility.

In order to facilitate the processing of applications, we ask all interested persons to attach only a single PDF file with all diplomas, employment certificates and/or other documents.

Their tasks

In the section “Contributions” of the Equalisation Fund

  • Analysis and determination of contributions for a portfolio of insured persons (employers, self-employed, inactive)
  • Replying to correspondence: in writing, by telephone or on the occasion of a personal conversation
  • Care of insured persons’ dossiers
  • Information to insured persons

Desired profile

  • EFZ as a businessman or equivalent education
  • Good knowledge of current computer programs
  • Sociability and skill in dealing with people
  • Social Security experience would be beneficial
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language
