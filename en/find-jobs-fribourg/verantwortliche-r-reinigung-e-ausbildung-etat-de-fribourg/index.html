Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Baubauamt (HBA) acts as a central service provider for all directorates, offices and institutions of the cantonal administration. In order to strengthen the supervision of the cleaning team of the Building Construction Office and to promote the further development of its competences, the Office creates a new post as part of the professionalisation of its activities.

Become part of our team!

The employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. More information can be found on this page.

Their tasks

  • Ensuring the quality, organisation and safety of cleaning work
  • Cleaning staff management and training
  • Coordination of the operations of external cleaning companies and the internal departments concerned
  • Planning of resources according to the requirements and the availability of employees
  • Organisation and monitoring of offers for introductory training and training for all cleaning staff

Desired profile

  • Professional cleaning technology EFZ, professional operating maintenance EFZ, degree as professional housekeeping or equivalent training
  • At least five years of professional experience in the relevant field of activity
  • Demonstrated experience in leading teams
  • Authorisation for apprenticeship maintenance
  • Mastering the German or French language with good knowledge of the other language
