Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for economic and cultural life in the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

The Bachelor of Biomedical Sciences has been offered at the University of Fribourg since 2006. For the planning and coordination of the BMS program, as well as for student counselling, the Medicine section is looking for a coordinator.

The coordinator is integrated into the Med-Office of the Medicine Section and is supported by the Secretariat. He or she works under the responsibility of the Chair of the Bachelor of Biomedical Sciences Commission.

Your missions

  • Coordination and planning of the Bachelor of Biomedical Sciences program:
  • Providing administrative and scientific follow-up to the BMS Study Committee
  • Plan and organize the curriculum with the various internal and external partners
  • Ensure documentation and communication between the various actors of the programme
  • Student Advice:
  • Conduct information sessions and provide individual advice, including to future students.
  • Educational development and support:
  • Provide advice and guidance to teaching unit managers and teachers.

Profile desired

  • Doctorate in a field of natural sciences, preferably in biomedicine or life sciences
  • Educational training and teaching experience
  • Good understanding of the competency approach and formative approaches
  • Experience in organizing and planning university education
  • Self-employment, rigorous and structured
  • Ability to integrate into teamwork, with leadership and communication skills
  • German and/or French, with very good knowledge of the other language (bilingual work)
  • English is required
