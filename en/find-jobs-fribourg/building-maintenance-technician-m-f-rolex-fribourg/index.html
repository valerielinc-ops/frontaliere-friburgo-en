Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for profiles of (M/F) Building Maintenance Technician to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Provide preventive and corrective maintenance of the building's technical facilities, including heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitation and automation
  • Control, adjust and optimize the operation of regulatory systems and installations to ensure their availability and energy performance
  • Diagnosing faults, handling technical alarms and carrying out necessary interventions in accordance with safety priorities and rules
  • Monitoring of centralized supervision and technical management systems, control equipment and building automation
  • Participate in the connection of means of production to technical networks, extension work and implementation projects
  • Coordinate and monitor external stakeholders to ensure the quality, compliance and safety of their services
  • Update technical data and maintenance plans, monitor response requests and contribute to the continuous improvement of facilities

Profile

  • CFCs in health, cold, building technology, automation or equivalent training
  • Additional training of type ES technician or Bachelor in building technology, energy, automation or equivalent field, an asset
  • Experience confirmed in the maintenance of complex technical installations, ideally in the industrial environment
  • Knowledge of HVAC, sanitary, regulatory systems and/or building automation, depending on the field of specialization
  • Mastery of a centralized technical management system, computer-assisted maintenance management tool and common IT tools
  • Knowledge of hydraulic and aeral installations, technical networks, energy automation or building communication protocols, an asset
  • Ability to manage emergencies, prioritize and provide picket service as required
  • Rigor, flexibility, sense of responsibility and adherence to safety protocols
  • Communication, teamwork and customer orientation

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
