Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For the Institute of Psychotherapy of the RFSM in Villars-sur-Glâne:

  • Ambulatory psychotherapeutic care
  • Evaluation and initial interviews of new applications and psychotherapeutic follow-up
  • Distribution of new applications, participation in a regular symposium with officials of the Institute of Psychotherapy
  • Administrative work related to patients (insurance reports, record keeping, etc.)

  • Master in Psychology (or equivalent recognized training)
  • Title of specialist in psychotherapy or at the end of post-graduate training

Profile

  • Psychotherapeutic practice experience
  • Mastery of the German language with good knowledge in French
