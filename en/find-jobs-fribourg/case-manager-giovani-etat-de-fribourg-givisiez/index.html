Who are we?

The Cantonal Institution of Social Insurance (ECAS) is an autonomous institution governed by public law which administers and coordinates the activities of the Caisse de compensation of the canton of Fribourg, those of the Cantonal Office of Disability Insurance and the Central Services. It thus constitutes a single competence centre in social insurance under the first pillar for insured persons and enterprises in the canton of Fribourg.

Why join us?

We offer a pleasant working environment, with modern infrastructure, ideally served by the public transport we support the use of. We encourage reconciliation between private and professional life and continuing training.

Your missions

Within the "Case Management Jeunes Business Partner" section of ECAS:

  • The Young Manager box is responsible for the processing of AI applications for people aged 13 to 25 who are affected in their health, in close collaboration with internal partners (rehabilitative counsellors, lawyers, RMS physicians) and external partners (schools, training settings, medical partners)
  • He/she educates and accompanies the application and coordinates the benefits developed specifically for young people in the context of the continuous development of the AI (detection and early intervention measures, etc.), until the assessment of the right to rent
  • He/she is also the reference contact person for cantonal networks (school, referral service, hospital, etc.), with whom he/she provides advice on disability issues.

Profile desired

  • Bachelor's degree in social sciences or health, or equivalent training
  • CAS in Case Management and/or Continuing Health Education, a more
  • Knowledge and experience in the fields of education, vocational training, social insurance and/or health
  • A recognised professional experience in administrative and social support for people
  • Organized person, able to manage priorities and emergencies
  • Strong analytical and synthesis capacity
  • Experience in administrative work and social support, with a very good sense of communication
  • Ability to work as a team and interdisciplinaryly
  • Perfect command of the French language, with good knowledge of the German language
