Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office for Food Safety and Veterinary Affairs (LSVW) has the task of protecting consumers by means of inspections and analyses against food and consumer objects which are hazardous to health, as well as against deception in connection with food, and of ensuring the welfare and health of animals, both for themselves and for the benefit of humans.

Become part of our team!

Do you want to work for the Freiburg population and become part of a dedicated team composed of diverse and complementary personalities? Join us to pursue a fulfilling profession in a manageable office and at the same time benefit from the stability and resources of a large employer such as the state of Freiburg.

In addition to this interesting environment, you can expect attractive working conditions: a pleasant working environment, a canteen, ergonomic workplaces, the services of the state as an employer and further training opportunities. We also promote bilingualism through language courses in the partner language. For more information, please follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Support of the Head of Section (Animal Health) in the diverse daily administrative work in German and French
  • Ensure administrative handling of animal disease dossiers
  • Preparation of general correspondence and processing of official documents of the section
  • Section contact person for all public and private requests
  • Management and tracking of federal databases in the field of animal health
  • Ensuring a high-quality service through strong communication, discretion and resilience
  • Efficient support as well as high operational readiness and availability in crisis situations

Desired profile

  • Basic Commercial Education (EFZ) and/or Professional Commercial Education (or equivalent) supplemented by several years of professional experience in a comparable capacity
  • Enthusiastic and dynamic personality with strong contact and communication skills
  • Working independently within a team
  • Very good organisational skills with proven pragmatic and efficient prioritisation
  • Responsibility, analytical thinking and solution-oriented approach to handling dossiers
  • Empathetic, discreet, flexible and helpful with a strong service orientation
  • Secure handling of common IT tools
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language as well as excellent editorial skills
