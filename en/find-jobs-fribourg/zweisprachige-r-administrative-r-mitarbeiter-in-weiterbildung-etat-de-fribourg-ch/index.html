The University of Economics (HSW-FR) Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bi- and trilingual training at bachelor and master level.

Become part of our team!

HES-SO//FR has an equal opportunities employment policy. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Planning of the annual training offer
  • Manage the registrations, check the admission documents, prepare and prepare the study contracts, the regulations, the class lists, etc.
  • Ensure processing of applications and support for participants
  • Check compliance with deadlines and receipt of mandatory work before and after the courses
  • Provide administrative support for certificate/diploma theses and final projects
  • Ensuring communication with the speakers (reminder email intervention, course documents, etc.)
  • Create and summarize course assessments
  • Collaboration with the Communications Department to regularly update the website and communication materials, including specific content for the social networks of training and information events
  • Creation and sending of newsletters to the participants of the training
  • Prepare and organise training-related events (diploma ceremonies, networking activities integrated into the curriculum and events with the alumni)

Desired profile

  • Graduate degree in administration/organisation (bachelor in business administration or equivalent education)
  • Several years of professional experience in the administrative management of training programmes
  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to work both independently and in a team: team spirit, the ability to create an effective exchange, versatility and initiative
  • Good written expressiveness and ability to coordinate projects
  • Ability to analyze, aggregate and process data
  • Secure handling of IT and administrative applications (MS-Office) as well as the willingness to familiarize yourself with new platforms
  • Interest in adult education and safety in dealing with different target groups
  • Perfect command of the French or German language as well as very good knowledge of English (at least C1)
