Who are we?

The SPC Bridges and Roadways Service ensures the sustainable development of roads by providing users with a safe and appropriate road infrastructure, managing public road areas and carrying out administrative tasks to achieve these objectives.

Your missions

As a chauffeur-e-cantonnier-ère you perform maintenance work on national roads (motorways):

  • Winter service (snow removal, salting, road condition monitoring)
  • Maintenance of green spaces
  • Cleaning (tunnels, pavements, slope, pipe curing)
  • Light and heavy signalling
  • Permanence of picket (night, Saturday, Sunday and public holidays)

Profile desired

  • SWC of a manual trade or training deemed equivalent
  • Driver's licence heavyweight and trailer (EC)
  • Sensitivity to biodiversity conservation
  • Aisance in various manual works
  • During the picket break, you are able to get to the workplace in 25 minutes
  • Mastery of the French language
