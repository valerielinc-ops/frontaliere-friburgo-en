Full description

Who are we?

Grangeneuve, a multidisciplinary competence centre, is passionately committed to vocational training, expertise, advice and services for the professions of land, nature, milk, agri-food and hotel and stewardship. Its activities are led by more than 280 collaborators working in a bilingual environment, where excellence, respect, collaboration and innovation are encouraged, in a spirit of valuing and transmitting heritage.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Support and advise the Directorate on communications and marketing strategy issues
  • Draft and coordinate the annual report, the report on the activities of the Council of State and the reports for the Consultative Commission
  • Managing the website and social networks for the Directorate
  • Assist and advise managers and collaborators in the field of specialized internal and external communication
  • Ensuring relations with the press and the media in general

Profile desired

  • Master in relation to the field of activity (letters, political sciences, communication, etc.) or training deemed equivalent
  • Post-grade training useful to the function (RP editor,...)
  • Several years of professional experience
  • Knowledge of Grangeneuve's business areas, an asset
  • Mastery of new media and social media
  • Perfect command of the French language with very good knowledge of the German language (B2-C1)
