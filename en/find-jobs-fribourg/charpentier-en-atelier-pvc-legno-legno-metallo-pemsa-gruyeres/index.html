Your assignment

  • Configuration and monitoring of automated equipment: regular, fine-tune and control the machines used in the different phases of production (cutting, processing, drilling, assembly of components) in order to ensure production compliant with quality standards.
  • Active participation in production: preparing materials, handling the carpentry elements and assembling the components, intervening at every stage until the final product.
  • Quality control of the pieces: carry out periodic checks to ensure the technical and visual conformity of the products, identifying any anomalies or deviations regarding the requirements defined.
  • Maintenance of the first level: perform the ordinary maintenance of the machinery (cleaning, visual inspections, simple controls), in strict compliance with current safety standards.
  • Accompaniment and professional development: benefit from a complete integration program with progressive internal training, which promotes the acquisition of skills and secure professional growth within the company.
