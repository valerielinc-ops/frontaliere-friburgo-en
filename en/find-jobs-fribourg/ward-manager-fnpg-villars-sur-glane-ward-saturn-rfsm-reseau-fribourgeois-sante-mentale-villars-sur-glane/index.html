Full description

Poste "Station Manager, FNPG Villars-sur-.Glâne - Station Saturn" au RFSM - Réseau fribourgeois santé mentale, Villars-sur-Glâne (FR). Le RFSM est le pôle de santé mentale du canton de Fribourg : psychiatrie adulte, enfants/adolescents, personnes âgées, addictions.

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence center specializing in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, more broadly, mental health.

Bilingual FNPG is constantly evolving and keeping up to date in the therapeutic field.

Their tasks

For the German and bilingual general psychiatric station Saturn for adults and older people in Villars-sur-Glâne:

  • Responsibility for the leadership and support of the care team
  • Analysis of practices and management of continuous improvement of performance
  • Ensuring the professional development of employees
  • Coordination of interventions of interprofessional team professionals and ensuring connection to external networks
  • Development of clinical projects in cooperation with the interprofessional team and the training area of the Nursing Directorate
  • Work planning and station operation organisation taking into account available resources

Desired profile

  • Recognised expertise in mental health
  • Further training in management at CAS level or willingness to complete such
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Identification with the ethical principles of the FNPG
  • Mastery of the German and/or French language with very good knowledge of the other language (at least level C1 in word and writing)

Personal and social skills

  • Leadership
  • Safe handling of complex situations
  • Resilience and ability to cope with stress
  • Competence in conflict management
  • Strong organisational talent
  • Self-employment and responsibility
