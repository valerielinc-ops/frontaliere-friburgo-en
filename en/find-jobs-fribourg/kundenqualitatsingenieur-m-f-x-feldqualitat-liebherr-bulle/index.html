Your missions

  • As a customer quality engineer, you analyze complaints, take charge of problem resolution projects using appropriate quality methods and tools (Shainin, 5 WHY, 8D),
  • You monitor actions initiated, coordinate Quality Rooms and lead 8D meetings as well as root cause analysis,
  • You regularly give feedback to Management and report complaints and serious problems to your managers,
  • You organize and deploy training on problem-solving methodology and ensure knowledge sharing within the company,
  • You track all key customer quality indicators in a dashboard,
  • You organize and lead customer meetings and visits,
  • You track non-recurrence and lessons learned by contributing to FMEA, design rules and change management,
  • You perform reliability analyzes and statistical analyzes to predict future issues in the field using methods such as Weibull. Your Profile
  • You have successfully completed your studies in the field of mechanical engineering or industrial engineering,
  • You have solid experience in mastering quality methods and tools,
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in the technical and quality field, including 3 years in the field of customer quality and continuous improvement,
  • You have followed specific training in the field of quality (i.e. 8D, VDA6.3, Six Sigma, SHAININ, ISO 9001, etc...),
  • You are proficient in French (C2) and English (C1), German being an asset (B1). Our Offer Can we motivate you to take on a role of responsibility in a dynamic and independent family business?
