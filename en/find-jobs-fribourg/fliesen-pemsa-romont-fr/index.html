Your task

  • Prepare the tile surfaces (sharing, screeds, waterproofing).
  • Place all kinds of coatings: tiles, majolica, mosaics, natural stone.
  • Perform cuts and finishes with precision.
  • Run the stuccoing and ensure the quality of the work done.
  • Read and interpret executive drawings.
  • Respect the safety standards and deadlines provided on site.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Consolidated experience as a tile player.
  • Mastery of laying techniques of tiles, majolica and mosaics.
  • Good project reading capacity and ability to work independently.
  • Attention to details, rigor and care in work.
  • Ability to work in both new construction and renovation sites.
  • Driving license and own vehicle, an advantage.
  • Team spirit, reliability and punctuality.
