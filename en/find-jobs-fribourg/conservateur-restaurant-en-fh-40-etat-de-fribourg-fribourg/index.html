Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office of Archaeology of the Canton of Freiburg (AAFR) identifies, inventories and documents archaeological heritage. It accompanies the administrative processes concerning the ground monuments located in archaeological zones and provides means for carrying out excavations and, where appropriate, obtaining ground monuments. It enhances the documentation created in the form of publications. In order to raise awareness among different target groups about the richness of the archaeological heritage, the Office organises open days as well as lectures and exhibitions

  • It also participates in training.

Become part of our team!

The Office of Archaeology and its sector Collections, Documentation and Information offer the participation in a multidisciplinary, creative and dynamic team dedicated to the management, preservation and valorisation of the cantonal archaeological heritage. Are you ready to work in the Laboratory for Conservation and Restoration (LCR) and put your skills at the service of the conservation and restoration of archaeological movable cultural assets? We look forward to your application. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Preparation of condition analyses of archaeological objects as well as defining and documenting suitable conservation and restoration measures
  • Carry out conservation and restoration work, in particular on inorganic materials, while respecting the integrity of the objects and taking into account traceability, sustainability and heritage conservation
  • Writing treatment reports and scientific articles
  • Packaging of archaeological objects and ensuring their conservation and helping to monitor the state of archaeological collections
  • Archiving the created documents and their integration into the information systems
  • Contribution to knowledge transfer through training of trainees and staff in proven methods of conservation and restoration as well as participation in cultural heritage transfer through guided tours for groups
  • Assistance in the preparation, construction and dismantling of exhibitions

Desired profile

  • You have a Master of Arts in Conservation and Restoration from a technical college or equivalent education
  • You have proven professional experience in the conservation and restoration of archaeological objects
  • They have excellent organizational skills and work very structured, methodical and efficient
  • They are curious and inventive. They like to learn new techniques and develop new skills
  • You are characterized by sociability and communication strength and maintain a good relationship with your employees
  • You enjoy working in a complementary team at the service of the archaeological heritage and can adapt your methods to the existing framework conditions
  • Knowledge of information systems (relational databases) is an advantage
  • You master the standard office software
  • You speak the German or French language and have a good knowledge of the other language
