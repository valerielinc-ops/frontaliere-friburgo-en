Be part of our team!

For Raiffeisen Moléson Bank, a very well-established bank in the southern canton of Fribourg, we are looking for a person like Credit Officer Senior (m/f), a private customer sector.

What are you waiting for?

Key responsibilities

  • Evaluate, approve or reject applications for credit within the jurisdiction assigned.
  • Provide the "Back" role in separating Front/Back functions for non-standard files from private customers.
  • Assist and advise client advisors in the analysis, structuring and processing of funding applications.
  • Identify, assess and communicate risks associated with credit operations, while providing appropriate solutions.
  • Analyse applications for special or exceptional authorisations and coordinate risk transfers with Raiffeisen Switzerland if necessary.
  • Ensure periodic review of credit commitments and compliance with regulatory and internal requirements.
  • Participate in the follow-up of Recovery files and support the teams involved in managing complex situations.
  • Contribute to the monitoring of the credit portfolio and the monitoring of compliance with agreed conditions.

Environment

  • Close collaboration with Front units and Risk teams.
  • Participation in continuous improvement of credit processes and cross-cutting risk management projects.

What are you bringing?

Experience confirmed

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in the analysis, validation or management of credit for private customers.
  • Strong knowledge of mortgage financing and usual guarantees.
  • Experience in managing complex situations or recovery files is an asset.
  • Real estate valuation skills, an asset

Technical expertise

  • Excellent control of financial analysis and reimbursement capacity of private customers.
  • Very good knowledge of banking regulations and credit risk management principles.
  • Ability to assess complex situations and to make sound and consistent decisions.

Training & Certifications

  • Bank training completed with higher education (federal patent, ES diploma, HES or equivalent).
  • Specialisation in credit or risk is an advantage.

Soft skills

  • Rigor and sense of responsibility.
  • Spirit of analysis and critical mind.
  • Ability to argue and defend a position constructively.
  • Excellent relational skills and taste for teamwork.
  • Orientation solutions and direction of priorities.
