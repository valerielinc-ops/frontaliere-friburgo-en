Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office for Civil Status and Naturalization ZEiA fulfils the legal tasks in the area of civil status and naturalizations. This includes, among other things, applications for the preparation of a marriage, family or first name changes, adoption procedures, declarations of recognition and naturalisation applications. The main tasks in the area of naturalisation are the preparation of naturalisation documents for the municipalities, the cantonal and federal authorities and the implementation of all related investigative measures.

Their tasks

The naturalisation investigator is responsible for processing naturalisation applications and checks whether the legal requirements are met. In particular, the tasks shall include:

  • Analysis of naturalisation dossiers
  • Control of residence conditions and integration
  • Conduct of talks with applicants
  • Writing of examination or Clarification reports
  • Cooperation and support with local, cantonal and federal authorities

Desired profile

  • Tertiary education (commercial basic education with specialist card, CAS, FH/HES) in the administrative field or equivalent training
  • Interest in integration issues as well as in the specificities of the Canton of Freiburg and Switzerland
  • Ability to conduct naturalisation talks
  • Strong listening, communication and organisational skills
  • Good analytical and editorial skills
  • Swiss citizenship
  • Excellent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of French in word and writing
