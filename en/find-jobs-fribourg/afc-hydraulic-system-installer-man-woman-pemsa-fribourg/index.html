For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of AFC Hydraulic Plant Installer (man/woman)

Your assignment

  • Build plumbing plants in newly built buildings and under renovation.
  • Install and connect the water supply and drain pipes.
  • Install sanitary fixtures: washbasins, toilets, showers, bathtubs and taps.
  • Perform assembly and connection of sanitary equipment.
  • Making cold and hot water distribution works.
  • Perform work of modification, repair and replacement of existing plants.
  • Locate faults, detect losses and intervene on faulty plants.
  • Read and interpret technical projects and schemes.
  • Check the sealing and proper operation of the plants.
  • Respect the technical standards and safety rules in force.

And now we're talking about you?

  • In possession of a Federal Qualification Certificate (CFC) as a health plant installer or equivalent training.
  • Experience in sanitary facilities and renovation works.
  • Good knowledge of materials, fittings and hydraulic equipment.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings.
  • Mastery of laying techniques, connection and assembly.
  • Autonomous, organized and meticulous person.
  • Service orientation and teamwork.
  • Category B driving licence is a preferential title.
