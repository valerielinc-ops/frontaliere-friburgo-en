Who are we? Grangeneuve, a multidisciplinary competence centre, is passionately committed to vocational training, expertise, advice and services for the professions of land, nature, milk, agri-food and hotel and stewardship. Its activities are led by more than 280 collaborators working in a bilingual environment, where excellence, respect, collaboration and innovation are encouraged, in a spirit of valuing and transmitting heritage. The sector Improving the structures of the Agriculture Section grants financial aid for rural construction, rural engineering, regional development projects and other measures to support farms. Why join us? We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength.
