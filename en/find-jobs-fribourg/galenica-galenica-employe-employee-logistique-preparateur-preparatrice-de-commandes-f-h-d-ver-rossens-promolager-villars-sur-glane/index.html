Full description

Workplace: Verfora AG, Route de Moncor 12, 1752 Villars-sur-Glâne

Do you like practical work, are reliable and appreciate teamwork? In our promotional warehouse, a varied activity awaits you around promotional material, order preparation and shipments. Thanks to your commitment, you contribute to the smooth running of our marketing activities.

Your area of action

  • Prepare and package promotional material and customer orders
  • Assemble displays, promotional packages and order packaging
  • Control receipt of goods and stocking of equipment
  • Participate in inventory inventories and controls
  • Participate in reconditioning and deconditioning of products and displays
  • Prepare materials for training, events and sponsorship
  • Ensure the order and cleanliness of the warehouse
  • Participate in shipping and transportation preparation
  • Provide support during seasonal campaigns and special projects

Your assets

  • Vocational training completed with CFC, preferably in logistics, or equivalent training
  • Experience in warehouse, logistics or production is an advantage
  • Self-employed, structured and reliable method of work
  • Good physical condition and pleasure in performing practical activities
  • Excellent team spirit and great flexibility
  • Good knowledge of German or French; knowledge of both languages is an asset
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office; SAP experience is an advantage
  • Accurate and quality-oriented work method, even in demanding situations

We provide this position without using external investment agencies.

We offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation or taking leave without pay
  • Pension fund with advantageous distribution – we cover nearly 2/3 of the premiums
  • Miscellaneous discounts in our pharmacies and other major brands
  • Opportunities to expand its skills through encouragement of training and development measures and opportunities for internal change
  • Taking into account the needs of families, with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of salary) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of salary), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Nominal actions at preferential prices – not only engage professionally for Galenica, but also invest

About us

A portfolio of products with excellent brands Verfora is a Galenica network company that markets top quality health and beauty products with well-known brands and products in almost all Swiss households: Perskindol®, Anti-Brumm®, Merfen®, Algifor® – to name but a few of the leading products. We have a strong position in the areas of pain, cough, cold and respiratory diseases. Our marketing and distribution activities are primarily aimed at all pharmacies and drugstores. At Verfora, you will take part in a mission: "to help people feel good, to be and stay healthy". In parallel, you are helping to continuously develop our product portfolio and gain new market segments.
