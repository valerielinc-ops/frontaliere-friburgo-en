The Position The primary purpose of the Event Partner is to provide administrative support to the disease area teams in terms of event management and ensure the administrative support in terms of logistics of employees in the affiliate. Handling national and international events and acting in line with Standard Operating Models of the Roche.

Main Responsibilities

Your Role

Organization and Event Management

  • Communicate between team members about evolving requirements and planning.
  • Ensure event follow up and coordination with different stakeholders .
  • Organize and coordinate international and national events logistics
  • Setting, communicating and maintaining timelines and priorities on each event
  • Administrative support to team members of affiliate chapters,
  • Collaboration with finance and procurement team for purchase requisition creation
  • Ensure HCP creation in the system
  • Ensure good record management of all the promotional and non promotional activities
  • Follow up on activities closure and archiving
  • Ensure team’s business travel accommodation
  • Ensure contracts are executed until service delivery and partners payment
  • Ensure the collection of documents to be submitted for signature by the authorized signatories such as agreements,
  • Organizing and managing files and supporting the employees with all organizational tasks.
  • Ensure the logistic part related to all internal and external events and meetings

Who you are

Mindset and Competencies

  • Strong organization skills

  • Autonomous and altruistic in supporting the global network
  • High sense of integrity in everything he/she does (ie open, honest, ethical and authentic)
  • Facilitator leveraging internal and external networks to create value, share and scale.
