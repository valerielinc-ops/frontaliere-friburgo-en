Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Energy Solutions Directorate develops global offers combining consulting, design, execution, maintenance and operation. It is also the single entry point for major projects, energy efficiency and automation, electrical engineering, facility management and innovation.

Mission

  • Participate in the implementation of automation projects for technical infrastructure for remote heating (CAD) and high voltage stations, as well as for HVAC projects, hydraulic or transport production;
  • Programming, testing and commissioning automation solutions;
  • Ensure the setting, maintenance and evolution of developed applications;
  • Carry out on-site commissioning and technical interventions with customers, mainly in French-speaking Switzerland;
  • Participate in facilities documentation and user training;
  • Work with project managers and stakeholders to ensure the quality and reliability of the solutions implemented;
  • Participate in the picket service for remote assistance and support of facilities under mandate.

Profile

  • Automatic CFC or equivalent training;
  • An initial experience in the field of infrastructure, ideally in the field of remote heating and/or CVC;
  • Aisance in customer contact;
  • Knowledge in programming SAIA and Siemens TIA Portal controllers;
  • Knowledge of SCADA/HMI solutions, including In Touch System Platform and WinCC;
  • At ease in the field and motivated to participate in the commissioning of technical facilities;
  • Autonomous, rigorous and with a good team spirit.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
