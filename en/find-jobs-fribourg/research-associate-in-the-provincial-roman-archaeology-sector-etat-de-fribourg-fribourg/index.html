Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office of Archaeology of the Canton of Freiburg (AAFR) identifies, inventories and documents archaeological heritage. It accompanies the administrative processes concerning the ground monuments located in archaeological zones and provides means for carrying out excavations and, where appropriate, obtaining ground monuments. It enhances the documentation created in the form of publications. In order to raise awareness among different target groups about the richness of the archaeological heritage, the Office organises open days as well as lectures and exhibitions

  • It also participates in the training.

Become part of our team!

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Head of archaeological interventions (prospecting, construction support, assessment, excavation), independently or in cooperation with other scientists or excavation technicians
  • Ensuring the quality of scientific documentation, surveying and inventories and, alone or in cooperation, drafting the intervention report and the findings reports
  • Archiving of documents and cooperation in recording in the information system
  • Collaboration on the inventory, investigation and administration of the mural collection
  • Collaboration in the investigation of references or found material and in the production of articles and other publications
  • Collaboration in the valorization of scientific results for a different audience in the context of publications, exhibitions, lectures or other events
  • If necessary, cooperation in the preparation of expert opinions within the framework of the building permit procedure

Desired profile

  • Graduate degree in Roman provincial archaeology or equivalent education
  • Good knowledge of Roman provincial archaeology in Switzerland and adjacent regions with specialization in ancient wall decoration and architecture
  • Interest in the archaeological heritage of the Canton of Freiburg
  • Proven experience in archaeological fieldwork, especially in the field of preventive archaeology
  • Organizational skills, operational leadership competencies, dynamics and adaptability
  • Team spirit combined with high sociability and team skills
  • Experience in scientific publishing
  • Mastering standard office software and geographic information systems (GIS) and basics of photogrammetry
  • Driving licence (category B or higher)
  • Mastery of the French or German language and very good knowledge of the partner language
