Who are we? The State Finance Administration (AFin) is available to the Council of State and State administrative units with the task of ensuring efficient, efficient and sustainable budgetary and accounting management. While most of his efforts are directed towards "inside", his action is also guided by the constant concern to defend the interests of the Fribourgeois taxpayer. The post will be attached to the Cantonal Financial Service, which is responsible for managing the receipt of invoices issued by the various government departments (with the exception of tax claims). In addition, the Office is responsible for litigation. Why join us? We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .
