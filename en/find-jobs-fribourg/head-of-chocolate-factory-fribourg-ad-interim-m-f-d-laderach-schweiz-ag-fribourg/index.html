Full description

High quality fresh chocolate - This is what the Swiss chocolate factory Läderach represents. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs more than 3,000 people from more than 80 countries throughout the group, and the trend is on the rise. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glaris. Here, in the picturesque setting of the Alps, we produce our fresh chocolates and constantly develop new and innovative ideas within agile teams.

The company's retail sector comprises more than 250 chocolate shops in more than 30 countries. This is where we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to rediscover fresh chocolate constantly. You want to enjoy it too? Then join us.

What Expects

  • You lead, motivate and develop a committed team, while promoting an open feedback culture
  • You plan staff placements on team schedules and actively participate in the recruitment process
  • You guarantee the proper functioning of the shop and the respect of hygiene and freshness standards
  • You take responsibility for turnover, performance indicators, cash counts and the achievement of store objectives
  • You analyze the performance indicators and define, with the regional management, targeted measures to develop the point of sale
  • You advise clients with competence and support in choosing from our wide assortment of confectionery and chocolates
  • You ensure an attractive presentation of the goods and ensure their availability through effective order management
  • You ensure the smooth running of cash transactions and guarantee correct counts

What you bring

  • You have several years of experience in selling and managing teams in retail. You stand out for excellent relationship skills and strong leadership
  • You have successfully completed training in the retail trade and/or have equivalent experience in the food field
  • You ideally have a first experience in chocolate
  • You give top priority to quality and customer orientation, even in the most intense times
  • You like to take on responsibilities, you show initiative within the team and you encourage positive collaboration
  • You have strong computer knowledge and you work independently, structured and solution oriented
  • You appreciate open and respectful communication and you have perfect command of French and English; and other languages are an additional asset
  • You are comfortable with flexible work schedules and are ready to assume responsibilities on weekends and holidays

What we offer

  • A stimulating working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with great autonomy and responsibility
  • The possibility of bringing new and innovative ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Attractive selling premium related to achieving common objectives
  • Generous reductions on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Good accessibility of sites

Own it. Grow it
