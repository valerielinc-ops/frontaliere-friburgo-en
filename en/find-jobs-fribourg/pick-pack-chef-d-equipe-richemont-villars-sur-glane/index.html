Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

CONTEXT

Richemont International Distribution recruits for its distribution centre in Villars-sur-Glâne, a Team Leader Pick & Pack. You will be responsible for ensuring the correct execution and compliance of Pick & Pack operations according to the regulations and directives as well as the operational management of the team.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Your main tasks will be

Thanks to your constructive relationship and your results orientation, you can create the right conditions to take your logistics operator team to achieve the goals of quality, cost, delay and safety.

You support them daily in the realization of their activity by coaching and animation of performance.

With your team spirit and analytical capacity, you and your colleagues are constantly developing the performance of your sector.

In addition, we can count on you to make the extra mile that will satisfy our customers 100%.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

To benefit from a higher education (HEG, University or equivalent), you justify a proven experience in team and project management.

You have good knowledge in logistics and Supply Chain.

You like to work in the field, at the heart of the activities, in a dynamic and challenging environment and are willing to work as a team in the afternoon (13h-22h).

Rigoury, organized and autonomous, you have good stress resistance and are able to understand/anticipate problems and view an impact or solution.

Excellent communicator, you ensure efficient communication with all stakeholders and you stand out by your proposal strength and ability to federate.

You are perfectly comfortable with the usual computer tools. Mastery of SAP and POWER BI (or other data analysis tool) is an asset.

You have a perfect command of French (C2) as well as an English level allowing you to communicate easily (min. B2).

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity.
  • We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity.
