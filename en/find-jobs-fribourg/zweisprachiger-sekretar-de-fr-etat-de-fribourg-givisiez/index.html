Who are we?

The Environmental Service ensures the balance between human beings and the environment under federal water protection and environmental protection laws. To this end, it monitors the quality of the environment, contributes to the prevention of harmful damage to new activities, ensures the remediation of existing installations causing damage and informs the authorities and the public about the behaviour causing harmful damage to the environment.
