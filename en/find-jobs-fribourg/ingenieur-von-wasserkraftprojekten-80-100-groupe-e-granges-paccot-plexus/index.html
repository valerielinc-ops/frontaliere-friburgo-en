Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Directorate of Electrical Energy operates hydraulic and thermal production infrastructure, develops production facilities from renewable energy sources and provides electricity supply on the best conditions.

Mission

  • Attached to the head of the Department of Hydraulics, you are piloting large-scale hydroelectric development projects.
  • You lead multidisciplinary projects for the renewal of power plants, production equipment, in the phases of study, realization and commissioning.
  • You establish and optimize their planning by coordinating economic, regulatory, social and environmental aspects.
  • You drive teams of experts both internal and external to the company.
  • You ensure that the quality, cost and time targets are met

Profile

  • Degree in engineering in mechanics or electricity
  • Minimum 7 years experience in the operation or construction of electrical production equipment
  • In-depth knowledge of the operation of hydroelectric installations
  • A recognized leadership, with the ability to bring together experts with a variety of profiles, create commitment around a common vision and advance projects in a complex environment.
  • Experience in managing major multidisciplinary projects in the field of hydraulic production or electrical energy
  • A sense of responsibility and teamwork, conceptual spirit, good communication and good editorial capacity.
  • Mastery of French or German, with good knowledge of the other language and English.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and team: "proud to collaborate together, we are moving towards success".
