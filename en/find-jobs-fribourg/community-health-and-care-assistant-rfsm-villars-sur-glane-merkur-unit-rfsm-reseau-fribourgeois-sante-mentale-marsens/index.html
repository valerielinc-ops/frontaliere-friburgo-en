Full description

Post "Assistant in Community Health and Care, RFSM Villars-sur-Glâne - Merkur Unit" at RFSM - Frebourgeois mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For Merkur Unit, General Adult Bilingual Psychiatry Unit

  • Working in pairs with nurses in the ASSC area of expertise
  • Provide quality care in partnership with the cared person and his/her surroundings, in accordance with the health care philosophy of the RFSM
  • Accompanying patients in daily tasks
  • Provide logistical, administrative and stewardship tasks within the unit
  • Contribute to the supervision of ASSC apprentices

Profile desired

  • Holder of a Community Care and Health Assistant Certificate
  • Have an interest in accompanying and supporting patients in need of psychiatric care
  • Desired experience in a field of personal care
  • Be responsible, organized, autonomous, committed and reliable
  • Training In-company trainer desired (FEE)
  • Mastery of the French or German language with a B2/C1 level in the other language (oral and written)
