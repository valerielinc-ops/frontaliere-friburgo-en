Full description

To strengthen our team, we are therefore looking for a for our Department of Internal Medicine at HFR Tafers

Senior physician or deputy senior physician (60-80 %)

Your responsibilities

  • Ensuring high-quality patient care on the inpatient ward.
  • Participation in training and close supervision of residents and medical students.
  • Helping shape education and continuing training for the department and the regional network
  • Contributing to the further development and continuous improvement of processes on the ward.

Your profile

  • For senior physicians: board certification in General Internal Medicine or an equivalent qualification.
  • For a deputy senior physician: at least 3 years of clinical experience in general internal medicine and successful completion of the written specialist examination.
  • Very good German and good French language skills.
  • Dynamic, open-minded and team-oriented personality with initiative.

We offer

  • Flat hierarchy with open and straightforward interaction in a collegial atmosphere
  • Active interdisciplinary collaboration with all departments of HFR
  • Work in an environment with a high quality of life and leisure opportunities in the vicinity of Freiburg and Bern
  • Specific support (internal curriculum) in the areas of Leadership and Medical Education
  • Ultrasound training (POCUS and abdominal sonography)

Start date
