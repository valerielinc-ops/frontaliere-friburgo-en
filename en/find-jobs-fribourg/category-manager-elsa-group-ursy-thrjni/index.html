Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products and in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

Luogo di Lavoro: Elsa Group, Elsa Group - Ursy, Rue du Plattiez 11, 1670 Ursy

Mansioni

  • Define the marketing mix of the category (product, price, distribution and promotion), manage the profitability of the portfolio and monitor performance indicators (sales, margin) in order to provide markets with appropriate corrective measures
  • Monitor the creation of new items according to the current process (and RACI) and be the guarantor of compliance with the planning proposed by Operations
  • Be the driving force for proposals, especially for innovations in its category (form/packaging/design/...)
  • Having responsibility for implementing an approach to continuous optimization of existing articles in its category
  • Have responsibility for decomplexing the product portfolio of the category
  • Be involved in the reflections of investments (technical projects)
  • Achieving a constant competitive watch, analysing the evolution of the category in markets and interested in consumer insights
  • Contribute to the definition of a development strategy for the category with the Markets
  • Potentially participating in trade fairs in Switzerland or abroad
  • Follow the marketing projects assigned to him and be responsible for the successful implementation plan

Requirements

  • Minimum 2 years experience: in a similar function

  • Mastery of ERP (SAP) computer tools, Office, web
  • Good Project Management and Marketing Knowledge
  • Agri-food and Category Management knowledge
  • Relationship in contacts and customer orientation
  • Sensitive to the themes of profitability
  • French (Good Mastery)
  • German (Very good knowledge)
  • English (preferably)
