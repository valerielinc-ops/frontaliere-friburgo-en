WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

From the beginning of August 2027, you will be trained for 4 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Polytechnician Capacity. During your apprenticeship, you will develop your technical skills and discover different areas of mechanics, depending on the activities and training opportunities.

During this training, you will learn how to make precise, unique or prototype parts. You will use conventional machines and CNC machines (digitally controlled). You will develop skills in programming these machines, and mastering different techniques (e.g. wire erosion or diving).

Responsibilities

  • Make complete mechanical assemblies
  • Carry out machining operations on conventional and CNC machines
  • Schedule and use CNC machines
  • Control the quality of the parts produced
  • Identify and analyse any defects
  • Participate in continuous improvement of production processes
  • Collaborate with different trades and departments

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

  • A good level of French in both oral and written skills to successfully complete vocational training
  • Good mathematical skills and interest in logical and technical reasoning
  • Interest, curiosity and motivation for precision mechanics, technique and watchmaking (an internship experience in this field would be an asset)
  • Manual dexterity, thoroughness and attention to detail
  • Autonomy, rigor and proactivity
  • Team spirit and relationship ease
