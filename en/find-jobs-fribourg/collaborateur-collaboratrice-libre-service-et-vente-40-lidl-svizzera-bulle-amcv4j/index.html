Full description

Do you enjoy having direct contact with customers? Do you want to put all your energy into offering our customers the best shopping experience? Would you like to be part of a great team? Then you have come to the right place. In the dynamic retail sector, interesting and varied career opportunities await you.

Your mission

  • Working at traditional checkouts
  • Assistance during scanning and payment at self-service checkouts
  • Assistance with technical questions or questions relating to operation
  • Approaching customers to encourage them to use self-service checkouts
  • Restocking merchandise and maintaining the store

Your profile

  • Experience in sales or a similar field is an asset
  • Flexibility in working hours (Monday to Saturday between 5h00 and 22h00, morning or afternoon shift)
  • Enthusiasm for direct contact with customers and sharing advice
  • Good physical fitness
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to handle stress and flexibility, even during busy periods
  • Good command of French (proficiency in other languages is an appreciated asset)
