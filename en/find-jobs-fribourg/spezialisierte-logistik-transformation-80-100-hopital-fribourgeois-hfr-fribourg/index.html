Full description

Do you want to contribute to the transformation of the hospital logistics of tomorrow? Are you a key player in the evolution of our Supply Chain?

The Logistics Directorate of the Frebourgeois Hospital (HFR) is looking for its Supply Operations Unit.

Specialist Logistics Transformation

80-100 %

Your mission

You analyse organizations, develop innovative solutions, pilot transformation projects and contribute to the modernization of our processes and tools in order to sustainably improve the performance of our activities at the service of employees and patients.

  • Design and implement innovative logistics solutions
  • Pilot transformation projects within your perimeter
  • Optimize logistics processes and promote their standardization
  • Ensuring functional governance of logistics tools
  • Identify business needs and coordinate their implementation
  • Define business indicators to assess process performance
  • Ensuring that the benefits of deployed solutions are monitored
  • Conduct impact assessments, audits and improvement plans
  • Ensure technological monitoring of hospital supply chain innovations

Your profile

A visionary of Supply Chain, you can imagine, develop and deploy logistics solutions that will make the HFR evolve. You are passionate about improving organisations and new technologies You appreciate understanding how processes work, analyzing complex situations and proposing concrete solutions.

You have

  • Higher education (federal patent, ES diploma, HES Bachelor or equivalent) in Supply Chain, logistics, business organization, process management
  • A recognised certification in continuous improvement (Lean, Six Sigma, etc.) is an important asset
  • Min. 5 years of experience in organizational transformation, continuous improvement, process optimization or Supply Chain, ideally within a complex environment
  • Confirmed experience in deploying organizational, digital or logistical solutions that have generated measurable improvements
  • Strong interest in innovation and pleasure in identifying, evaluating and implementing new technologies
  • Good understanding of ERP systems and their business processes is an asset
  • Excellent analysis and problem solving capability
  • Initiative, autonomy and strong focus results
  • Human contact is a natural source of motivation, allowing to unite interlocutors from different professions, to animate working groups and to accompany change
  • Very good mastery of French, knowledge of German an asset.

We offer you

  • A strategic position at the heart of the Hospital Supply Chain transformation
  • The possibility of developing and implementing innovative solutions with a concrete impact on the operation of the establishment
  • Various projects combining innovation, digitalisation and continuous improvement
  • Close collaboration with business teams and internal partners
  • A stimulating environment for autonomy and skills development
  • The opportunity to contribute to the design of the logistics of the future HFR
