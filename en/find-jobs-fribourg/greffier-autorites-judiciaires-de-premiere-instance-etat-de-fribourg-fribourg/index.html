Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Peace Court is primarily the child and adult protection authority. It shall act on request or on its own initiative. Upon completion of the procedure, a decision shall be taken as to whether to impose a measure. The peacekeeper also has competences in inheritance law as well as in the area of judicial prohibitions of entry.

Their tasks

  • Constitution of resolutions in civil law, in particular in the area of adult and child protection law in French and German
  • Participation in meetings of the Adult and Child Protection Authority and minutes at court hearings
  • Management of the court clerk: contacts with the persons concerned and the persons close to them as well as with branch offices, in particular for the legal assistance of advisers and third parties
  • Telephone and general information
  • Legal investigations
  • Management of court records and constitution of general correspondence

Desired profile

  • Completed law degree (licence or master)
  • Several years of professional experience, preferably as a court clerk
  • Excellent knowledge of law, independence, resilience and teamwork
  • Communication and organizational ability as well as pleasant manners
  • Excellent IT knowledge
  • Mastering French or German with very good oral and written knowledge of the other language
