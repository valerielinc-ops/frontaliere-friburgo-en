Context

The objective of this internship is to enrich your academic background with a stimulating professional experience in an international environment.

In our Supply Chain department in charge of the distribution of finished goods, you will be part of a highly established team and you will contribute to the following missions:

Collaborate with markets, marketing and external supplier on their problematics,

Create reporting and maintain existing reports, particularly under Power BI or Anaplan

Participate in continuous improvement projects related to the department activity

Your profile

You are in your last year master’s degree in engineering with a Supply Chain focus.

You have advanced skills in Excel and/or experience with Power BI.

You have robust analytical skills, and you easily analyze and work with Data and Information Systems.

You are organized, autonomous and reliable.

You have a very good command of French and English.
