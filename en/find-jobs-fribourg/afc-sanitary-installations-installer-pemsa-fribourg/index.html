Your assignment

  • Realize the complete installation of sanitary facilities in newly built buildings and under renovation.
  • Pose and connect water, exhaust and gas pipes according to current designs and standards.
  • Mount and set up the sanitary fixtures (washers, toilets, showers, bath tubs, water heaters, etc.).
  • Perform maintenance work, emergency intervention and repair of sanitary facilities.
  • Check the quality of the plants and perform sealing tests.
  • Respect the safety standards, deadlines and quality standards of the company.
  • Effectively collaborate with other professional figures in the yards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • In possession of an AFC (Federal Capacity Certificate) as a hydraulic installer or a training considered equivalent.
  • Proven experience in the hydraulic sector in Switzerland is an advantage.
  • Good readability and understanding of technical drawings.
  • Knowledge of Swiss standards and regulations in force.
  • Autonomous, rigorous and equipped with a good team spirit.
  • Category B driving licence desirable.
  • Good physical form and sense of responsibility.
