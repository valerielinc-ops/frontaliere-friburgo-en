Full description

Introduction For our Research & Development division, we are looking for a Prototypist Technician (M/F) whose main mission will be to contribute to the manufacturing of prototypes in various materials using various technologies on our Romont site. Responsibilities

  • Program and machine complex watch components in a wide variety of materials on CNC machines using technologies such as milling and grinding
  • Dimensionally control the manufactured components and associated tooling
  • Ensure delivery of components in compliance with quality requirements and deadlines
  • Document and sustain the studies as well as the measurements carried out Profile
  • Polymechanic CFC or equivalent
  • Confirmed experience in grinding, machining of various materials and CAD/CAM programming of CNC machines
  • Experience in various technologies such as laser texturing of materials
  • Experience in machining molds or tools necessary for the manufacture of watch components, an asset
  • Versatile, autonomous, agile and team-oriented personality
  • Ability to adapt and learn, curiosity, rigor, pragmatism and sense of innovation
  • Flexibility to travel to our Geneva sites if necessary Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and first-rate social benefits. We thank you for sending a complete application file (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
