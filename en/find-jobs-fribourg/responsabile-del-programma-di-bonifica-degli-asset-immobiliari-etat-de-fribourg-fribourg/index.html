Who are we?

The Buildings Service develops the major new construction or renovation projects by promoting the architectural competitions and intended for the activities of the state of Fribourg: Training, Justice and Police, Social Domain, Administration, Research Laboratories, Logistics are some of the areas in which we pilot the state projects.
