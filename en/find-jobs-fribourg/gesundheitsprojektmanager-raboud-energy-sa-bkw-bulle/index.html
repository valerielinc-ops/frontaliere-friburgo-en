Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

(all genres)

As a Health Project Manager, you are responsible for the full management of your projects, from acquisition to final delivery.

Your tasks

  • Develop and maintain your customer portfolio, set offers and negotiate prices.
  • Pilot health projects in their entirety.
  • Initiate implementation plans and plans with the technical office.
  • Coordinate the various stakeholders (architects, agents, suppliers, subcontractors and other trades).
  • Organize assembly teams in collaboration with other project leaders.
  • Order the equipment and ensure the smooth running of the works.
  • Monitor the financial aspects of projects (budget, costs, billing).
  • Ensure the quality of the facilities until the works are delivered.
  • Actively participate in internal coordination sessions.

Your profile

You are an organized, autonomous and solutions oriented person.

You bring in particular

  • An installation CFC or projector in sanitary building technology or equivalent training.
  • A proven experience in managing health projects of all sizes.
  • Excellent organizational and planning capacity.
  • A common sense of negotiation and a strong sensitivity to costs.
  • Ease of dealing with clients and partners.
  • Natural leadership and teamwork.
