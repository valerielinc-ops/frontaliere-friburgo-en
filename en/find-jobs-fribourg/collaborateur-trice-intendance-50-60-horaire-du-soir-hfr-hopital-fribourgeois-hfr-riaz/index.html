To complement its team, the Logistics Directorate of the Fribourgeois Hospital (HFR) is looking for the Intendance service of the HFR Riaz un-e

Co-worker Intent 50-60% (evening schedule)

Your mission

  • clean and maintain care units (public, sanitary, rooms, offices and common premises)
  • ensure routine, intermediate and full cleaning, in accordance with the applicable hygiene and safety standards
  • follow the disinfection standards of the Unit for the Prevention and Control of Infection (UPCI)
  • establish internal controls for the stock of equipment such as cleaning products
  • take initiatives relating to the organisation of work, respecting its field of knowledge
  • work also on weekends, holidays according to planning
  • be versatile in the various sectors of stewardship
  • flexibility in working hours according to the needs of the service

Your profile

  • SWC of Stewardship Manager, SWC of Cleanliness Officer or a few years of experience in a similar position
  • very good knowledge of the French language: oral and written expression and understanding
  • provided with a common sense of organization, dynamic and precise, good physical and flexible resistance
