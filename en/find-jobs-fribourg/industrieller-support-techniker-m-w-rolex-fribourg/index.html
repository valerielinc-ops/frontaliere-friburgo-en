Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for profiles of (M/F) Industrial Support Technician to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure level 2 maintenance and support of IT equipment integrated with the means of production
  • Diagnosing and resolving incidents related to computer workstations, business applications and industrial network infrastructure
  • Configure positions connected to machine tools, benches and measuring devices
  • Set up industrial networks for the transfer of machining programmes and data exchange
  • Set up and configure network equipment and internal communication applications
  • Assist suppliers in telemaintenance operations on industrial equipment
  • Participate in projects to deploy and evolve IT solutions in the industrial environment

Profile

  • SWC informatician or equivalent training
  • Experience confirmed in Level 2 industrial support
  • Good understanding of technical computing, including networks, hardware, software, security systems and automation
  • Knowledge of industrial means of production and communication protocols
  • Experience in participation in industrial environment projects
  • Certification in IT Services Management, an asset
  • Technical versatility and ability to adapt quickly to various technologies
  • Communication, team spirit and strong customer orientation
  • Rigor, organizational sense, proactivity and stress resistance

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
