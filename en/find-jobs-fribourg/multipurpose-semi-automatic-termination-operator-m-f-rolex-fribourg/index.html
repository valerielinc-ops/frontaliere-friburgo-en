Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a versatile (M/F) termination operator whose main mission will be to perform polishing operations on semi-automatic machines.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out production monitoring on semi-automatic machines (loading/unloading) in accordance with production standards and procedures
  • Perform standard changes in autonomy, know the tools and consumables required for production and verify compliance
  • Perform simple mechanical operations on semi-automatic machines
  • Follow manufacturing instructions and procedures
  • Ensure dimensional and aesthetic control of parts produced according to defined criteria
  • Perform production tracking computer input

Profile

  • Training of polisher/terminator, AFP of mechanical practitioner or operator training on automated machines (AMO)
  • Experience in polishing or finishing desired components
  • Imperative experience in industry and control of semi-automatic machines
  • Significant experience on automated machines or experience as operator-regulator on machining means
  • Good visual acuity, rigorous, methodical and attention to detail

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
