We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Planner of electrical installations

Your assignment

  • Process electrical plans and schematics using CAD software.
  • Participate in the design and sizing of electrical installations.
  • Perform the technical calculations necessary for the projects.
  • Draft contract specifications and technical documentation.
  • Ensure project monitoring in collaboration with project managers, customers and installation companies.
  • Check project compliance with current standards and regulations.
  • Relief on site and update projects based on the progress of the works.
  • Participate in coordination meetings with the different subjects involved in the project.

And now we're talking about you?

  • In possession of an AFC as an electric designer
  • Professional experience in such a role is an advantage.
  • Master of technical drawing software (AutoCAD, Eplan, Caneco or equivalent).
  • Good knowledge of Swiss electrical standards (NIBT).
  • Ability to work independently and on team.
  • Sense of organization, precision and rigour.
  • Good relationships and ease in communication with customers and partners.
  • French mastery; knowledge of any other language is an advantage.
