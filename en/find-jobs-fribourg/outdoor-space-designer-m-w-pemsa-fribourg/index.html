Your assignment

And now we're talking about you?

  • Experience in landscape design, outdoor spaces or civil engineering is an advantage.
  • We are also available to evaluate profiles from other sectors of the technical design that want to reorient itself towards the design of outdoor spaces.
  • Good mastery of computer-assisted drawing software (AutoCAD or equivalent).
  • Team spirit, autonomy and ability to adapt.
  • Interest in accommodation projects and work in collaboration with field staff.
