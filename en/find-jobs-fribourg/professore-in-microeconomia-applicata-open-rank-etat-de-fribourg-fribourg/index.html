Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research center, employer and event organizer all in one. Their innovative strength makes them an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its founding in 1889.

The position includes one 100% doctoral assistant. The University offers attractive working conditions and a competitive salary. The position is open rank, in accordance with University statutes.

Seeking to promote an equitable representation of women and men, the University strongly encourages applications from women. Having signed the CoARA declaration, the University of Friborg emphasizes qualitative assessment of academic achievement.

Important

Please note : Do not use the "apply" button at the top of this page: your application will not be processed. Apply electronically at Appl-Micro@unifr.ch

Candidates should submit a complete application package (motivation letter, CV, list of publications, teaching evaluations, administrative experience, third-party funding, research and teaching statement, three references, and three representative publications)

Your tasks

  • The successful candidate will teach courses in French and English at the Bachelor’s and Master’s levels. Two courses will be given in French in Microeconomics at the Bachelor’s level. The research profile as well as offered Master courses should fall within applied microeconomics. Possible areas include, for example, labor economics, health economics, education economics, competition, personnel economics, or innovation economics.

Desired profile

  • The successful candidate must have a doctorate (Ph.D.) in economics or a related field, and demonstrate an excellent research record in applied microeconomics, strong teaching skills, and the ability to supervise theses at the Bachelor’s and Master’s level.
  • The University of Friborg is bilingual (French/German). Fluency in French is required and knowledge of German (oral level B1) is expected. If this is not the case, it must be acquired within two years of taking up the position.

Contact person

Prof. Dr. Berno Büchel, berno.buechel@unifr.ch
