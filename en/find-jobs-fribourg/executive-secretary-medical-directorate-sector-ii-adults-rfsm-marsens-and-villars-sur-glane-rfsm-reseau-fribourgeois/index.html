Full description

Position "Secretary of Management, Medical Directorate Sector II - Adults, RFSM Marsens and Villars-sur-Glâne" at the RFSM - Frebourgeois mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

Directly attached to the Medical Directorate of the Adult Psychiatry Sector (Director and Assistant Medical Officer/Director), you will have the following main tasks:

  • To provide administrative support for the organization of clinical, scientific and administrative activities of the Medical Directorate
  • Administrative management of the Executive Secretariat
  • Follow fee notes, budgets and certain routine administrative or accounting tasks
  • Ensure, catalogue and coordinate the projects and files entrusted by the Medical Directorate
  • Coordinate working meetings (sector college) and perform various tasks arising from the sessions: PV, correspondence, transmission of information, follow-up of files.
  • Organize internal and external events on behalf of the Medical Directorate (seminars, formations, assemblies, external sessions).
  • To collaborate in organizing the training activities of the RFSM Academy
  • Assist the Medical Directorate in the recruitment and management of medical personnel
  • Collaborate in medical custody planning
  • Ensure the link between the Medical Directorate and other branches and services of the RFSM
  • Collaborate with the administrative coordinators of RFSM Marsens and RFSM Fribourg
  • Ensure the confidentiality of sensitive information

Profile desired

  • You have an EWC of a business employee and a Federal Patent d-Assistant Management is desired, or title deemed equivalent
  • You benefit from a professional experience confirmed in a similar position for at least 3 years, with a strong interest in the field of mental health
  • You know how to operate with flexibility and autonomy
  • You have a sense of initiative, are reliable, conscientious and assume your responsibilities
  • You have excellent mastery of the usual computer tools
  • You have excellent oral and written knowledge of French, with very good knowledge of German (B2) or the reverse
