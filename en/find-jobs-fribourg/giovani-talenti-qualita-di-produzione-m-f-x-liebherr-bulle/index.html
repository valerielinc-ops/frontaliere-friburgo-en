Full description

Are you eager to kick-start your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our internship programme and benefit from a hands-on learning experience to develop your skills and gain valuable professional experience. We are looking for candidates available for a 12-month internship starting on March 1, 2027. Your responsabilities

  • You create the right transparency and visualization of defects and nonconformities to support the defect culture/Q-Mindset inside the organization,
  • You review, verify and regularly update the product & process control plans including reaction plans, as well as their creation if not yet existing,
  • You support the production organization in analyzing nonconformities and implementing immediate and corrective actions, including customer related issues,
  • You manage and support the task forces for specific production issues,
  • You conduct statistical evaluations, SPC, capability studies (complaints, manufacturing, assembly and test bench data),
  • You prepare the regular KPI reports, as well as the internal and external audits ,
  • You support the change management process, as well as the FMEAs,
  • You provide support in internal and customer sample inspection reports (VDA/PPAP),
  • You assist in concession process for production related nonconformities and manage non recurrence. Your profile
  • You hold a master’s degree in mechanical or industrial engineering and have a good knowledge of quality management,
  • You are proficient in statistical analysis,
  • You are proficient in quality methods and tools (Pareto, QRQC, 8D, Ishikawa, PDCA, FTA, FMEA, Lean-6 Sigma) and demonstrate a strong commitment to quality and continuous improvement,
  • You are fluent in French and English. German is a asset,
  • You are proficient in the Minitab statistical software as well as the MS Office suite,
  • You enjoy working in a team and have strong analytical and synthesis skills,
  • You stand out for your thoroughness, attention to detail, and sense of responsibility. Our offer You will have the opportunity to immerse yourself in the professional world and gain diverse and exciting experience within an international family-owned company.We offer you the chance to take on interesting tasks, assume responsibilities and bring your own ideas to life. You will receive training, support and guidance from a dynamic team. You will benefit from modern working conditions, including flexible working hours, the possibility of working from home, an on-site company restaurant and many other benefits. Your workplace will be located in Bulle, in the beautiful Gruyère region of Switzerland. Shape your personal and professional development with us!
