Full description

Who are we?

Mobility has many environmental and economic impacts. In recent years, its management and planning have become increasingly important, given the increase in individual mobility and the use of public transport. The SMo Mobility Service manages the canton's mobility aspects.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Management of cantonal and regional planning
  • Verification of mobility aspects at cantonal, regional and communal level
  • Verification of the compliance of Local Development Plans (LDPs) and Retail Development Plans (PADs) with respect to mobility (validation of traffic studies)
  • Development, coordination and monitoring of sectoral planning
  • Advice to municipalities and the Bridges and Roads Service for mobility infrastructure plans (PIM)
  • Advice to representatives and communes

Profile desired

  • University (Master) or EPF or HES engineering training or training deemed equivalent with experience in legislative and mobility planning
  • Interest in implementing legislation, standards and planning instruments
  • Mastering the German language with very good knowledge of the French language
