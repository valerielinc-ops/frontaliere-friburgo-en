Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. CONTEXT

Richemont International Distribution recruits for its distribution center in Villars-sur-Glâne, a Logistician CFC Apprentice for the re-entry of August 2027.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

As an apprentice, you will learn how to manage the flow of goods from receipt to shipment. You will participate in the control of deliveries, the registration of products in computer tools and their storage, ensuring their identification and storage.

You will prepare the orders, carefully package the products and contribute to their shipment within the deadlines and quality requirements. You will also be involved in inventory monitoring and inventories to ensure data reliability and commodity availability.

As you learn, you will become familiar with the handling equipment and digital tools used in logistics. In each of your activities, you will apply safety instructions and adopt environmentally friendly practices.

This course is for anyone who appreciates concrete activities, movement and teamwork, and wants to discover the different stages of the supply chain.

HOW ARE YOU TO USE US?

You will have completed your compulsory schooling at the beginning of August 2027 and show a strong interest in logistics, commodity management and computer tools.

Methodical and organized, you like to work with precision and have good ease with the numbers. You enjoy an active profession and exercise care in handling products as well as in capturing information.

Curious and motivated to learn, you know how to follow instructions and invest in the tasks entrusted to you. Your team spirit, sense of responsibility and progressive autonomy will be assets to succeed in your learning.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity.
  • We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity.
  • We take care of the world we live in.
