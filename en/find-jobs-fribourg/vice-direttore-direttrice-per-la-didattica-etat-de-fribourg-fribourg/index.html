Their tasks

As a member of the management team, you have strategic responsibility for the teaching sector. In this role, you will contribute to the definition of institutional orientations and ensure excellence, coherence and further development of the educational offer of the HSW-FR.

  • Participation in the management and strategic development of HSW-FR
  • Management of academic managers and coordination of study programmes and associated services (bachelor, master, continuing education, EMBA, language and career centres)
  • Ensure the academic quality of courses and the continuous improvement of training offers
  • Monitor the accreditation procedures and the implementation of the AACSB standards and the “Assurance of Learning” (AoL) processes
  • Promoting educational innovation, digitalisation and the integration of artificial intelligence into teaching
  • Further develop and expand the training offer in order to strengthen the attractiveness and positioning of HSW-FR
  • Promoting synergies between degree programmes, applied research and business

Additional information

  • Level of employment: 40-50 %
  • This position can be supplemented 80-100% by the second currently advertised position as Deputy Director aF&E and Industry Contacts

Desired profile

  • Doctorate (PhD) or equivalent degree in a relevant field, ideally complemented by further training in management or leadership
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss higher education system and landscape, especially in the field of teaching, as well as the ability to become familiar with international standards of quality assurance and accreditation, in particular AACSB
  • Experience in managing and developing training programmes
  • Strong interest in educational innovations, digital transformation and the use of artificial intelligence in teaching
  • Leadership, team spirit and the ability to navigate an environment of experts
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language as well as knowledge of English
