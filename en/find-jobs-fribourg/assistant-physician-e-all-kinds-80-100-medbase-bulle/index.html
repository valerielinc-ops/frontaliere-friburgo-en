Welcome to Medbase Bulle

Located in the heart of the southern extension of Gruyère Centre, the Medbase Bulle medical centre extends over 650 m2 and benefits from new, bright and well-equipped infrastructures, including a laboratory and radiology service. The site hosts an expanding team, currently composed of some 15 collaborators (doctors and medical assistants) engaged in interprofessional care, mainly in general internal medicine and rheumatology. A medical clinic provides emergency care from Monday to Saturday. Medbase Bulle attaches particular importance to the balance between work and private life. All jobs are open to part-time work with flexible working hours. For doctors, collaboration is possible under paid or independent status, to be agreed upon during the interview.

What You Can Do You take care of patients under supervision and deepen your knowledge of general primary internal medicine. Medbase offers you a constructive and caring environment for your post-graduate training.

  • Management of patients in general internal medicine under supervision
  • Self-employment in the face of patients with the possibility of constantly referring to a professional doctor present (you never be alone with us!)
  • Primary medical activity according to the principles of Managed Care
  • Regular trainings such as: Journal-Club, Emergency situation in bed, CIRS, ECG courses and sutures etc.

What you bring

  • Degree in medicine obtained and interest in post-graduate training in general internal medicine
  • A desire to learn and progress in a caring atmosphere
  • Team spirit and orientation solutions
  • A sense of responsibility and empathy
  • Good command of French (minimum B2 level)

To achieve great things, one must have a vision of the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Fribourg?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Fribourg's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Fribourg's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
