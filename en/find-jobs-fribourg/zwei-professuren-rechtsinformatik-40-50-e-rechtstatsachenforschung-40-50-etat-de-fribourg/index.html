40-50% for each of the two professorships.

We are happy to accept your application for one of the two professorships, but it is also possible to apply for both professorships at the same time.

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser all in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

Their tasks

The posts are for the time being limited to five years.

  • They teach and research in the field of legal informatics (including legal tech) or legal fact research.
  • They supervise students and support doctoral students in writing their dissertation.
  • In addition, you are involved in further education and faculty self-management.

Desired profile

  • They have excellent scientific skills and are enthusiastic about teaching.
  • You have a doctorate in a field that enables you to make significant contributions in the field of legal informatics or legal fact research.
  • You will also have in-depth legal knowledge about which you can prove yourself with a university BLaw, a MALS or a similar degree.
  • You have already published in the field of legal informatics and legal fact research.
  • Ideally, you have already worked in teaching. You also have the ability to raise third-party funds and are ready to participate in academic self-management.
  • You master either the German or the French language perfectly and the other language at a high level, which allows you to teach in this language as well. It is also beneficial if you are able to teach in English.
