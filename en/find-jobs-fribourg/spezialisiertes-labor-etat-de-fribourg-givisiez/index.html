Who are we ?

The Environmental Service ensures the balance between humans and the environment in accordance with federal laws on water protection and environmental protection. To this end, it observes the quality of the environment, contributes to the prevention of harmful damage from new activities, ensures the sanitation of existing installations causing damage and informs the authorities and the public about behavior causing harmful damage to the environment.

Why join us?

Your missions

  • Carry out physicochemical analyzes on environmental samples
  • Participate in field sampling campaigns
  • Apply and contribute to maintaining the requirements related to the quality assurance system according to the ISO 17025 standard
  • Ensure the preparation, management and traceability of samples in the laboratory
  • Contribute to the general functioning of the laboratory and actively collaborate within the team

Desired profile

  • You hold a CFC as a chemical laboratory assistant or training considered equivalent
  • You are comfortable with laboratory work carried out in a standardized environment
  • You enjoy field work and collecting environmental samples
  • You have good teamwork and collaboration skills
  • You are comfortable with technical work as well as the use and maintenance of testing equipment
  • You have excellent writing skills and are fluent in French or German, with very good knowledge of the other language
