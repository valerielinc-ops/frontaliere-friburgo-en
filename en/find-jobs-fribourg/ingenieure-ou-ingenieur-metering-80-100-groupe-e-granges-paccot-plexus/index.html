Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Electricity Distribution Directorate builds, maintains and operates the electricity distribution network. Through the development of innovative offers, it ensures the technological mastery of projects such as the realization of the intelligent network or public lighting.

Mission

  • Actively contribute to the evolution of Smart Metering solutions by ensuring the matching of business needs, regulatory developments and technical constraints associated with measurement systems;
  • Supervising Smart Metering systems to ensure the smooth operation and evolution of the meter to data chain in collaboration with internal and external stakeholders;
  • Identify opportunities for system and process improvement, support functional or technical developments, guide their implementation and contribute to simplification and automation of operational activities;
  • Define, maintain and evolve measurement principles, counting relationships and technical standards to ensure consistency and reliability of counting data;
  • Support field teams, trades and internal partners by providing reference expertise and second-level support in the areas of Smart Metering and measurement systems;
  • To enhance Smart Metering data by developing analytical, reporting and decision support tools that contribute to operational efficiency

Profile

  • Bachelor or Master in electricity, computing, telecommunications or equivalent domain;
  • Experience in electrical networks, measurement systems, Smart Metering or comparable technical infrastructure;
  • Experience in transversal technical projects, with the ability to understand interconnected information systems, to drive evolutions and to transform business needs into operational solutions;
  • Knowledge of tools for analysing, valuing data and automating operational processes and tasks (Power BI, Python or equivalent technologies);
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities, autonomy, initiative, solution orientation and ability to collaborate with various interlocutors.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success."
