Who are we? The Service de l'enseignement obligatoire de langue française (SenOF) is responsible for the conduct of teaching and the management of teaching at different levels of compulsory education in the French-speaking part of the canton. It also supervises private schools and home education. Why join us? The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link . Your missions In accordance with the guidelines laid down by the Compulsory Education Service and the DFAC, the deputy or assistant director of a CO school is responsible for assisting the director in his or her organizational, administrative and pedagogical management of the school, the quality of teaching and education, and collaboration with all school partners. This position can be supplemented by teaching hours. Profile desired

  • Secondary degree I (master)
  • Minimum 8 years of professional experience (education)
  • If possible CAS in management and management of training institution or training deemed equivalent
  • Good knowledge of school level
  • Ability to build positive human relationships with youth and adults
  • Experience in conducting educational projects and/or working groups
  • A sense of communication, collaboration and responsibilities within a team
  • Computer software mastery
  • Interest in student monitoring
