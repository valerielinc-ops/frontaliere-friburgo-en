Full description

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

Become part of our team!

Would you like to accompany young people in their training as a teacher for TTG and promote specialist development in their participation in a new study programme?

To complement our team in the field of didactic design, we are looking for a creative, open and communicative personality who is broad-based in design and works well-founded and future-oriented.

We offer a self-determined working environment in a practice-oriented, academic environment with diverse and varied tasks in the training of teachers for primary and secondary level 1 in textiles and technical design.

Their tasks

  • Development and implementation of courses as well as evaluation of student performance in the field of expertise, practice and didactics in the training of teachers for primary and secondary level 1
  • Supervision of students in their professional training (mentorate)
  • Cooperation in the administrative work of the specialist team
  • Participation in further education for teachers possible

They bring with them

  • a master’s degree in didactics TTG, educational sciences, design or comparable disciplines
  • a teaching diploma for primary and/or secondary level 1
  • Teaching experience in primary and/or secondary school in design
  • University didactic experience desired
  • High communication skills and pleasure in working with students and teachers
  • a future-oriented didactic understanding of the subject of TTG
  • Enjoyment of conceptual work for the design of a new study program
  • Safe mastery of the German language with good knowledge of the French language
