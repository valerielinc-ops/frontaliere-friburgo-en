Full description

Reference code: JR136009

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

Be part of an exciting experience within our Richemont International SA Financial team based in Fribourg. As part of our European Payment Factory managing payments for more than 100 Richemont entities, you will actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our payment factory processes, while managing and defining optimized payments and receipts flows.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Reporting to the Payment factory Manager, this role will be responsible for the following responsibilities, including but not limited to:

PROJECT LEAD

  • Gather, validate, and document RISA FIN payment factory team’s operational requirements
  • Provide hands-on technical support for the integration of new entities and processes into the Payment Factory, including SAP testing: prepare test scenarios, execute tests, document results, and follow up on defects through resolution
  • Work with business stakeholders to review and improve existing processes, leveraging available resources and tools to automate routine tasks and reduce time spent on repetitive work
  • Estimate implementation costs and evaluate business benefits
  • Support business transition and help to establish change (train and guide end users)
  • Promote the payment factory tools across our entities with presentation and training to all departments involved
  • Organize and participate to meetings and workshops with internal and external stakeholders
  • Participate and coordinate improvements initiatives and specific projects in the RISA FIN payment factory department
  • Assist line management in prioritizing projects
  • Prepare project progress reports with project managers; comment and communicate consolidated results to management
  • Maintain a risk and issue register, following up on related action plans

Tools

  • Build and present management dashboard using Business Intelligence tools or any other tool based on needs
  • Organize dedicated training on payment factory tools
  • Identify, propose and coordinate roll-out of targeted tools for each process
  • Assist with process automation via BI, SQL, ACL, Python or other solutions

OPERATIONAL Payment Factory

  • Manage payment runs and exceptional payment
  • Coordinate the month-end closing, preparing and analysing Trial balance schedules, and performing ad-hoc analysis
  • Participate to the monitoring of bank relationships
  • Support the financial closing process
  • Support in cash management

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

Personal and methodological skills

  • Critical mind with ability to challenge the status quo and propose improvement opportunities
  • Creative and out-of-the-box thinker
  • Agile and continuous-improvement mindset, with a practical approach to testing ideas, adapting to changing priorities, and delivering incremental improvements that reduce routine workload
  • Reliable, rigorous and diligent with an analytical mindset
  • Excellent project management skills
  • Strong appetite for technology
  • Strong work integrity

Social skills

  • Team player
  • Ability to communicate appropriately according to audience
  • Public speaking and presentation skills

Education and experience

  • Minimum 4 years of professional experience in either a similar role or in a Big 4 environment
  • University degree in corporate finance or equivalent
  • Hands-on SAP expertise, including experience in testing new entity and process integrations, preparing and executing test scenarios, documenting results, and following up on defects
  • At ease with advanced Excel functionalities
  • Work experience with data visualization such as PowerBI, SAP Analytics Cloud and/or Looker
  • Experience in project management
  • Fluent in French and English
