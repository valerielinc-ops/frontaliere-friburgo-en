Their tasks

The research group Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy of the University of Freiburg is looking for a diploma assistant or psychotherapy. a diploma assistant for ongoing projects on eating disorders, projects of the psychotherapeutic practice office and the establishment of a project for digital prevention.

  • Study administration, recruitment and supervision of study participants
  • Conduct structured clinical interviews and experimental investigations
  • Data management and data preparation
  • Participation in the establishment of a digital prevention project
  • Contribution to project reports and publications

Desired profile

  • Master in Psychology (MSc), ideally with a focus on Clinical Psychology
  • Experience with structured clinical interviews (e.g. DIPS) as well as knowledge in R or SPSS
  • Careful, independent working methods, organisational skills and teamwork
  • Very good command of the German language, good knowledge of English
