Grangeneuve is a multidisciplinary centre of excellence that is passionate about vocational training, expertise, advice and services for the professions of nature, milk, food and hotel industry. More than 280 employees work in a bilingual environment that promotes excellence, respect, collaboration and innovation as well as the appreciation and transmission of cultural heritage.

The Structural Improvements sector processes requests for financial support for the realisation of agricultural projects through non-repayable contributions and investment loans.

Their tasks

  • Cooperation and support in all technical and administrative tasks of the structural improvements sector of the Agricultural Section
  • Processing and processing subsidy applications for soil improvement projects, in particular in the areas of agricultural pathways, irrigation, drainage, water supply to Alpine pastures, land transfers, soil rehabilitation and restoration after natural events
  • Participation in the implementation of the cantonal irrigation strategy

  • Interest in agriculture and interdisciplinary projects
