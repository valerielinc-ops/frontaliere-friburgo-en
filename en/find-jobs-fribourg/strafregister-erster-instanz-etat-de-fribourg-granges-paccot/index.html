Who are we?

The Sarine District Court is the court of first instance competent for the Sarine District in the canton of Fribourg. This bilingual tribunal employs about sixty employees who contribute daily to the proper functioning of justice and to the service of the population. He sits in civil and criminal matters and also assumes the jurisdiction of several specialized courts, such as the Prud'hommes Court or the Leases Court.

Working at the Tribunal de la Sarine means engaging in a challenging, collaborative and demanding professional environment, offering diverse missions, a stable working environment and conditions conducive to skills development.
