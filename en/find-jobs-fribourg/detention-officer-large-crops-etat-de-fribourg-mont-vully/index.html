Activity rate 90-100% Location Mont-Vully, CH, Lac Posting time 12.07.2026 Effective date 01.08.2026 or to be agreed Type of contract CDI Who are we? The Friburg detention facility (EDFR) brings together prison structures in the canton of Fribourg. It has a status under public law and has legal personality. It is administratively attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport (DSJS), under the high supervision of the Council of State. The institution shall ensure the custody, accommodation, care and treatment of persons in custody entrusted to it and shall contribute to the achievement of the objectives assigned to the execution of custodial sentences and measures Why join us? The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees.   • Work within plant production (large crops, mill, orchard, green areas)
  • Provide care and constant supervision of detainees taking into account the various detention regimes
  • Instruct and train inmates on vehicles, agricultural machinery and manual work
  • Collaborating in the resocialization of detained persons through positive action
  • Respecting discipline, order and security among detainees
  • Apply and enforce existing regulations and instructions Profile desired
  • Agricultural CFC, or equivalent training, with several years of professional experience in crop production
  • Federal Patent of Detention Officer of the Swiss Centre of Competence in Enforcement of Penal Sanctions, or Be Ready to Attend Training
  • Very good knowledge of plant production
  • Skills in the use of agricultural vehicles and machinery
  • Good physical and mental condition, as well as flexibility and stress resistance
  • Meaning of human relations, communication and conflict management
  • Available to work with irregular schedules based on work and weather
  • Available outside working hours for cases of force majeure
  • Good knowledge of IT tools (MS Office)
  • Excerpts from prosecutions and blank criminal records
