Full description

Anchored in its region, our Bank supports its clients at every stage of their lives through personalized solutions and quality advice. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a 100% private core customer advisor.

Aufgaben

  • Welcome, inform and support our clients with professionalism and friendliness.
  • Guide clients in the use of banking automation and digital tools to promote their autonomy.
  • Advise a private clientele in all its current banking needs, while building lasting trust relationships.
  • Actively develop your client portfolio and leverage its potential in a comprehensive and proactive advisory approach.
  • Identify business opportunities and propose appropriate solutions in the areas of savings, payments, investments and foresight.
  • Contribute to the development of our modern distribution model and promote our digital solutions.
  • Work closely with the Bank's specialists to provide comprehensive and personalized support.
  • Participate in prospecting activities, customer events and the Bank's outreach in its region.

Anforderungen

  • Full banking training with experience in customer counselling.
  • Excellent sense of hospitality, communication and service.
  • Ability to build lasting relationships and enthusiasm through a proactive and solution-oriented approach.
  • A committed, motivated, flexible person with a strong team spirit.
  • Organization, rigour and focus.
  • Autonomy in managing your business while enjoying the collaborative work.
  • Experience with IT tools and digital solutions.
  • Initiative, reliability and willingness to actively contribute to the development of your client portfolio and the Bank.

Wir bieten

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.
