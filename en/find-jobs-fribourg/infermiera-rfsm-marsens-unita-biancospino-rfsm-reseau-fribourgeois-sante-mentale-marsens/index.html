Full description

Post "Infirmary, RFSM Marsens - Unit Aubépine" at RFSM - Frebourgeois mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

The Aubépine unit is a hospital unit on the Marsens site. It accommodates elderly people with mental health, including major neurocognitive disorders (demences). People in crisis and/or decompensated SCPDs come there for a check-up leading to a diagnosis, an adaptation of medication treatment or a period of observation with a view to a reorientation in a place of life adapted to their needs.
