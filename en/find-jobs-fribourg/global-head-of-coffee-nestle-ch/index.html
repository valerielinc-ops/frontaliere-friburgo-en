Full description

Location: Romont & Vevey, Switzerland Work model: On-site / Full time 100% Contract type / duration: Permanent

Shape & pioneer the next generation of Nespresso coffee experiences.

As Global Head of Coffee R&D at Nespresso, you will help define the future of premium coffee . Bringing deep, end-to-end Coffee R&D expertise from origin at the farm through green coffee transformation to the final consumer experience, you will serve as Nespresso's highest coffee authority. You will champion Nespresso Coffee Excellence and ensure the integrity of the consumer coffee experience is sustained over time. Combining your own expertise with the breadth of the Nestlé coffee R&D network, you will unite science, consumer insight and business ambition behind a bold vision. Leading a multidisciplinary Coffee and Sensory Organisation, you will turn pioneering advances into distinctive experiences across our B2C and B2B platforms and embed coffee excellence throughout Nespresso, bringing it to life for colleagues and consumers, in our boutiques and beyond.

How you'll make an impact as Global Head of Coffee R&D

  • Set the global Coffee R&D vision and strategy, translating it into technology roadmaps, investment priorities and portfolio choices that shape our long-term competitive advantage
  • Mobilise the full Nestlé coffee R&D network, from plant science to transformation, roasting and sensory, behind Nespresso's coffee ambition
  • Shape the coffee innovation portfolio across Nespresso's evolving B2C and B2B coffee platforms, making decisive choices on value, risk, quality, investment and speed
  • Be the voice of the consumer coffee experience in critical end-to-end decisions, defining the principles of coffee and sensory excellence and ensuring cost, operational and sustainability choices strengthen Nespresso’s quality & distinctiveness.
  • Forge strategic partnerships that accelerate scientific breakthroughs and deliver measurable impact across the organisation
  • Build & lead a world class multidisciplinary Coffee and Sensory organisation, building the talent, capabilities and accountability needed to deliver at scale
  • Embed coffee excellence throughout Nespresso and serve as its highest coffee authority externally, bringing our expertise to life with internal colleagues, in our boutiques and across industry, academia, media and thought leadership.

What will make you successful

  • Demonstrated end-to-end expertise in coffee research and development, covering origin, green coffee, transformation, roasting, extraction, sensory science, and product experience, complemented by multiple leadership roles in the coffee and FMCG sectors.
  • Visionary & strategic thinking, with the ability to connect emerging science, consumer needs and business opportunities into a compelling long-term direction
  • A strong record of turning science and technology into distinctive, premium experiences and scalable commercial outcomes
  • Leadership of world-class specialists through purpose, trust and clarity, creating high performance without micromanagement
  • Enterprise influence and strategic judgement, with excellent communication, fluency in English, influencing senior stakeholders with purpose, trust, clarity and sound judgement
  • Commercial courage to prioritize investment, challenge established thinking, manage trade-offs and stop work that will not create sufficient value
  • Bringing our WE CAN mindset to life through collaboration, courage, speed and continuous learning, while driving performance with respect and care at the foundation.
