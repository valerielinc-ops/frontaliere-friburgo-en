Main responsibilities

  • Join and strengthen our team and assume responsibilities such as: MODEL REVIS 80-100% (F/H/D) FRIBOURG YOUR TACHES:
  • Modeling under Autodesk Revive of reinforced concrete structures and metal structures, as well as civil engineering works (terraces, temporary and permanent supports, underground works)
  • Creation and updating of BIM models and associated documentation for all project phases, with particular emphasis on the implementation phase
  • Integration of existing state and point cloud models (as-built)
  • Production of general plans, coordination plans, formwork plans and rebar plans, as well as metres, list of materials and nomenclatures from models
  • Close collaboration with our engineers and technical specialists

Key requirements

  • SWC of draftsman-builder or civil engineer degree
  • Minimum 3 years full-time BIM modeling experience
  • Solid control of Autodesk Revit and CAD/DAO software such as AutoCAD
  • Knowledge of Civil 3D and/or Tekla Structures are a plus
  • Experience of BIM collaboration platforms (Autodesk Construction Cloud, Trimble Connect, BIMcollab) appreciated
  • Knowledge of IFC standards and BIM (SIA, buildingSMART) directives is a plus
  • Good technical understanding and experience in structural civil engineering, highly valued infrastructure or underground works
