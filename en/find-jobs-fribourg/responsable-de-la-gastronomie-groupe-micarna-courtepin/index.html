Responsable de la Gastronomie — groupe Micarna

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros.

Mansioni

  • You manage kitchens and restaurants on our Courtepin and Bazenheid sites
  • You harmonize the practices between the two sites, especially in terms of products, quality and process
  • You standardize processes to improve the efficiency and quality of restaurants
  • You develop and implement standardization concepts
  • You centrally manage resources and costs
  • You guarantee working conditions that meet safety, health and welfare requirements
  • You are the main supplier contact and you manage the orders of goods
  • You perform various administrative tasks
  • You offer balanced and modern dishes
  • You contribute to good nutritional health by taking into account the different activities of employees

Required

  • Professional experience: 3 to 5 years of experience in collective restoration
  • Higher school: CFC cook with superior training in catering / hotel
  • French (Good mastery) German (Good mastery): French-German bilingual or with a very good level in both languages (B2/C1).
  • You stand out for a strong performance orientation and results
  • You show excellent customer orientation and a sense of pronounced service
  • You are available and flexible for regular travel between Courtepin and Bazenheid
  • You know the usual computer tools.
  • You have excellent control over budgetary control
  • You have very good leadership skills

Cosa offeriamo

  • Migros Club School: 70% reimbursement of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
  • Restaurant: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points Cumulus: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
