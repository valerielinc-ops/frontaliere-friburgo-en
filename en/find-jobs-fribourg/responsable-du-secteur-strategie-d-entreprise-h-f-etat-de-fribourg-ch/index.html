Full description

Who are we?

Grangeneuve, a multidisciplinary competence centre, is passionately committed to vocational training, expertise, advice and services for the professions of land, nature, milk, agri-food and hotel and stewardship. Its activities are led by more than 280 collaborators working in a bilingual environment, where excellence, respect, collaboration and innovation are encouraged, in a spirit of valuing and transmitting heritage.

The Enterprise Strategy sector supports agricultural enterprises in their development through individual counselling and continuing training (vulgarization). Its main areas of activity are the operating budget, the transfer of heritage, inter-farm collaboration and agricultural activities.
