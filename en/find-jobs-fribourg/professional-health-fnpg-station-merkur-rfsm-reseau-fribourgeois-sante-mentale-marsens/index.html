Full description

Poste "Expert Health, FNPG, Station Merkur" au RFSM - Réseau fribourgeois santé mentale, Marsens (FR). Le RFSM est le pôle de santé mentale du canton de Fribourg : psychiatrie adulte, enfants/adolescents, personnes âgées, addictions.

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence centre specialising in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, more broadly, mental health.

Bilingual FNPG is constantly evolving and keeping up to date in the therapeutic field.

Their tasks

To complement the nursing staff of the FNPG’s German general psychiatric station Mercury in Villars-Sur-Glâne:

  • Work in the FaGe competence area in the binomial with a nurse
  • Professional quality care in partnership with patients and their relatives and in accordance with the FNPG nursing philosophy
  • Supporting patients in the activities of daily life
  • Taking over logistical, administrative and domestic tasks on the station
  • Cooperation in the supervision of FaGe learners

Desired profile

  • Professional Health (FaGe) with federal certificate of ability
  • Interest in accompanying and supporting psychiatric patients
  • At least one year of experience in the care sector
  • Responsible, organized, independent, committed and reliable personality
  • French or German as the main language, with knowledge of the other language at level B2/C1 (verbal and written)
