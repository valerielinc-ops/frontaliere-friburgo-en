Be part of our team!

What if your next professional challenge starts here? Our front-office team in Belfaux is looking for its future customer advisor. Ready to find out what's waiting for you?

What are you waiting for?

  • Development and monitoring of clients in basic services: identification of customer needs and provision of appropriate solutions for day-to-day management, savings, means of payment, investments and basic provision
  • Client portfolio development: acquisition of new customers, development and monitoring of the existing portfolio, while actively contributing to market exploration actions
  • Collaboration and teamwork: identification of specific needs and referral of clients to specialist advisors, promoting effective collaboration within the team
  • Commitment to a responsible environment: development in a cooperative bank with strong values, actively contributing to a sustainable and responsible approach, giving meaning to professional activity

What are you bringing?

  • Completed banking training or equivalent in another area of finance
  • A confirmed experience in consulting and/or selling (a banking experience is a plus)
  • An ease in exchanges, with a developed sense of listening, understanding and adaptation
  • An enthusiasm to advise, serve and convince, while adopting a positive attitude and a positive behaviour
  • Strong willingness to learn, develop continuously and achieve ambitious goals
  • An ease in using digital tools

By joining us, you will integrate a human-sized bank and a committed team, where each advisor will contribute daily to create a lasting relationship of trust with customers. Vincent Bardet Member of management Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
