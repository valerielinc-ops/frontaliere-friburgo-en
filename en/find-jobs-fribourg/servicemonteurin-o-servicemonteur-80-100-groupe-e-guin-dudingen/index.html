Full description

We operate in Western Switzerland and support our customers in implementing their projects by offering comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, gentle mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the reconciliation of private and professional life.

Thanks to its expertise, the Technical and Infrastructures Directorate is a customer-oriented and powerful partner for interdisciplinary projects in the areas of electrical installations, switchboards and electrical infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar systems, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, sanitation, water treatment, sales and installation of kitchens and household appliances as well as plant maintenance.

Are you looking for a new challenge in a dynamic and future-oriented company? Would you like to work in a family and committed team that jointly develops innovative solutions for the future? With us, you can expect exciting tasks, attractive working conditions and an environment that actively supports your professional and personal development.

Your mission
