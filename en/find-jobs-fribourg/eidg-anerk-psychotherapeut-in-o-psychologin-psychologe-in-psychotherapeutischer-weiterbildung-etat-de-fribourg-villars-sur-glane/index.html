Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence centre specialising in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, more broadly, mental health. Bilingual FNPG is constantly evolving and keeping up to date in the therapeutic field.

Their tasks

For the Institute of Psychotherapy of the FNPG in Villars-sur-Glâne:

  • Outpatient psychotherapeutic treatments
  • Clarifications and indication discussions for new registrations as well as psychotherapeutic follow-up
  • Allocation of new registrations and participation in regular meetings with the responsible persons of the Institute for Psychotherapy
  • Administrative work in connection with patients (reports to insurance companies, management of patient dossiers, etc.)

  • Master in Psychology (or degree recognized as equivalent)
  • Further education title in psychotherapy or shortly before completion of further education

Your profile

  • Ongoing or completed psychotherapeutic training (cognitive behavioural therapy, systemic, psychoanalytic or person-centered psychotherapy)
  • Practical psychotherapeutic experience
  • Mastery of the German language with good knowledge of French
