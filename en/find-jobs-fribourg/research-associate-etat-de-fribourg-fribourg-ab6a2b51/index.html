The Office for Mobility (MobA) is responsible for issues related to mobility. Mobility has numerous environmental and economic impacts. Due to increased individual mobility and the increase in public transport, planning and steering mobility are becoming increasingly important.

Their tasks

  • Management of cantonal and regional planning
  • Examination of mobility aspects at cantonal, regional and local level
  • Examination of site and detailed development plans with regard to mobility (validation of transport studies)

• Development, coordination and follow-up of sectoral planning

  • Advising municipalities and the civil engineering office on the preparation of mobility infrastructure plans (MIP)
  • Advising planners/municipalities

Desired profile

  • Higher education (Master), degree as an engineer/in ETH or FH or equivalent education and experience in the field of legislation and mobility planning
  • Interest in the application of legislation, standards and planning tools

• Master of the German language as well as very good knowledge of the French language
