Appointment authority / department / institution

KSVA is an independent public institution that manages and coordinates the activities of the Equalisation Fund of the Canton of Freiburg and the Cantonal Invalidity Insurance Office. It thus constitutes a unique competence center for the insured persons and companies of the Canton of Freiburg of all social insurances of the first pillar.

Become part of our team!

We offer a pleasant working environment with a modern infrastructure that is ideally connected to public transport. We promote the reconciliation of private and professional life, training and mobility.

In order to facilitate the processing of applications, we ask all interested persons to attach only a single PDF file with all diplomas, employment certificates and/or other documents.

Their tasks

In the section "Discovery Service" of the Cantonal Invalidity Insurance Office

  • They assess the need for assistance in day-to-day activities for applications for helplessness compensation (minors, IV and AHV)
  • They produce detailed clarification reports
  • You work interdisciplinary within the IV position
  • You are actively working with the network (provider, medical and social network)
  • They assess the restrictions in the household when registering for an IV pension

Desired profile

  • FH diploma in social work, health or in education considered equivalent
  • They are skilled at listening and talking.
  • You can analyze and summarize the individual situation of the person concerned, taking into account their health impairment, as well as the economic and individual situation
  • They have editorial skill.
  • You are interested in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You hold a category B driving licence
  • You have several years of professional experience in the social and/or health sector
  • Experience in the field of paediatrics is of great advantage and is highly appreciated
  • Mastery of the French (word and writing) or German (word) language with very good knowledge of the other language
