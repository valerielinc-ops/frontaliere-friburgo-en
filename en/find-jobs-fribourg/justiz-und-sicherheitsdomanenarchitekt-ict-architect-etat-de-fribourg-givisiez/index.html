Who are we?

The IT and Telecommunications Service (SITel) ensures the availability, security and development of the information systems and digital infrastructures of the State of Fribourg.

Within the Applications section, the Justice and Security (JS) sector supports the digital transformation of entities working in the fields of public security, justice and the execution of sovereign tasks.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of backgrounds and profiles that makes the strength of the State of Friborg. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To find out more, follow this link.

Your missions

We are looking for a Justice and Security Domain Architect responsible for ensuring the architectural governance, evolution, coherence and performance of the information system in the Justice and Security domain.

Unlike a solution architect, whose role is to design the architecture of a particular solution or project, the domain architect acts as the representative of enterprise architecture within his or her domain. He or she defines the evolution trajectory of the information system, guarantees its alignment with the State's digital strategy, manages its urbanization and ensures the application of the organization's architectural, technological and methodological standards.

In this context, it constitutes the local relay for corporate architecture and represents the architectural authority in the Justice and Security field to business beneficiaries, projects and technical teams.

Reporting to the Authority: Head of the Justice and Security Sector

Your responsibilities

Domain governance and architecture

  • Define and maintain the architectural vision of the Justice and Security domain in line with the State's target architecture
  • Manage the urbanization of information systems in the field and promote their rationalization
  • Govern the domain architecture according to TOGAF principles and institutional standards
  • Develop and maintain the application, informational and technical mapping of the field
  • Contribute to the cantonal digital strategy and translate its orientations into concrete development trajectories for the field

Domain architecture referent

  • Be the reference point of contact for all architectural questions related to programs, projects, missions and operational activities in the field
  • Advise business managers, project managers, product managers and technical teams in structuring choices
  • Issue the necessary architectural notices as part of the governance processes and HERMES Metro milestones
  • Represent enterprise architecture interests within the domain and ensure alignment of initiatives with defined standards

Alignment and compliance

  • Guarantee the application of State technical, technological and architectural standards
  • Evaluate the relevance, consistency and impacts of changes to the information system
  • Identify deviations from the target architecture, propose remedial measures and monitor their implementation
  • Monitor architectural deviations and contribute to compliance checks
  • Participate in the activities of the Architecture and Technology Committee (CAT) and the architectural community

Transformation support

  • Contribute to the modernization of applications and platforms in the field
  • Support the adoption of cloud architectures, transversal digital services and new technologies
  • Participate in the management of the life cycle of solutions and the control of technical and application debt
  • Formulate strategic recommendations to improve the quality, security, interoperability and resilience of the information system
  • In specific cases, be able to define transition architectures before reaching the target architect. The most used basic models are available on MonSITel
