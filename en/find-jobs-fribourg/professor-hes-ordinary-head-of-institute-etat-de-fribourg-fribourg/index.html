Who are we?

The University of Management (HEG-FR) of Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland (HES-SO)- Accredited by AACSB, it offers bilingual and trilingual training at the bachelor and master levels.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg strong. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

The holder assumes the academic and operational responsibility of an institute active in the fields of entrepreneurship, innovation and the development of organizations. He or she contributes to strengthening applied research, developing interdisciplinary projects and anchoring HEG-FR in its economic, institutional and academic environment.

  • Develop and position the Institute's activities in line with the strategic priorities of HEG-FR and the needs of its environment
  • Structure and animate the institute in the key fields of a management school
  • Initiate, acquire and pilot projects, including with third-party funding
  • Enhancing the scientific quality, impact and value of the Institute's activities, including through publications, performance indicators and knowledge transfer
  • To contribute to the development of the new research organisation by promoting clear, collaborative and evolving governance and better coordination between teams
  • Strengthen the link between research, teaching and field needs, in particular by integrating the Institute's themes into bachelor, master and continuing training
  • Designing and delivering practice-oriented teaching in bachelor's, master's and continuing education programmes

Profile desired

  • Doctorate in a relevant field, such as strategic management, operational management and value chains
  • Confirmed experience in applied research, third-party fund acquisition, project conduct and collaboration with external partners
  • Good knowledge of issues related to entrepreneurship, innovation and the development of organisations, whether they are enterprises of different sizes, public, social or umbrella organisations
  • Experience in acquiring and managing third-party funds
  • Ability to gradually emerge a vision, in an agile and responsive approach, while uniting teams and building collaborative governance
  • Experience in tertiary education and interest in the link between teaching, applied research, service provision and knowledge transfer
  • Academic, economic or institutional network
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language and English

Additional information

  • Four-year term, renewable, as Professor HES ordinary, head of institute. He is associated with an indeterminate contract in the position of Associate Professor HES
