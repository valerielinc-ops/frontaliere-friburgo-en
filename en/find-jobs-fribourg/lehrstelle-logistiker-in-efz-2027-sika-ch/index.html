About the role For the start of the apprenticeship in August 2027, we are looking for interested young people for the apprenticeship as a logistician/in EFZ. Various materials are delivered and processed every day, and semi-finished and finished products must be stored and shipped. The tasks of the logistician include the receipt of goods, entry inspection, storage and relocation, inventory, warehousing and the packaging and securing of the transported goods, with the aim of providing the right quantity of the right goods in a timely and cost-effective manner at the right place. Forklifts, electric vehicles and other technical aids facilitate physical work. The computer has also made no stop in front of the warehouse, all goods incoming and outgoing must be recorded. Our versatile and exciting basic education allows you an optimal start into the working world.
