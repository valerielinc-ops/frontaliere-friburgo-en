Full description

Location: Verfora AG, Route de Moncor 12, 1752 Villars-sur-Glâne

Do you want to make a difference in the pharmaceutical sector and help shape the availability of safe, effective and regulatoryly compliant health products? You take responsibility for a versatile portfolio over the entire product lifecycle.

You can do that.

  • Ensure regulatory compliance of your pharmaceutical, dietary and medical product portfolio from development to end of product lifecycle
  • You create, write and submit regulatory documentation and keep dossiers and product labels up-to-date according to the applicable requirements
  • You develop project-specific submission strategies and coordinate them directly with authorities and partners in Switzerland and internationally
  • You accompany development teams at an early stage, identify regulatory requirements and risks and derive viable options for action.
  • You work closely with quality, R&D, marketing and medical affairs and bring your regulatory perspective to portfolio decisions
  • You follow regulatory and scientific developments, especially in the pharmaceutical sector and depending on the portfolio of herbal medicines and phytotherapy

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree in pharmacy, chemistry, biology, biomedical sciences or a comparable natural science discipline
  • You have very good professional knowledge of German and French in words and writing and can work independently in both languages
  • You have good knowledge of English for scientific and regulatory documentation as well as for exchange with international partners
  • You analyze complex scientific and regulatory issues accurately and clearly represent your position to internal teams, partners and authorities
  • You reliably organize several priorities, identify challenges early on and develop pragmatic solutions
  • You have a special affinity for herbal medicines, herbal substances or phytotherapy
  • Experience in this area is beneficial
  • Ideally, you will bring about two years of experience in regulatory affairs in the pharmaceutical industry

You work at the locations Villars-sur-Glâne (main working location) and Worb in a hybrid working model with home office.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

A product portfolio with top brands Verfora is a company in the Galenica network and markets high-quality health and beauty products with well-known products and brands found in almost every Swiss household: Perskindol®, Anti-Brumm®, Merfen®, Algifor® – just to name a few of the top products. We have a strong position in the areas of pain, cough, cold and respiratory diseases. Our work in marketing and sales is primarily aimed at all pharmacies and drugstores. At Verfora, you are part of a mission: “Help people feel good, get and stay healthy.” At the same time, you help to continuously expand our product portfolio and open up new market segments.
