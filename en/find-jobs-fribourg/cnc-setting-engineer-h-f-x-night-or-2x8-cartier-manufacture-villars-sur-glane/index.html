WHAT YOUR IMPACT IS

Mission

Within our precious department, you are a member of a team of about 10-12 employees, you work on 2x8 team or night shifts in a CNC machine fleet (mainly milling) and perform adjustments and production.

Responsibilities

Ensure the driving, adjustment, commissioning of our CNC milling machines of type Chiron FX/FZ, Micro 5 (UGV), Bumotec S181, CT501 (meulage) during the machining of our precious watch dressing parts

To guarantee the conformity of the manufacture by carrying out visual and dimensional checks and, if necessary, make the necessary corrections in accordance with the instructions of the workshop

Prepare the machining station by raising tools and positing and checking the conformity of the tools. If necessary, make necessary tool corrections.

Ensure the transfer of instructions to relay teams

Participate in the cleaning and maintenance of equipment, machinery and tools and comply with OHS Occupational Health and Safety rules

To participate in the continuous improvement of processes and working environment and in training activities in the context of developments in the manufacturing process

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

Polymechanic CFC, Production Mechanic CFC or BEP/BTS in the field of precision mechanics

Confirmed professional experience in CNC machining

Knowledge of 5-7 axis CNC machines with Fanuc command would be a plus

Good mastery of the usual dimensional control tools, Marcel Aubert and 3D control type Zeiss

A rigorous, motivated, organized and conscientious person who can work as a team. Analytic, you can detect problems and track them to their resolution in a dynamic of constant improvement.
