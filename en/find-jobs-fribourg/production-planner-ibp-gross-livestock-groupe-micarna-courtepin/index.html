Full description

Are you passionate about planning and optimizing processes? Join Micarna SA and help ensure the right balance between our customers' demand, production capacity and resource management every day. Thanks to your sense of anticipation, you play a key role in the performance of our fresh meat production.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Mansioni

  • You develop, manage and optimize production plans in the short, medium and long term for the hair animals sector (beef, calf, lamb)
  • You plan and pilot the slaughter and cutting processes, while guaranteeing the optimal recovery of raw materials, with a particular emphasis on the supply of meat for the meat industry
  • You coordinate capacities with different production sites and adapt planning flexibly in the event of deviations, stresses or disturbances
  • You monitor stocks, identify risks throughout the value chain and implement appropriate measures
  • You analyze the data and simulate different scenarios in case of changes in demand, delays in supply or interruptions in production
  • You work closely with production, sales, procurement, logistics and other internal partners, and actively participate in planning and coordination sessions
  • You guarantee high availability of goods and contribute to economic optimization of stocks and processes
  • You are actively involved in the continuous development, digitization and automation of planning processes in SAP

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: experience in a similar function. Knowledge of the meat or food industry is an asset.
  • Initial or continuing training completed: Vocational training completed, combined with a high affinity for IT and systems, as well as a strong interest in data-based planning and process optimization
  • French (Very good knowledge) German (Good knowledge) English (Preferably): Good knowledge of French and German (minimum B2 level in both languages). English is an additional asset.
  • Experience with SAP IBP, SAP R/3 or SAP S/4HANA as well as excellent knowledge of Excel
  • Analytical spirit and ability to understand complex interactions, with a strong ease in the analysis of numbers
  • Initiative, sense of responsibility and ability to keep an overview even in a dynamic environment
  • Team spirit, excellent communication skills and fun working with different departments
  • Experience in production planning, equipment or Supply Chain, an advantage

Contatto

Candidature and contact Sabrina Zimmermann

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the specialized department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Final maintenance prior to contract establishment: Before establishing the contract, we conduct a final interview to clarify the remaining open issues of both parties and to resolve the contractual details. When all points are clarified, a written offer is submitted. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week
