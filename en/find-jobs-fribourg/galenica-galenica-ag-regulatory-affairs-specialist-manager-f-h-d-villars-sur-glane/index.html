Full description

Workplace: Verfora AG, Route de Moncor 12, 1752 Villars-sur-Glâne

You wish to make a concrete contribution to the provision of safe, effective and compliant? Within our Regulatory Affairs team, you take care of a varied portfolio and accompany it throughout its entire life cycle.

Your area of action

  • You have the regulatory responsibility for a portfolio consisting mainly of medicines, food supplements and medical devices, from development to marketing authorisation and then throughout their life cycle
  • You prepare, write, submit and update regulatory records and labelling to ensure compliance with applicable requirements
  • You define bid strategies adapted to projects and interact directly with the competent authorities and our partners in Switzerland and internationally
  • You accompany development teams from the early stages, identify regulatory requirements and risks and propose solid and pragmatic options
  • You work closely with Quality, R&D, Marketing and Medical Affairs teams and contribute to your portfolio decisions
  • You follow the relevant regulatory and scientific developments, especially in the pharmaceutical field and, according to your portfolio, for herbal medicines and herbal medicine

Your assets

  • You have a university degree in pharmacy, chemistry, biology, biomedical sciences or a comparable scientific discipline.
  • You master French and German at a professional level, in both oral and written, and you can work independently in both languages.
  • You have a good command of English to process scientific and regulatory documentation and exchange with international partners
  • You write with precision, analyze complex scientific and regulatory issues, organize several priorities and defend your positions in a clear and constructive way
  • You are interested in herbal medicines, plant substances or herbal medicine; experience in this field is an asset
  • You ideally have about two years of experience in regulatory affairs in the pharmaceutical industry

The position can be exercised from Villars-sur-Glâne (main workplace) and Worb, in a hybrid work model including telework.

We offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation or taking leave without pay
  • Pension fund with advantageous distribution – we cover nearly 2/3 of the premiums
  • Miscellaneous discounts in our pharmacies and other major brands
  • Opportunities to expand its skills through encouragement of training and development measures and opportunities for internal change
  • Taking into account the needs of families, with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of salary) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of salary), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Nominal actions at preferential prices – not only engage professionally for Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly by department/position

About us

A portfolio of products with excellent brands Verfora is a Galenica network company that markets top quality health and beauty products with well-known brands and products in almost all Swiss households: Perskindol®, Anti-Brumm®, Merfen®, Algifor® – to name but a few of the leading products. We have a strong position in the areas of pain, cough, cold and respiratory diseases. Our marketing and distribution activities are primarily aimed at all pharmacies and drugstores. At Verfora, you will take part in a mission: "to help people feel good, to be and stay healthy". In parallel, you are helping to continuously develop our product portfolio and gain new market segments.
