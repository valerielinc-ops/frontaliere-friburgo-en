Missions

  • You perform the maintenance of the production tools by respecting the objectives of quantity, quality and time and/or range
  • You set tools independently
  • You are progressing from the measuring benches
  • You measure tools on adjustment benches
  • You manage anomalies (processing of non-compliant parts, quality alerts, production problems, etc.) and go back the necessary situations to the team leader
  • You process claims
  • You calibrate the means of clamping
  • You handle tools using a pallet
  • You guarantee order and storage of your workstation, in accordance with the 5S
  • You clean and enter the different machines in the Profile sector
  • You have a CFC of Polymechanic, production mechanic (m/f/x) or equivalent training in mechanics
  • You have experience in the field of tools
  • You have good knowledge of French
  • You are a flexible person, especially for team schedules in 3*8
  • You are an accurate and reliable person, appreciate the teamwork and autonomously
