Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Human Resources Directorate is the partner committed to anticipating, defining and developing the Group's culture, leadership and organization.

Mission

  • Lead HR Partnership and Recruitment to meet the needs of the company and ensure high-value, high-quality services Support managers and managers in managing human, organisational and managerial issues to support the company's performance
  • Defining and deploying the attractive recruitment strategy while contributing to the anticipation and development of the necessary skills and retention of talent
  • Ensure consistent and equitable application of HR policies, processes and practices within the organization
  • Advise management in organizational developments and workforce and skills planning
  • Lead and develop a team of HR Business Partners and recruitment specialists, fostering collaboration, engagement, operational excellence and achieving goals

Profile

  • University or HES training in human resources, business management, law or equivalent, supplemented by a federal HR patent
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in human resources, including ideally 3 years in a managerial position
  • Experience confirmed as HR Business Partner Senior, HR Manager or equivalent, as well as in management of social partners and negotiation
  • Excellent knowledge of Swiss labour law, social insurance and HR processes
  • French current, German knowledge and mastery of Microsoft 365 and SIRH tools
  • Federated leadership, sense of partnership, orientation solutions and ability to support change

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
