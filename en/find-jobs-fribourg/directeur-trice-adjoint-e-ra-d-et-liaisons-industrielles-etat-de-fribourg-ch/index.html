Who are we?

The University of Management (HEG-FR) in Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual and trilingual training at the Bachelor and Master level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

As a member of management, you ensure strategic responsibility for the area of Applied Research & Development (Ra&D) and relations with economic and academic partners. As such, you contribute to the definition of institutional orientations, support the development of research activities, strengthen collaborations and ensure the visibility of the HEG-FR.

  • Participate in the governance and strategic development of the HEG-FR
  • Define and implement the strategy for applied research and development and the mandates
  • Managing the portfolio of research activities and projects, acquiring external funding and regional, national and international collaborations
  • Conduct and support the development of R&D institutes, centres of expertise and services
  • Develop and strengthen relationships with businesses, public authorities and institutional partners to strengthen knowledge transfer and mandate activities
  • Ensuring the scientific quality of research activities and compliance with institutional standards and AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business)
  • Strengthen synergies between research, training and professional circles in order to increase the impact and visibility of the HEG-FR

Additional information

  • Activity rate: 40 - 50 %
  • This job can be completed up to 80 – 100% by the second position currently in the competition of assistant director-e-Education

Profile desired

  • Holder of a PhD in a relevant field or equivalent training, ideally complemented by continuing training in management or leadership
  • Experience confirmed in leadership or team management within an academic institution or complex organization
  • Recognized in applied research and proven experience in the acquisition and management of competitively funded projects
  • Good knowledge of research funding mechanisms and academic and economic innovation ecosystems
  • Experience in developing partnerships between high schools, companies and public or private institutions
  • Federated leadership, strategic vision and ability to mobilize expert teams in a collaborative environment
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language, as well as English
